# 90-Day Execution Plan — Solo Operator

**Date:** 2026-08-05 → 2026-11-03
**Channel:** board channel only (`01-RECONCILIATION.md` §5)
**Playbook:** `docs/04-SALES-PLAYBOOK-BOARD-CHANNEL.md`, with the August

## The shape of this plan

Three phases, and the first one is not selling:

| Phase | Weeks | Goal |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Validate** | 1–3 | 15 discovery conversations. Learn whether the pitch and the price survive contact. Zero pressure to close. |
| **2. Sell** | 4–9 | Run the `docs/04` chain end to end. Target: 3 paying associations. |
| **3. Compound** | 10–13 | Convert wins into references, case study, and repeatable content. Decide on PM channel. |

### Why weeks 1–3 are not selling

`docs/09` logged in March that **customer validation was skipped** — no discovery
calls with target buyers before building. That debt is still unpaid five months
later, and it is now the largest single risk in this plan.

You are about to ask a volunteer retiree to pay **$199/mo against a $55
incumbent** on a value proposition that has never been tested on a real buyer.
If that price or that framing is wrong, discovering it across 15 low-pressure
conversations in three weeks costs you three weeks. Discovering it across six
months of failed closes costs you the year.

This is not a delay. Discovery calls surface the trigger events, the objections,
and the actual words boards use — all of which are direct inputs to Phase 2. And
some of these conversations will convert anyway, because a discovery call with a
qualified board president naturally becomes a demo when they ask to see it.

## Phase 1 — Validate (weeks 1–3)

**Goal: 15 conversations with board presidents of 25–149 unit self-managed
associations. Not demos. Conversations.**

### Build the list (week 1, ~1 day)

Florida DBPR maintains a public condominium registry; county property appraiser
records give unit counts and association names. Filter to: 25–149 units,
Palm Beach / Broward / Miami-Dade. Target list size: **150** — you need ~10x your
conversation goal.

For each, capture the trigger fields from `02-POSITIONING.md` §1: annual meeting
timing, recent board turnover, milestone-inspection/SIRS exposure, and whether
they currently have any portal at all (check — it takes 30 seconds and it is your
opening line).

### Run the conversations (weeks 1–3)

Use the trigger-specific open in `02-POSITIONING.md` §5.1, **not** the February
deadline script. Ask for 15 minutes. What you are trying to learn:

1. How do they run the association today? (Get the specifics — which inbox, whose
   spreadsheet, which filing cabinet.)
2. How many hours a month does it cost them personally?
3. What did they do about the website requirement, if anything? Why?
4. Has an owner ever asked them for records they couldn't produce?
5. What would they expect something like this to cost? **Ask before you quote.**
6. Who actually decides, and when does the board next meet?

Log every answer verbatim — this is the `docs/08` collected-yeses discipline
starting early, and it is also your price research.

### Week 3 checkpoint — decide before proceeding

| Signal | Meaning | Action |
|---|---|---|
| ≥8 of 15 describe real operational pain **and** expect ≥$100/mo | Pitch and price hold | Proceed to Phase 2 as written |
| Pain is real but price expectation clusters at $50–75 | You're priced against CondoSites in their heads | Do **not** cut to $99 reflexively. Re-test with the per-unit framing (§4.1) and the audit. If it still fails, revisit tiering before scaling outbound |
| <5 describe meaningful pain | The wedge is wrong | **Stop.** Do not start Phase 2. Reassess — this is the outcome the whole phase exists to catch |

### Engineering in parallel (weeks 1–3)
- **B4** placeholder social proof — before the first call points anyone at the site
- **B1** compliance-checker lead capture
- **B2** content route scaffold

## Phase 2 — Sell (weeks 4–9)

**Goal: 3 paying associations.** Run `docs/04`'s chain: cold contact → combined
discovery+demo → **14-day compliance audit** → audit review → close.

### Weekly operating rhythm

| Day | Block | Activity |
|---|---|---|
| Mon | AM | 3 hrs prospecting — 25 new contacts |
| Mon | PM | Pipeline review; nothing sits >5 days silent |
| Tue–Thu | AM | Meetings and demos |
| Tue–Thu | PM | Audit setup and check-ins for live prospects |
| Fri | AM | Writing — one resource article (§Phase 3) |
| Fri | PM | Engineering: whatever readiness item is current |

### Targets

Working backward from `docs/04`'s conversion rates (25–30% to meeting, 70–80% to
audit, 65–75% vote yes, 90% sign) — conservatively, ~3 closes needs **~10 audits**,
from **~14 demos**, from **~55 meetings-worth of contact attempts**. At 25
contacts/week for six weeks (150 contacts) that is achievable but has no slack.

**If you are behind at week 6, cut prospecting volume and protect the audits.**
Three deeply-supported audits that close beat ten neglected ones that stall. A
stalled audit is worse than no audit — it burns a prospect permanently.

### Non-negotiables from the playbooks
- Never leave a buying-commitment conversation without a signed agreement, a
  dated next step, or an honest death (`docs/06` cardinal rule)
- Never >5 days silent during an audit (`docs/04`)
- Capture yeses verbatim throughout (`docs/08`) — they are the Minimize ammunition
- At signature, trade any concession for a written testimonial + reference
  commitment (`02-POSITIONING.md` §4.3) — you need proof assets more than margin

## Phase 3 — Compound (weeks 10–13)

1. **Case study** from the best Phase-2 customer. Compliance score movement,
   hours saved, owner login rate. This is the asset that makes every subsequent
   deal easier — and it's the first real content for `/resources`.
2. **Replace placeholder proof (B4)** with the real testimonial and logo.
3. **Publish weekly.** By week 13 you should have ~10 articles targeting the
   queries your ICP actually types: *"Florida condo website requirement 25 units,"
   *"718.111(12)(g) compliance checklist,"* *"condo association records request
   Florida."* This is five months late; it will not pay off inside the 90 days.
   Do it anyway — it never pays off if it never starts.
4. **Ship B3** (PM inbound form) and evaluate the PM channel with the ammunition
   you now have: *"three associations in Palm Beach already run on this."*

## Risks this plan accepts

- **The window is partly spent.** Many easy prospects already bought. Phase 2
  will contain more displacement selling than the February plan assumed.
- **$199 vs. $55 is unvalidated.** Phase 1 is the mitigation; the week-3
  checkpoint is the circuit breaker.
- **Solo capacity is the binding constraint.** The plan spends ~80% on selling
  and ~20% on engineering. The failure mode is inverting that — retreating into
  code because code is comfortable and cold calls are not. Guard the Monday
  prospecting block above everything else on this page.
- **No mobile app.** Dropped as a differentiator (`01-RECONCILIATION.md` §2.1).
  If discovery reveals boards asking for it unprompted, that is a genuine signal
  worth reopening — but it is a post-revenue decision.
