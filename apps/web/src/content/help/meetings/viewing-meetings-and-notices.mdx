## Upcoming meetings

Open Meetings to review upcoming dates, times, locations, and agendas. Communities often attach supporting materials there before the meeting begins.

## Past records

Past meetings remain useful after the event. Return to the same page to find minutes and related documents when they have been posted.

## For admins

Admins can also use the Meetings area to create notices and keep required materials visible for the community.
