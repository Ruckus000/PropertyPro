Meetings lists every board or owner meeting your association has scheduled, along with the notice, agenda, and posted materials. You can also see past meetings once their minutes are approved.

## Find upcoming meetings

<StepByStep>
<Step title="Click Meetings in the sidebar">
The default view shows upcoming meetings first, with the next meeting at the top.
</Step>

<Step title="Review the notice">
Each row shows the meeting type (board, annual, special, owner), the date and time, and the location or call-in details. Click the row to open the full notice.
</Step>

<Step title="Open the agenda">
If an agenda is posted, it appears on the meeting page along with any supporting materials. Download or read inline.
</Step>

<Step title="Save it to your calendar">
Use your browser's date-picker integration, or copy the meeting into your calendar app manually. If you want a reminder ahead of time, open Settings and turn on calendar event reminder emails for meetings and due dates.
</Step>
</StepByStep>

## Past meetings and minutes

Scroll below the upcoming list to see past meetings. Once a meeting's minutes are approved and posted, the minutes appear on the meeting page.

<Callout type="tip" title="Minutes follow approval">
Minutes don't appear until the board has approved them at a subsequent meeting. If minutes are missing for a meeting that happened weeks ago, the board may still be finalizing them.
</Callout>

## Statutory context

<Callout type="florida-statute" title="Why the notice timing matters">
Florida statute sets minimum notice windows — generally 14 days for owner meetings and 48 hours for board meetings. Your notices are marked with their posting status so you can see that the rule was followed.
</Callout>

## What you can do from a meeting page

As a resident, you can:

- Download the notice, agenda, and any attached materials.
- Subscribe to updates (notifications fire when the agenda changes or minutes are posted).
- See whether there's a quorum expected for an owner meeting.

If your community has board polls or e-voting enabled, those links also appear on the meeting page when they're relevant.

## Can't find a meeting?

- **Check the community switcher** at the top of the page if you belong to more than one community.
- **Look in the past meetings list** — if the meeting already happened, scroll down.
- **Ask your contact** — if the meeting was announced verbally but never posted, the notice may be late. Contact Management through the help center.
