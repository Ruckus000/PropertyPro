Meetings lists every board or owner meeting your association has scheduled, along with the notice, agenda, and posted materials. You can also see past meetings once their minutes are approved.

## Find meetings on the calendar

<StepByStep>
<Step title="Click Meetings in the sidebar">
The page opens on a month calendar grid showing scheduled meetings. The switcher at the top offers three views of the same meetings: **Calendar**, **Schedule** (upcoming and past meetings as a list, with each meeting's notice window and status), and **Minutes** (every past meeting and whether its minutes are on the record).
</Step>

<Step title="Click a day">
Select any day on the calendar to open the day-detail panel. Meetings on that day appear with their type, time, and location.
</Step>

<Step title="Open a meeting">
Click a meeting to open the detail modal. You'll see the time, location, compliance deadlines, and any attached documents.
</Step>

<Step title="Save it to your calendar">
Copy the meeting details into your calendar app manually. Reminder emails follow the same calendar visibility you already have and are controlled by your notification settings — you set the timing in **Settings → Notifications**.
</Step>
</StepByStep>

## Past meetings and minutes

Switch to **Schedule** and choose **Past**, or open the **Minutes** view, to find past meetings without paging back through the calendar. Once a meeting's minutes are approved and posted, they appear as attached documents on the meeting detail modal.

<Callout type="tip" title="Minutes follow approval">
Minutes don't appear until the board has approved them at a subsequent meeting. If minutes are missing for a meeting that happened weeks ago, the board may still be finalizing them.
</Callout>

## Statutory context

<Callout type="florida-statute" title="Why the notice timing matters">
Florida statute sets minimum notice windows — generally 14 days for owner meetings and 48 hours for board meetings. Your notices are marked with their posting status so you can see that the rule was followed.
</Callout>

## What you can do from a meeting page

As a resident, you can:

- View the meeting time, location, and compliance deadlines.
- Open and download any attached documents from the detail modal.

## Can't find a meeting?

- **Check the community switcher** at the top of the page if you belong to more than one community.
- **Open the Schedule view and choose Past** if the meeting already happened.
- **Ask your contact** — if the meeting was announced verbally but never posted, the notice may be late. Contact Management through the help center.
