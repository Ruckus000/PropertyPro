PropertyPro publishes ICS (iCalendar) feeds for your community's meetings. Subscribing means your calendar app pulls updates automatically — no manual re-adding when a meeting gets rescheduled.

## Available feeds

Two feeds are available:

- **All community meetings**: `https://<your-community>.getpropertypro.com/api/v1/calendar/meetings.ics`
- **My meetings** (meetings you personally are expected at): `https://<your-community>.getpropertypro.com/api/v1/calendar/my-meetings.ics`

Replace `<your-community>` with your community's subdomain. You can find the right URL by opening your community dashboard and copying the domain from the address bar.

## Subscribing in Google Calendar

<StepByStep>
<Step title="Open Google Calendar">
Go to calendar.google.com on desktop (the mobile app doesn't support subscribe flows).
</Step>

<Step title="Other calendars → From URL">
In the left sidebar, click the + next to "Other calendars" and pick **From URL**.
</Step>

<Step title="Paste the ICS URL">
Paste either of the feed URLs and click Add calendar.
</Step>

<Step title="Confirm">
The calendar appears in your list and meetings populate within a few minutes.
</Step>
</StepByStep>

## Subscribing in Apple Calendar

1. Open Calendar on macOS.
2. File → New Calendar Subscription.
3. Paste the ICS URL.
4. Pick an auto-refresh interval (daily works well).

On iOS/iPadOS:

1. Settings → Calendar → Accounts → Add Account → Other.
2. Add Subscribed Calendar.
3. Paste the URL.

## Subscribing in Outlook

Outlook's "Add from web" feature accepts ICS URLs. In Outlook on the web, open Calendar, click Add calendar → Subscribe from web, and paste the URL.

## How often does the feed refresh?

PropertyPro refreshes the data whenever it's requested. Most calendar apps fetch hourly or daily by default — you can often tighten that to every 15 minutes in the app's settings.

<Callout type="tip" title="Changes appear on your next fetch">
If a meeting is rescheduled, cancelled, or added, your calendar app picks up the change on its next refresh cycle. If you need an update immediately, most apps have a manual refresh button.
</Callout>

## Privacy

The `my-meetings.ics` feed is personalized — it only includes meetings where you're expected. The feed URL acts like a secret token; anyone with the URL could see the same meeting list. Treat it as private and don't share it.

The `meetings.ics` feed shows all public community meetings and is safe to share within your community.

## Unsubscribing

Delete the subscribed calendar in your app. Future meetings stop syncing; past meetings you already have stay on your calendar until you remove them manually.
