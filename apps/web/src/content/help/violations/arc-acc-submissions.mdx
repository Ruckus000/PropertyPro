Architectural Review Committee (ARC) or Architectural Control Committee (ACC) submissions are how residents request permission to make exterior changes — paint, landscaping, structures. PropertyPro displays the submission queue and archives records.

<Callout type="florida-statute" title="HB 1203 written-reason requirement">
HB 1203 requires that any denial of an architectural request state specific reasons — typically citing the governing document or rule violated. PropertyPro's ARC queue is read-only today (there is no in-app field to enter a decision or reasons), so it is your responsibility to record the written reasons in your committee records and deliver them to the resident directly.
</Callout>

## The review queue

Open the ARC submissions queue to see:

- **Title**
- **Project Type**
- **Unit**
- **Status**
- **Submitted**

Click **Review** on any row to open the detail panel. Deadline tracking and assigned reviewers are not shown in PropertyPro — track those per your bylaws outside the app.

## Reviewing a submission

<StepByStep>
<Step title="Open the submission">
Click **Review** on a row. The detail panel shows Status, Description, Est. Start Date, Est. Completion, Review Notes (if any), Decided date (if set), and Submitted date.
</Step>

<Step title="Consult the rules">
Cross-check against your architectural guidelines. Many communities keep this document in the library under Governing Documents.
</Step>

<Step title="Record your decision outside the app">
Approving, denying, or requesting more information is not available in the PropertyPro UI today. Record decisions through your committee's normal process and communicate outcomes to the resident directly.
</Step>
</StepByStep>

## Approving with conditions

If your committee approves with conditions, document them in your committee records and communicate them to the resident. Conditions may include material or color restrictions, completion deadlines, or post-work inspection requirements.

## Denying a submission

<Callout type="warning" title="Always include specific reasons">
A denial without specific reasons is legally vulnerable. Good denials cite the specific rule (e.g., "Section 5.2 of the Architectural Guidelines prohibits pitched roofs above 18 feet") and, where possible, suggest a conforming alternative. Deliver the written reasons to the resident directly — the ARC queue has no decision or notification step today.
</Callout>

Because the queue is read-only, decisions and their reasons are communicated outside the app — keep a copy of the written reasons in your committee records.

## Extensions and appeals

If you need more time than your bylaws allow, communicate with the resident about the delay. PropertyPro does not track extension requests or pause review clocks — manage deadlines per your bylaws outside the app.

## Record retention

Approved submissions form part of the architectural history of the unit. A future owner may inherit whatever was approved. Keep the submission, plans, and decision in the record permanently — the review queue archives rather than deletes.

## Tips for a smooth queue

- **Schedule a weekly ARC review** to clear the queue regularly.
- **Template good denials** — capture common rule citations in your committee's reference doc.
- **Use photos generously** — approve photos stored on file help resolve "as-built vs. approved" disputes years later.
