Florida statute specifies both **which** records associations must make available and **when** — usually within 30 days of creation. PropertyPro tracks each obligation as a requirement on the Compliance dashboard, where you link the document that satisfies it.

<Callout type="florida-statute" title="Condo: § 718.111(12)(g)">
Condo associations with 25 or more units must post certain records on an association-managed website. The statute calls out bylaws, articles, rules, financial records, contracts over a threshold, meeting materials, and more.
</Callout>

<Callout type="florida-statute" title="HOA: § 720.303">
HOAs with 100 or more parcels have parallel requirements. Document categories differ slightly; the compliance dashboard shows only the categories that apply to your community type.
</Callout>

## Records the statute requires

The Compliance dashboard tracks one requirement per statutory record type. The common ones:

- **Governing documents** — declaration / bylaws / articles of incorporation / rules.
- **Financial records** — annual budget, financial report, reserve study, audit.
- **Meeting materials** — agendas, notices, minutes.
- **Contracts** — any vendor or service contract above the statutory threshold.
- **Insurance** — current certificates of coverage.
- **Question & Answer sheet** — required Q&A sheet for prospective purchasers (condos).
- **Video recordings** — virtual meeting recordings on a rolling 12-month basis (condos, when applicable).
- **Affidavits** — affidavits required by Chapter 718 (condos).
- **Conflict-of-interest contracts** — contracts involving a director or officer financial interest (when applicable).
- **Bids** — bids received after bidding closes (when applicable).
- **Inspection reports** — structural / milestone inspection reports (when applicable).
- **SIRS** — structural integrity reserve study (when applicable).

These are compliance **requirements**, not document-library categories. You satisfy each one by linking a document to it on the Compliance dashboard, whatever category that document lives under in the library.

## How the 30-day window works

<StepByStep>
<Step title="A record is finalized">
A document is approved, signed, or adopted — for example, meeting minutes are approved at the next meeting.
</Step>

<Step title="Upload it and link it">
Use **Upload** or **Link Existing** on the matching requirement row on the Compliance dashboard. PropertyPro records the posted date when you link or upload through that row.
</Step>

<Step title="PropertyPro tracks the clock">
Each requirement has a deadline from the checklist item (creation date plus 30 days, or a rolling window). Items due inside 7 days show as **Action needed** and count toward the **Due inside 7 days** KPI.
</Step>

<Step title="Audit trail is preserved">
Even if you post early, the posting time is saved in the audit log. Late postings are not retroactively "fixed" — they remain visible in the history.
</Step>
</StepByStep>

## What counts as "posted"

In PropertyPro, a requirement counts as satisfied once you **link a document to it** on the Compliance dashboard (or upload through the row's **Upload** button). Uploading a file to the library alone doesn't satisfy a requirement — you have to link it through the checklist row.

<Callout type="warning" title="Link the document, don't just upload it">
A budget sitting in the library won't satisfy the "Annual Budget" requirement until you link it on the Compliance dashboard. After uploading, open Compliance and attach the document to the requirement it covers.
</Callout>

## Common pitfalls

- **Superseded records**: replacing an old file keeps the statutory obligation satisfied as long as the current version is available.
- **Unit-count changes**: crossing the 25-unit (condo) or 100-parcel (HOA) threshold flips new requirements from Not Applicable to required.

## Where to look

- Open **Documents** to review what's posted today.
- Open **Compliance** to see what's missing or overdue.
- Screenshot the compliance dashboard before each board meeting to keep the board oriented.
