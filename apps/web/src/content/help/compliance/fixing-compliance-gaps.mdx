Every row on the compliance dashboard falls into one of four buckets. This is a practical guide to moving each one to Satisfied.

## Overdue

The record is required and past the statutory window. Top priority.

**How to fix**:

1. Identify the specific record needed — the row title tells you (e.g., "Annual Budget — 2026").
2. Upload it under the correct category on Documents. Set the finalization date to when it was actually approved, not today.
3. If the record truly doesn't exist, see the "Document doesn't exist" section below.

<Callout type="warning" title="Don't backdate">
If a document was genuinely late, don't set a fictitious earlier finalization date. The audit log preserves both; creating false early dates is worse than late posting.
</Callout>

## Unsatisfied (not yet due)

The record is required but its deadline hasn't passed. Fix proactively before it flips to Overdue.

**How to fix**:

- If the record exists, upload it now.
- If it's pending (e.g., minutes awaiting board approval), schedule the approval step and post immediately after.

## Not applicable

The requirement truly doesn't apply to your community type or size. Mark it so.

**How to fix**:

- Open the row and click **Mark as Not Applicable**.
- Enter a short reason for the audit log (e.g., "Community has 12 units; §718.111(12)(g) website requirement applies only to 25+ units").
- The row drops out of the score calculation.

## Satisfied

The record is posted and within the required window. Nothing to do — verify during your monthly audit that the right file is linked.

## Document doesn't exist

Sometimes the compliance row asks for a document your community never produced — an old reserve study, minutes from a meeting that didn't happen, etc.

**Options**:

1. **Commission the missing record** — if the community should have it (for example, you need a current reserve study), engage the appropriate professional.
2. **Mark as Not Applicable with a robust reason** — only if the requirement truly doesn't apply.
3. **Acknowledge the gap in minutes** — if the record is permanently lost, note this in the next board meeting and post the minutes showing the gap was discussed.

## Late posting patterns

If late postings are happening repeatedly, the fix is procedural:

- Add a standing agenda item "Confirm compliance posting caught up this week" to board meetings.
- Appoint a specific owner (CAM, secretary) for each category.
- Set calendar reminders 20 days before known deadlines.

## When a row reads wrong

If PropertyPro claims a requirement exists that you don't think applies, open a feedback note on this help article and message your success contact. Statutes and community types occasionally drift from what the engine expects — we'd rather fix the engine than force you to mark Not Applicable with a weak reason.

## After every fix

Refresh the dashboard. The score should tick up as rows flip to Satisfied. If it doesn't:

- Verify the file is under the correct **category**.
- Check the **finalization date** isn't in the future.
- Confirm the file uploaded cleanly (non-zero bytes, opens correctly).

## Keeping the score high

Aim for no Overdue rows, ever. Aware-colored rows (8–30 days to deadline) are the comfort zone. If you're regularly fire-fighting, your cadence is too loose — tighten the process before the score crashes.
