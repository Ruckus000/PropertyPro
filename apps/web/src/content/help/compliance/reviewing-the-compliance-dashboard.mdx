The compliance dashboard is your single source of truth for which statutory obligations are satisfied, which are overdue, and which need attention soon. Work it top to bottom and the weekly review stays short.

<Callout type="florida-statute" title="What this tracks">
Documents and meeting notices that Florida statutes require your association to post within a specific window. For condos that's mainly §718.111(12)(g). For HOAs with 100+ parcels it's §720.303. The calculator enforces both.
</Callout>

## Read the score first

The top card shows your current compliance score and how it has trended over the last 90 days. Below it, requirements are grouped into four buckets:

- **Satisfied** — required records are posted on time.
- **Overdue** — past the statutory window and still missing.
- **Unsatisfied** — required but not yet due, or posted late.
- **Not applicable** — requirements that don't apply to this community type.

Start with **Overdue** before anything else. Those are the entries that will keep your score below where it could be.

## Work a gap to resolution

<StepByStep>
<Step title="Open an overdue item">
Click the row to see what record is missing — often a document category (like "Annual budget") or a meeting minute.
</Step>

<Step title="Post the record">
Follow the link into Documents or Meetings to upload or attach the missing file. The compliance dashboard refreshes automatically once the record is accepted.
</Step>

<Step title="Recheck the score">
Return to the dashboard. The row should now show as Satisfied, and your score should tick up.
</Step>

<Step title="Mark genuine exemptions">
If a requirement truly does not apply (for example, a 25-unit rule for a 20-unit building), mark it Not Applicable with a short reason so the entry stops influencing the score.
</Step>
</StepByStep>

## Watch the 30-day window

<Callout type="warning" title="Post within 30 days">
Condo records must be posted within 30 days of being finalized. PropertyPro flags anything within 7 days of the deadline so you can act before an item flips to Overdue. Treat the Aware and Urgent colors as your weekly reminder.
</Callout>

## Keep the cadence

- **Weekly**: clear the Overdue bucket. If anything's stuck, note why in the associated document.
- **Monthly**: audit Not Applicable entries. If community type changed (for example, new unit count), some may need to flip back to required.
- **Quarterly**: capture the compliance summary for the board packet (screenshot the dashboard or follow your community's existing reporting process).

## What this page does not do

PropertyPro surfaces the timing and completeness of required records. It does not provide legal advice. If you're unsure whether a specific document satisfies a statute, loop in your association attorney.
