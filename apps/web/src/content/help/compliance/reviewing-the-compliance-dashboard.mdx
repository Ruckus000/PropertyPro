## Understand the current status

The compliance dashboard groups obligations into satisfied, overdue, unsatisfied, and not applicable states so you can quickly see what needs attention.

## Work from the gaps

Start with overdue and unsatisfied items. Those usually point to missing documents, notices, or meeting records that need follow-up.

## Keep records fresh

Posting records promptly and keeping meeting materials current helps the dashboard stay accurate and reduces last-minute cleanup work.
