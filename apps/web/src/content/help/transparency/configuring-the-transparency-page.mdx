The transparency page is a community-facing site your association can publish to display posting and access-control status for governance records, financial summaries, and SIRS status. It helps boards track what is on file — it does not by itself satisfy Florida's website or records-posting obligations.

<Callout type="florida-statute" title="Florida website and records requirements">
Florida law may require certain condominium and homeowners' associations to make specified records available through an association website or mobile application, including protected electronic sections for owner or employee access, as applicable. For condominium associations with 25 or more non-timeshare units, §718.111(12)(g) requires specified records to be posted or made available digitally and requires a protected electronic location that is inaccessible to the general public and accessible only to unit owners and association employees. Unit owners must receive username and password access on written request for protected sections containing required notices, records, or documents.

For homeowners' associations with 100 or more parcels, §720.303 imposes a similar website or application requirement and protected-access framework for parcel owners and association employees, with specified documents to be posted or made available through the association's website or mobile app.

The categories and timing of required records differ between condominium associations and homeowners' associations.

PropertyPro's transparency page shows posting and access-control status. It does not determine which records your association must post, which records must be redacted, or which users must receive access credentials. Protected access does not override statutory redaction or non-disclosure requirements. Consult counsel on posting, redaction, and credential procedures.
</Callout>

## What shows on the page

The transparency page has four sections. Each shows posting **status** — not the documents themselves:

- **Document Posting Status** — checklist rows with Posted, Not yet posted, or Not required labels and dates.
- **Meeting Compliance Records** — meeting notice compliance records.
- **Minutes Tracking** — a grid showing which months have minutes on file.
- **Access Controls** — portal password protection, individual credentials, and public notices page status.

The page refreshes automatically as compliance checklist rows are linked or unlinked.

## Initial setup

<StepByStep>
<Step title="Open Settings → Transparency">
Visit `/settings/transparency` (admin-only).
</Step>

<Step title="Confirm the public URL">
The transparency page publishes at your community's public subdomain. Verify that it resolves.
</Step>

<Step title="Acknowledge and enable">
Check the acknowledgment box, then click **Save Settings** to enable the page. It goes live immediately at `/<subdomain>/transparency`. Disabling the toggle and saving takes the page down.
</Step>
</StepByStep>

## SIRS transparency

For condos of a certain age and size, Florida requires a milestone inspection and a structural integrity reserve study (SIRS). The transparency page displays:

- Whether a milestone inspection has been completed
- The completion date
- A brief summary of findings (as provided by the engineer)

<Callout type="warning" title="PropertyPro doesn't assess adequacy">
The SIRS section displays factual data — inspection completed, date, engineer's summary. PropertyPro does not evaluate whether reserves are adequate or whether the inspection found issues that require action. That assessment is the engineer's and the board's responsibility.
</Callout>

## What not to include

The transparency page is public. Do not include:

- Personal information about individual owners (addresses, phone numbers, balances)
- Ongoing litigation details
- Attorney-client privileged communications
- Information on open violations affecting specific units

The page is public and never renders owner-level data such as these — there is no exclusion filter to configure.

## Updating

The page updates automatically as compliance checklist rows are linked or unlinked.

## Frequently asked questions

- **"Can we password-protect records?"** Florida law expressly contemplates protected electronic areas — for condos, accessible only to unit owners and association employees; for HOAs with 100+ parcels under §720.303, accessible to parcel owners and association employees. It does not divide records into a simple "public internet" vs. "password-gated" model. Consult your attorney on which records must be posted publicly, which belong in a protected owner-access area, and how to issue credentials.
- **"Can we restrict who sees historical meeting minutes?"** The statute governs what must be posted or made available; historical minutes are generally included. Check with your attorney if you're unsure about a specific period or access tier.
- **"Does this replace individual owner access?"** No — §718.111(12)(g) and §720.303 each require website or app availability plus protected owner access for covered associations. PropertyPro's transparency page is one tool for tracking status; it does not replace owner portal access or counsel-approved credential procedures.
