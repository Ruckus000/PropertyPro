The transparency page is the community-facing website your association publishes to meet Florida's open-records requirements. It displays factual information about your community — governance records, financial summaries, SIRS status — without requiring visitors to log in.

<Callout type="florida-statute" title="Why it exists">
§718.111(12)(g) requires condo associations with 25+ units to maintain a website with specific posted records. §720.303 imposes similar requirements on HOAs with 100+ parcels. The transparency page is PropertyPro's answer to that statutory obligation.
</Callout>

## What shows on the page

The transparency page has four sections. Each shows posting **status** — not the documents themselves:

- **Document Posting Status** — checklist rows with Posted, Not yet posted, or Not required labels and dates.
- **Meeting Compliance Records** — meeting notice compliance records.
- **Minutes Tracking** — a grid showing which months have minutes on file.
- **Access Controls** — portal password protection, individual credentials, and public notices page status.

The page refreshes automatically as compliance checklist rows are linked or unlinked.

## Initial setup

<StepByStep>
<Step title="Open Settings → Transparency">
Visit `/settings/transparency` (admin-only).
</Step>

<Step title="Confirm the public URL">
The transparency page publishes at your community's public subdomain. Verify that it resolves.
</Step>

<Step title="Acknowledge and enable">
Check the acknowledgment box, then click **Save Settings** to enable the page. It goes live immediately at `/<subdomain>/transparency`. Disabling the toggle and saving takes the page down.
</Step>
</StepByStep>

## SIRS transparency

For condos of a certain age and size, Florida requires a milestone inspection and a structural integrity reserve study (SIRS). The transparency page displays:

- Whether a milestone inspection has been completed
- The completion date
- A brief summary of findings (as provided by the engineer)

<Callout type="warning" title="PropertyPro doesn't assess adequacy">
The SIRS section displays factual data — inspection completed, date, engineer's summary. PropertyPro does not evaluate whether reserves are adequate or whether the inspection found issues that require action. That assessment is the engineer's and the board's responsibility.
</Callout>

## What not to include

The transparency page is public. Do not include:

- Personal information about individual owners (addresses, phone numbers, balances)
- Ongoing litigation details
- Attorney-client privileged communications
- Information on open violations affecting specific units

The page is public and never renders owner-level data such as these — there is no exclusion filter to configure.

## Updating

The page updates automatically as compliance checklist rows are linked or unlinked.

## Frequently asked questions

- **"Can we password-protect it?"** The transparency page is an opt-in feature your association controls; consult your attorney about what your community must make available (§718.111(12)(g) / §720.303).
- **"Can we restrict who sees historical meeting minutes?"** The statute governs what must be posted; historical minutes are generally included. Check with your attorney if you're unsure about a specific period.
- **"Does this replace individual owner access?"** No — owners retain their individual access to the full records under separate provisions of Florida statute.
