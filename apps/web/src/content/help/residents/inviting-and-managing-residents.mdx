The resident roster is the source of truth for who can see what in your community. Keeping it clean speeds up everything from announcements to violation handling.

## Invitation vs. self-service

Two paths add residents:

- **Invitation** — you send an invite; resident clicks the link and lands ready to go.
- **Self-service join** — resident submits a request; you approve or deny.

Invitations are faster and ensure unit numbers are correct from the start. Self-service is useful for communities where you can't easily get resident emails in advance.

## Sending an invitation

<StepByStep>
<Step title="Open Residents">
From the sidebar, click Residents (or `/dashboard/residents`).
</Step>

<Step title="Click Add Resident">
Enter the resident's email, unit number, and role. Keep **Send invitation email** checked to email them a sign-up link pre-filled with their unit and role.
</Step>

<Step title="Review and send">
The resident receives an email invitation with a direct sign-up link.
</Step>

<Step title="Resend if needed">
Each resident row has a **Send Invite** button to resend the invitation. There is no Sent/Accepted/Expired status tracking in the list.
</Step>
</StepByStep>

## Bulk invitations

For onboarding at scale (new communities, quarterly new-owner intake):

1. Download the CSV template from the Import page (`name`, `email`, `role`, `unit_number` columns).
2. Fill in each row.
3. Upload. PropertyPro validates the file and creates resident records.
4. Review the summary — any rows that failed validation show the reason.
5. Send invitations afterward using **Send Invite** on each resident row — import does not bulk-email invitations.

## Role changes and offboarding

Changing a resident's role or removing them from the community is not available in the UI today. Contact PropertyPro support if you need to update roles or revoke access.

## Merging duplicate residents

If a resident has two accounts (old email, new email), contact PropertyPro support. There is no automatic merge and no self-service email-change or removal flow in the UI.

## Audit trail

Resident invitations and roster changes are captured in the compliance audit log. If a resident later disputes access or role assignment, you have a timestamped record.
