In an emergency your association can alert everyone at once. Alerts come by email, and by text message if your community has text alerts turned on.

<Figure shot="em-r" alt="SMS Emergency Notifications card in Settings">

To get texts, open **Settings** from your account menu. Under **Text Message Alerts**, select **Verify Phone Number**, enter the code we text you, then check the consent box.

</Figure>

- Emergency alerts are emailed even if you have turned off other email.
- If you do not see **Text Message Alerts** in Settings, your community sends alerts by email only.
- Reply STOP to any text to stop them.
- If you are in danger, call **911** first.
