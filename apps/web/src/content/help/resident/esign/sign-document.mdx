<StepByStep>

<Step title="Open the request" shot="sg-card">

Select the link in the email, or **Sign now** on your dashboard’s **Documents to Sign** card. The card also shows when the request expires.

</Step>

<Step title="Read the document and select each highlighted field" shot="sg-field">

The bar at the top shows who you are signing as. If the sender added a note, select **Message from sender** to read it. Type into any text fields; the counter at the bottom shows how many fields are done.

</Step>

<Step title="Draw, type or upload your signature" shot="sg-modal">

A window opens with **Draw**, **Type** and **Upload** tabs. On a phone, drawing with your finger works well. Uploads must be PNG or JPG, under 2 MB.

</Step>

<Step title="Check the consent box and select Finish" shot="sg-finish">

**Finish** turns on once every required field is done and you have agreed to sign electronically.

</Step>

</StepByStep>

When you finish, the page says **Signing complete**.

- The signed copy is not emailed to you. Ask the sender for a copy.
- If something is wrong, select **Decline**, add a reason, and select **Confirm Decline** rather than ignoring it.
- If the request has expired or was cancelled, the page says so. Ask the sender for a new link.
- If others must sign first, the page shows **Waiting for another signer** until it is your turn.

<Callout type="florida-statute" title="Legal effect">

Under the federal E-Sign Act and Florida’s Uniform Electronic Transactions Act, your electronic signature counts the same as ink once you consent to signing electronically.

</Callout>
