Open **Reserves** in the sidebar. The **Reserve Register** lists each major component with its category, install year and expected life.

<Figure shot="rv-list" alt="Reserve Register with component cards and years left">

Each component shows years left, **Due this year**, or **Past expected life**, with its expected end-of-life year. Replacement cost and the amount currently reserved appear when the association has entered them.

</Figure>

The countdown is simple arithmetic on the numbers the association entered. It does not reflect the component’s condition or whether enough money is saved.

<Callout type="info" title="Not a reserve study">

The register is not a reserve study, a SIRS or a milestone inspection. Those are separate documents in **Documents**.

</Callout>
