**Insurance** in the sidebar shows the association’s master-policy summary and its wind-mitigation reports. Owners can see it; tenants cannot.

<Figure shot="ins-card" alt="Master Policy section with the policy card, dates and deductibles" />

## Certificate for your lender

<StepByStep>

<Step title="Select Request a Certificate for My Lender">

It is on the policy card under **Master Policy**. If it is grayed out, the association has not added the agent’s email yet.

</Step>

<Step title="Enter your unit and the lender’s details" shot="ins-coi">

Enter **Your unit**, the **Lender / title company** and **Their email**. Add the **Loan / reference number** if you have it, then select **Send request**. The request goes to the association’s insurance agent, who replies to you directly. PropertyPro does not issue certificates.

</Step>

</StepByStep>

## Wind-mitigation report

Florida insurers must offer discounts for wind-resistant construction. Under **Wind Mitigation**, select **Download Report**, then **Send to My Insurance Agent**. This opens a ready-made email in your mail app; attach the PDF you downloaded. Your insurer decides what credit applies. Shutters and impact windows may need your own paperwork.

<Figure shot="ins-wind" alt="Wind Mitigation section with Download Report and Send to My Insurance Agent buttons" />

If a report has expired, you are warned before it downloads or the email opens.
