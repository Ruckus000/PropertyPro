<Callout type="warning" title="This is not an insurance claim">

A report creates a dated record for your association or property manager. It does not notify any insurer or start a claim. PropertyPro is not a public adjuster (§626.854). Contact your own insurer to file a claim; claim deadlines apply and the app does not track them.

</Callout>

<StepByStep>

<Step title="Select Report Damage" shot="st-btn">

Open **Storm Damage** in the sidebar. The button is at the top right.

</Step>

<Step title="Describe it" shot="st-form">

Enter the **Location** (“Building B, 3rd floor hallway”), the **Type of damage** (roof, water / flooding, structural, exterior / siding, common area, other), the **Severity**, and a **Description** of what you saw. **When did it happen?** is optional. Do not include anyone’s personal details; the board and management can read the report.

</Step>

<Step title="Select Report Damage">

It appears in your list as **Logged**. Management changes the status as they review it. You see only your own reports.

</Step>

</StepByStep>

For anything life-threatening, call **911** first.
