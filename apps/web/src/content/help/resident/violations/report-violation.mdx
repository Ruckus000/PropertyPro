<StepByStep>

<Step title="Select Report Violation">

It is in the sidebar under **Management**.

</Step>

<Step title="Choose the unit">

Under **Unit the report is about**, pick the home where the problem is. That is usually a neighbour's unit; your own units are marked **(your unit)**.

</Step>

<Step title="Choose the category and severity" shot="vr-cat">

Pick a **Category**, such as **Noise**, **Parking** or **Unauthorized Modification**, and a **Severity**: **Minor**, **Moderate** or **Major**.

</Step>

<Step title="Describe what happened and add photos" shot="vr-desc">

Say what, when and where in **Description** (up to 4,000 characters). Under **Photo Evidence** you can add up to 3 photos (JPEG, PNG, WebP or GIF, up to 10 MB each). Photos are the best evidence.

</Step>

<Step title="Select Submit Violation Report" shot="vr-send">

The association reviews it. Your report is listed under **Your reports** below the form, with its status. A report about your own unit opens to its full record; a report about another home does not, because that record belongs to that household and the board.

</Step>

</StepByStep>

<Callout type="info" title="Common areas go to maintenance">

A violation is filed against a unit. For a problem in a shared space, such as a hallway, the pool or the parking garage, or for something that needs repair, submit a maintenance request instead.

</Callout>

<Callout type="info" title="Who sees your report">

The household you report does not see your name; only the board and management do. While your report is still **Reported**, it is not shown to that household at all. They see it once the association acts on it, for example by sending a notice.

</Callout>

<Callout type="info" title="There is a daily limit">

You can file up to 10 reports a day. If you reach it, the form tells you when you can file again.

</Callout>

<Callout type="info" title="Your account needs a unit">

If the page says you are not linked to a unit yet, you cannot file a report. Ask your manager to link your unit.

</Callout>
