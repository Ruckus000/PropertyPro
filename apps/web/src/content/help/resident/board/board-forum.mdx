The forum is under **Board**, but everyone in the community can read and post. Managers can pin and lock threads and delete replies.

<Figure shot="fr-list" alt="Forum with a pinned thread and recent discussions" />

<StepByStep>

<Step title="Open Board, then Forum, and select New Thread" shot="fr-new">

Give it a clear **Title** and a short opening post in **Body**.

</Step>

<Step title="Select Create Thread">

Everyone in the community can open it and reply with **Post Reply**. Posts and replies are plain text, up to 8,000 characters. You can delete your own replies; a deleted reply leaves a placeholder so the conversation still makes sense.

</Step>

</StepByStep>

A **Locked** thread can be read but not replied to.

<Callout type="tip" title="Posts are permanent records">

Forum posts are kept as community records and may be read in a legal dispute. Keep them factual and polite. Forum search is not available yet, so check pinned threads first.

</Callout>
