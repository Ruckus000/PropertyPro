Florida law lets you vote electronically only if you have agreed to e-voting. If you have not, or prefer paper, ask your association for a paper ballot. If **Board** has no **Elections** tab, elections are not turned on for your community yet.

<StepByStep>

<Step title="Open Board, then Elections" shot="bal-open">

Each election shows when it opens and closes, and its status. Select one, then **View Details** to see the candidates.

</Step>

<Step title="Choose your candidates and cast your ballot" shot="bal-cast">

Under **Cast your ballot**, pick up to the number of candidates shown, or choose **Abstain**. Select **Review Ballot**, check your choices, then **Cast Ballot**. Each unit gets one ballot, even if several owners share it. You get a receipt code as proof your ballot was received.

</Step>

</StepByStep>

<OnlyFor types="condo_718">

<Callout type="florida-statute" title="Florida Statute §718.128">

Each owner must agree to electronic voting separately. Paper ballots stay available to anyone who wants one. When an election uses a secret ballot, the review step says so.

</Callout>

</OnlyFor>
