The digest is built from what is already in PropertyPro, so nobody has to write it. It skips any period with no new activity. It goes to unit owners once the community turns it on: weekly digests arrive on Mondays, monthly ones on the 1st.

<StepByStep>

<Step title="Open Settings">

Select your initials at the top right, then **Settings**.

</Step>

<Step title="Choose how often" shot="dig-card">

On the **Community digest** card, under **How often you receive it**, choose **Weekly**, **Monthly** or **Off**. The change saves right away. Every digest email also has a one-click unsubscribe link.

</Step>

</StepByStep>

If the card says the board has not turned on the community digest yet, your community has not started sending it. Ask your manager.

<Callout type="info" title="Not an official notice">

Meeting notices, voting and other required notices are still sent separately. The digest does not replace them.

</Callout>
