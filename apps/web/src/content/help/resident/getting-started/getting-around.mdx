<StepByStep>

<Step title="Use the tabs" shot="gs-nav-r">

The tabs across the top of the page are **Dashboard**, **Documents**, **Meetings** and **Announcements**. Everything else is under **More**, grouped by **Community** and **Management**: items such as **Board**, **Operations**, **Payments** (unit owners only) and **Report Violation** appear there when your community uses them. On a phone, tap the menu icon at the top left for the same list.

</Step>

<Step title="Search your community" shot="gs-search">

The search bar at the top of every page finds pages, documents, announcements, meetings and your maintenance requests. Press **⌘K** (Ctrl+K on Windows) to open it from anywhere.

</Step>

<Step title="Open help" shot="gs-help">

Select the **?** button at the top right, or press **?** on your keyboard. Help opens with guides for the page you are on.

</Step>

</StepByStep>

<Figure shot="gs-helppanel" alt="Help panel with articles relevant to the dashboard">

Under **Relevant to this page**, the help panel suggests articles for where you are. Its search covers all help articles and your community’s FAQs. Select **Visit Help Center** to see everything.

</Figure>

Your account menu is the circle with your initials at the top right. It has **Settings**, **Help Center**, **Data Export** and **Log out**. **Data Export** works for board members and managers.

<OnlyFor types="condo_718 hoa_720">

<Callout type="info" title="If you hold a board seat">

A board seat adds two things to a normal owner account: running elections from **Board → Elections**, and exporting community records from **Data Export**. Guides for both appear in this help center once you are seated. Day-to-day administration, such as uploading documents or posting notices, is done by your manager.

</Callout>

</OnlyFor>
