The dashboard is the first page after you sign in. It tells you, in one sentence, whether anything needs you.

<OnlyFor types="condo_718 hoa_720">

<Figure shot="dash-r" alt="Dashboard with a status sentence and the This week schedule" />

The first card is a short sentence. It says **You're all caught up** or tells you how many documents are waiting for your signature. It also names the next meeting and when it is.

<StepByStep start={2}>

<Step title="This week" shot="dash-week">

The next seven days, starting today, with meetings and other dates. Days with nothing on them say **Nothing scheduled**. Open **Meetings** for the agenda and notice.

</Step>

</StepByStep>

## Quick actions

Below the schedule is a row of buttons for common jobs, such as **Ask for a repair**, **Find a document**, **Report a problem** and **Update my contact info**. A button only appears if your community uses that feature. With a board seat, a **Sign** button with a number also appears when documents are waiting. Everyone else can see waiting documents in the status sentence at the top and in the **Documents to Sign** card.

## The numbers

A row of counts links to the full pages. **Open violations** takes you to the page where you report a rule violation. **Upcoming meetings** counts meetings in the next 30 days.

## Further down

<StepByStep start={3}>

<Step title="Documents to Sign" shot="dash-sign">

Appears only when something is waiting for your signature. Select a document to sign it.

</Step>

<Step title="Recent Announcements" shot="dash-ann">

Up to five announcements, pinned ones first, then newest. Select the **Announcements** tab at the top of the page to read the full post.

</Step>

</StepByStep>

## Board members

With a board seat you also see **Needs attention**, a to-do list next to **This week**. It lists late and upcoming compliance documents, meeting notices to post and violation hearing notices. Select **View** on a row to see the item. Owners and tenants without a board seat do not see this list.

<Figure shot="dash-board" alt="Needs attention list as a board member sees it, with a View button on each row" />

With a board seat, the numbers also include the community-wide count of open maintenance requests. Select **Open violations** to see every unit's violations. See **Review and enforce violations**.

</OnlyFor>

<OnlyFor types="apartment">

In an apartment community the dashboard shows a **Maintenance** card with the number of open requests, **Recent Announcements**, and **Quick Actions** such as **New Maintenance Request**.

</OnlyFor>

A short setup checklist may appear at the top, with steps like reading the latest announcement and setting your notification preferences. Select the **X** to dismiss it.
