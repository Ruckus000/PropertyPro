Most people join from an invitation email. Open the link in the email and set your password, and your account is linked right away. If you did not get an invitation, send a join request.

## Send a join request

<StepByStep>

<Step title="Select Join a community">

If your account is not linked to any community yet, PropertyPro shows **Let’s get you connected** after you sign in. Select **Join a community**.

</Step>

<Step title="Find your community" shot="gs-join-comm">

Under **Search for your community**, type at least two letters of the name, then select it. Only communities that use PropertyPro are listed.

</Step>

<Step title="Enter your unit and role" shot="gs-join-unit">

In **Unit identifier**, type your unit exactly as it appears on association records, such as “Unit 101” or “Lot 12”. Under **I am a(n)**, choose **Owner** or **Tenant**.

</Step>

<Step title="Select Submit Request" shot="gs-join-submit">

Your community’s manager reviews it. You get a notification when it is approved. If it is not approved, the notification includes the reason when the manager gave one.

</Step>

</StepByStep>

<Callout type="tip" title="Common reasons for a delay">

The unit does not match association records (check leading zeros and letters), or you chose the wrong role. You can have one pending request per community, and you cannot edit it. If a request is not approved, you can send a new one after 30 days. Contact your manager if it needs changing sooner.

</Callout>

Already a member of one community and want to join another? Ask that community’s manager to add you.

## Switch between communities

If you belong to more than one community, select the community name at the top of the sidebar and choose another. You can also open your account menu at the top right and select **Switch Community**. The whole portal, including help, switches to that community.
