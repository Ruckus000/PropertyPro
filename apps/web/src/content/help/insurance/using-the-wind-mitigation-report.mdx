Your building has been inspected for wind resistance — how the roof is attached, how it connects to the walls, how openings are protected. Florida insurers must offer discounts for these features, and your insurer may not know about them unless you tell them. You can't inspect the building yourself, so your association shares the report with you.

<Callout type="tip" title="Worth ten minutes at renewal">
The best time to do this is when your policy is up for renewal, or when you're shopping for a new one.
</Callout>

## Send it to your insurer

1. Go to **Insurance** in the sidebar.
2. Choose **Download Report** and save the PDF.
3. Choose **Send to My Insurance Agent** — this opens a pre-written email in your mail app. **Attach the PDF you just downloaded**, then send it.

Your agent will reply to you directly about whether any wind-mitigation credits apply to your policy.

## What to expect

The report describes the building as it was on the inspection date shown. Your insurer decides what credits, if any, apply to your policy — it depends on your coverage and your carrier. Some policies see a meaningful reduction in the wind portion of the premium; others see less.

PropertyPro doesn't contact your insurer and doesn't provide insurance advice. We just make sure the document is in your hands.

## Frequently asked questions

**My report says "Expired." Is it useless?**
Insurers generally accept these forms for about five years. An expired form may not be honored — your board has been notified and can commission a new inspection.

**What about my shutters or impact windows?**
Openings are often specific to your unit and may not be covered by the building-level report. Ask your agent what they need for your own openings.

**I'm renting. Does this help me?**
Wind-mitigation credits apply to the policy covering the unit, so this is mainly useful to owners. If you carry renters insurance, it covers your belongings rather than the structure.

**There's no report here.**
Your board hasn't posted one yet. It's worth asking about — one inspection benefits every owner in the building.
