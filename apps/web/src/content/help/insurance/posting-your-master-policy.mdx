Every time a unit sells or refinances, the lender needs to verify the association's master policy — and owners currently chase this through the agent or a paid service. Posting a summary once lets owners see the coverage, download the declarations page, and send a certificate request straight to the agent of record.

<Callout type="info" title="A summary, not the policy">
This is a convenience summary. The insurer's policy and any certificate the agent issues control — enter what the declarations page says and let owners confirm details with the agent.
</Callout>

## Post the policy

1. Go to **Insurance** and choose **Add Policy**.
2. Enter the carrier, policy type, and dates. Type the coverage and deductible **as written on the declarations page** — including the hurricane deductible (often a percentage, not a dollar amount).
3. **Add the agent of record's email.** This is what lets owners send certificate requests to the right person — without it, the request button stays disabled.
4. Optionally attach the declarations-page PDF from your document library so owners can download it.

## At renewal

Florida master policies change carriers often. When the policy renews, edit the summary and upload the new declarations page — a two-minute job. The page shows a warning banner once a policy's expiry date passes, so owners always know if the summary might be stale.

## Frequently asked questions

**Does PropertyPro issue the certificate?**
No. Only the licensed agent can issue a certificate of insurance. PropertyPro relays the owner's request to the agent, who replies to the owner directly.

**Why free-text coverage and deductibles?**
Declarations pages express limits in many forms (blanket limits, sublimits, percentage hurricane deductibles). Free text lets you post exactly what the page says instead of forcing it into fields that could mislead.

**Do tenants see this?**
No. The master-policy summary is visible to unit owners and staff, not tenants.
