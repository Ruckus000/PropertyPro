Selling or refinancing your unit? Your lender or title company needs a certificate of insurance showing the association's master policy. Instead of chasing the agent yourself, you can send the request from PropertyPro and the agent handles it.

<Callout type="tip" title="Do this early in your closing">
Certificates can take a few business days, so send the request as soon as you have your lender's details.
</Callout>

## Send the request

1. Go to **Insurance** and find the **Master Policy** card.
2. Choose **Request a Certificate for My Lender**.
3. Enter your unit, and your lender or title company's name and email (and loan number if you have it).
4. Send it.

The association's insurance agent receives your request and issues the certificate directly to your lender — they'll reply to your email if they need anything.

## Frequently asked questions

**Who actually issues the certificate?**
The association's licensed insurance agent — not PropertyPro. We only pass your request to the agent of record and set the reply to go straight to you.

**The request button is greyed out.**
Your board hasn't added the agent's email to the policy yet. Ask them to add the agent of record so requests can be sent.

**How will I know it's done?**
The agent replies to you directly, usually within a few business days. Forward the certificate to your lender once you receive it.
