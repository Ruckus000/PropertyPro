<StepByStep>

<Step title="Open Board, then Polls" shot="poll-list">

**Board** opens on **Polls**. Only open polls are listed. A poll with an end date shows when it ends and closes automatically.

</Step>

<Step title="Pick your answer and select Submit Vote" shot="poll-vote">

Some polls allow more than one answer. You cannot change your vote once it is submitted. Results appear on the poll after you vote, or when it ends.

</Step>

</StepByStep>

<Callout type="info" title="Polls are not binding">

Polls help the board understand opinions. Decisions that are legally binding, such as board elections, use a formal ballot.

</Callout>
