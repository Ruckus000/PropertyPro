Payments is shown to owners. Tenants do not see it.

<Figure shot="pay-cards" alt="Current Balance, Total Due and Overdue Items cards">

The cards show your **Current Balance**, the **Total Due** on unpaid charges, and how many **Overdue Items** you have.

</Figure>

<StepByStep>

<Step title="Check the charges">

Open **Payments** in the sidebar. The **Upcoming** tab lists unpaid charges with the due date, status, amount, any late fee and the total. If you own more than one unit, choose it under **Unit** first. If something looks wrong, contact your manager **before** paying; a payment is much harder to undo than a question.

</Step>

<Step title="Select Pay Now" shot="pay-now">

It is at the end of each unpaid charge. If there is no **Pay Now** button, your association does not take payments through PropertyPro yet; the page shows who to contact to arrange payment.

</Step>

<Step title="Enter card or bank details" shot="pay-form">

The **Make Payment** window shows the amount and any late fee. Choose **Card** or **US bank account** and enter your details. They go straight to Stripe and are not saved in PropertyPro.

</Step>

<Step title="Select Pay">

The button shows the amount, for example **Pay $350.00**. Once the payment goes through, the charge moves to **Payment History** and a receipt is emailed to you. Bank account payments can take a few business days to clear.

</Step>

</StepByStep>

## Statements and history

The **Payment History** tab lists past payments with the due date, the date paid, the amount and the status. Select **Download PDF** for a statement of your account.

<Figure shot="pay-hist" alt="Payment History tab" />

<Callout type="tip" title="Card declined?">

It is usually your bank. Try another card or pay by bank account.

</Callout>
