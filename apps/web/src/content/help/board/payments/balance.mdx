- **Charges** are your regular assessments (the recurring dues every owner pays) and any one-time special assessment the board approved, such as for a roof. Each one is a row with its **Due Date**.
- **Status** is **Pending** until the charge is paid, or **Overdue** once the due date has passed. **Partially paid** means part of it has been paid.
- **Late Fee** is a flat fee added once when a charge is still unpaid after the grace period your association set. **Total** is the amount plus any late fee.
- **Current Balance** covers everything on your account. A negative balance is a credit, for example from an overpayment or a correction.

<Figure shot="bal-clear" alt="All caught up message">

When everything is paid, the **Upcoming** tab says **All caught up!**

</Figure>

## Common questions

- **I paid by check.** PropertyPro only records payments made with **Pay Now**. If you paid another way and the charge still shows as unpaid, contact your manager.
- **I was charged twice.** Contact your manager right away.
- **The late fee looks wrong.** Check your governing documents, then ask your manager. Managers can waive late fees.
