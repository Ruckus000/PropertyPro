<StepByStep>

<Step title="Open Account Settings">

Select your initials at the top right, then **Settings**. Under **Personal Settings**, select **Account**.

</Step>

<Step title="Edit your name or phone" shot="acc-profile">

**Full Name** is what neighbors and staff see. **Phone** is optional. Your sign-in **Email** cannot be changed here; contact support to change it.

</Step>

<Step title="Select Save Changes" shot="acc-save">

The change takes effect right away.

</Step>

</StepByStep>

## Change your password

Under **Password**, enter your **Current Password**, then your **New Password** and **Confirm New Password**, and select **Update Password**. The new password needs at least 8 characters, with an uppercase letter, a lowercase letter, a number and a special character. Use a password you do not use anywhere else.

<Figure shot="acc-pw" alt="Password section with current and new password fields" />

## If you forgot your password

- On the sign-in page, select **Forgot password?** and enter your email.
- Open the reset link in the email. It expires; if it stops working, request a new one.
- No email? Check spam, and make sure you used the address on file.

<Callout type="warning" title="If someone else may have signed in">

Change your password right away, check that your email and phone have not changed, and tell your community manager.

</Callout>
