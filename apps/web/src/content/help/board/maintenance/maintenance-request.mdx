<Callout type="warning" title="Emergencies">

For gas, flooding, fire or a safety threat, call **911** and your association’s emergency line first. Submit a request afterward for the record.

</Callout>

<StepByStep>

<Step title="Select Submit Request" shot="mr-submit">

Open **Operations** in the sidebar. It is at the top right. A **Submit Request** panel opens.

</Step>

<Step title="Enter a title and your unit" shot="mr-cat">

Give it a short **Title**, such as “Kitchen sink leak”. Enter your **Apartment/Unit Number** as it appears on association records, for example 4B.

</Step>

<Step title="Describe the problem" shot="mr-desc">

In **Description**, say where, what you see, and since when: “Leak under the kitchen sink, dripping steadily since this morning.” Mention how staff can get in.

</Step>

<Step title="Choose the category and priority" shot="mr-prio">

**Category** is Plumbing, Electrical, Hvac, General or Other. For **Priority**, use **Urgent** for active damage or a safety problem, **High** or **Normal** for most repairs, and **Low** when there is no rush.

</Step>

<Step title="Add photos" shot="mr-photos">

Under **Photos (max 5)**, choose up to 5 images. One clear photo often saves a visit.

</Step>

<Step title="Select Submit Request" shot="mr-send">

The request appears under **Requests** as **Submitted**. If its status changes, you get an email and a notification.

</Step>

</StepByStep>

<OnlyFor types="condo_718 hoa_720">

For a neighbor breaking the rules, [report a violation](help:report-violation) instead. For exterior changes to your home, [ask for architectural approval](help:arc-request).
