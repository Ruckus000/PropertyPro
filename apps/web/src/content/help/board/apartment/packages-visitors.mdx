## Your packages

You get a notification when the front desk logs a package for your unit. Open **Packages** in the sidebar: **My Packages** lists the ones waiting for pickup, with the carrier, tracking number and when it arrived.

<Figure shot="pk-mine" alt="My Packages list with a package waiting for pickup" />

## Register a guest

<StepByStep>

<Step title="Open Visitors and select Register Visitor" shot="vs-pass">

Choose **One-Time**, **Recurring**, **Vendor** or **Permanent**, and enter the **Visitor Name**, **Purpose** and arrival time. You can add vehicle details. Select **Register Visitor**; a passcode is created. Select **Share** on the pass, or open its QR code, to send it to your guest.

</Step>

</StepByStep>

**My Visitors** shows your unit’s passes under **Active**, **Upcoming** and **Past**. You get a notification when your guest is checked in.
