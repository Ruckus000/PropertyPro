Announcements are updates from your association: maintenance work, rule reminders, events. They appear on your dashboard, on the **Announcements** page, and in email if you have chosen to get them. You see announcements sent to everyone and to owners. Announcements sent to **Board members only** reach you by email.

<Figure shot="an-list" alt="Announcements page with a pinned post at the top" />

<StepByStep>

<Step title="Pinned posts stay at the top" shot="an-pin">

Your association pins urgent posts under **Pinned** until they unpin them. Select a title, or **View details**, to read the whole announcement.

</Step>

</StepByStep>

Some announcements are set to stop showing after a date, such as the end of a maintenance window.

<Callout type="tip" title="Too much email?">

In [notification settings](help:notifications), switch to **Daily digest** or **Weekly digest**, or clear **Announcements**. They still appear on your dashboard.

</Callout>

Announcements are published by your manager. A board seat does not add a way to post one, so to send one to the community, ask your manager.
