The dashboard is the first page after you sign in. It shows what is new and what needs you.

<OnlyFor types="condo_718 hoa_720">

<Figure shot="dash-r" alt="Dashboard with announcement and meeting cards" />

<StepByStep>

<Step title="Recent Announcements" shot="dash-ann">

Up to five announcements, pinned ones first, then newest. Open **Announcements** in the sidebar to read the full post.

</Step>

<Step title="Upcoming Meetings" shot="dash-meet">

The next five meetings with type, date, time and place. Open **Meetings** for the agenda and notice.

</Step>

<Step title="Documents to Sign" shot="dash-sign">

Appears only when something is waiting for your signature. Select a document to sign it.

</Step>

</StepByStep>

Where your community tracks violations, a **Violations** card shows recent reports, with a **Report** link to file a new one.

</OnlyFor>

<OnlyFor types="apartment">

In an apartment community the dashboard shows a **Maintenance** card with the number of open requests, **Recent Announcements**, and **Quick Actions** such as **New Maintenance Request**.

</OnlyFor>

A short setup checklist may appear at the top, with steps like reading the latest announcement and updating your notification preferences. Select the **X** to dismiss it.
