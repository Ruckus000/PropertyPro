Check your architectural guidelines first. They are usually in **Documents** under Governing Documents.

<StepByStep>

<Step title="Open ARC Requests and select New request">

**ARC Requests** is not in the sidebar for residents. Select the search bar at the top of the page (or press **Ctrl+K**, **⌘K** on a Mac), type **ARC** and choose **ARC Applications**. Then select **New request** at the top right.

</Step>

<Step title="Describe the project" shot="arc-new">

Enter a **Title**, choose the **Type of project** (or **Other** and describe it), and explain the work, materials, colors and who will do it under **What are you planning?**. **Estimated start** and **Estimated completion** are optional. If you have more than one unit, pick the **Unit**.

</Step>

<Step title="Select Submit request" shot="arc-send">

You are notified when the committee decides. Any notes they write, including conditions on an approval, appear on your request as **Committee notes**.

</Step>

</StepByStep>

<Callout type="tip" title="Plans and photos">

The request has no attachments. Describe the work in detail, and send drawings or photos to your manager separately if the committee asks for them.

</Callout>

<Figure shot="arc-mine" alt="Your ARC requests with statuses">

Your requests are listed on **ARC Requests** with their status: **Submitted**, **Under Review**, **Approved**, **Denied** or **Withdrawn**. You can select **Withdraw** until a request is decided. Withdrawn requests stay on your list.

</Figure>

<Callout type="info" title="Your account needs a unit">

If the form page says your account isn’t linked to a unit yet, ask your manager to link your unit.

</Callout>
