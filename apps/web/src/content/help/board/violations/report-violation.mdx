<StepByStep>

<Step title="Select Report Violation">

It is in the sidebar under **Management**.

</Step>

<Step title="Choose the category and severity" shot="vr-cat">

Pick a **Category**, such as **Noise**, **Parking** or **Unauthorized Modification**, and a **Severity**: **Minor**, **Moderate** or **Major**.

</Step>

<Step title="Describe what happened and add photos" shot="vr-desc">

Say what, when and where in **Description** (up to 4,000 characters). Under **Photo Evidence** you can add up to 3 photos (JPEG, PNG, WebP or GIF, up to 10 MB each). Photos are the best evidence.

</Step>

<Step title="Select Submit Violation Report" shot="vr-send">

The association reviews it. Your report is listed under **Your Reports** below the form, where you can open it and follow its status.

</Step>

</StepByStep>

<Callout type="info" title="Reports are linked to your unit">

There is no unit field: the report is filed under your own unit. If the problem is at another unit or in a common area, say exactly where in the description.

</Callout>

<Callout type="info" title="Your account needs a unit">

If the page says you are not linked to a unit yet, you cannot file a report. Ask your manager to link your unit.

</Callout>
