Violations recorded against your unit are listed under **Your Reports** on the **Report Violation** page. Open one to see its status, the description and the timeline, including when a notice was sent and any hearing date. When a notice is sent, every owner and tenant of the unit gets an email and an in-app notification. Your association may also send the formal notice by mail.

<Figure shot="vn-notice" alt="Violation with its status, description and timeline" />

Statuses are **Reported**, **Noticed**, **Hearing Scheduled**, **Fined**, **Resolved** and **Dismissed**.

<StepByStep>

<Step title="Fix it, if you agree">

Correct the issue within the time your bylaws or the written notice give. Take a photo and tell your manager.

</Step>

<Step title="Ask, if you are not sure">

Contact your manager or the board with a specific question.

</Step>

<Step title="Dispute it, if you disagree">

Send your explanation and evidence to your manager or the board, and ask for a hearing. Hearings normally need **14 days’** notice. You or a representative can attend.

</Step>

</StepByStep>

<Callout type="florida-statute" title="Notices must give reasons">

Florida law (HB 1203, 2024) requires specific written reasons, usually the rule that was broken. If yours does not cite one, tell the board.

</Callout>

If the deadline passes, the association may fine you. A fine is added to your unit’s balance, which owners see in **Payments**, and the violation shows **Fined**. There is no reply or dispute button on the violation; contact your manager directly.
