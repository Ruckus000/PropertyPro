<StepByStep>

<Step title="Find the pass, or select Register Visitor" shot="vs-reg">

Open **Visitors** in the sidebar. Residents often create passes ahead of time; filter by **Today**, **Expected**, **Checked In** or **All**, or by guest type. For walk-ins, select **Register Visitor** and choose the guest type, name, purpose and **Host Unit**.

</Step>

<Step title="Select Check In" shot="vs-list">

Passes show **Expected**, **Checked In**, **Checked Out**, **Expired**, **Overstayed**, **Revoked** or **Revoked On-Site**. The host resident is notified at check-in.

</Step>

<Step title="If Denied-list match found appears, stop">

The visitor matches an entry on the denied list. Review the match; do not select **Check In Anyway** unless you are sure it is a different person.

</Step>

<Step title="Check out, or revoke with a reason" shot="vs-revoke">

Select **Check Out** when they leave. To refuse or cancel a pass, select **Revoke**, give a specific reason, and select **Revoke Pass**. The host resident is notified.

</Step>

</StepByStep>

## The denied list

Open the **Denied List** tab and select **Add to Denied List** to record a person’s full name, the reason and, if known, their vehicle plate. Check-in compares each visitor against it.

<Figure shot="vs-deny" alt="Denied List tab with the Add to Denied List button" />

<Callout type="warning" title="Visitor records are private">

Share them only with staff, the host resident, and law enforcement with a warrant. Keep records tied to an incident until it is resolved. If anyone is threatening, call **911** first.

</Callout>
