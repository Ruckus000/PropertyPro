<StepByStep>

<Step title="Select Log Package" shot="pk-log">

Open **Packages** in the sidebar. **Log Package** is at the top right of **Package Logging**.

</Step>

<Step title="Enter the unit, recipient and carrier" shot="pk-form">

Enter the **Unit** and **Recipient Name**, and pick the **Carrier** (UPS, FedEx, USPS, Amazon, DHL or Other). **Tracking #** and **Notes** are optional. Select **Log Package**; the unit’s residents get a notification.

</Step>

<Step title="Find it under Pending" shot="pk-pending">

When the resident arrives, select **Pending**, then **Mark Picked Up** on the package’s row.

</Step>

<Step title="Enter who picked it up and select Confirm Pickup" shot="pk-pickup">

Type the name in **Picked Up By**. Only release to the current resident of record.

</Step>

</StepByStep>

<Callout type="tip" title="Say what you are responsible for">

Post a package policy, for example that management makes reasonable efforts but is not liable for loss, and train staff to use the same words.

</Callout>
