<StepByStep>

<Step title="Open Settings" shot="ntf-prefs">

Select your initials at the top right, then **Settings**. Scroll to **Notification Preferences**.

</Step>

<Step title="Choose an email frequency" shot="ntf-freq">

Under **Email frequency**, choose **Immediate**, **Daily digest**, **Weekly digest** or **Never**.

</Step>

<Step title="Choose what you hear about">

Check or clear **Announcements**, **Meeting notices** and **In-app notifications**. Set **Calendar event reminder timing** and choose which reminders you get, such as **Meetings** and **Community assessment due dates**. Where your plan includes the insurance hub, you can also turn **Insurance alerts** on or off.

</Step>

<Step title="Select Save Preferences" shot="ntf-save">

**Preferences saved.** appears when it is done.

</Step>

</StepByStep>

## Your notifications

Select the bell in the top bar to see your latest notifications. Select one to open it, or select **Mark all read**. Select **View all notifications** for the full list, where you can filter by type or show **Unread only**.

<Figure shot="ntf-center" alt="Notifications page with type filters and Mark all read" />

<Callout type="tip" title="Emails not arriving">

Check your spam folder and mark PropertyPro email as “Not spam”. If several residents report the same problem, the sending domain may need to be allowed by your email provider.

</Callout>
