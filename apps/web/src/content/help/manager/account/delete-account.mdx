To leave one community but keep your account, ask that community’s manager to remove you. Deleting your account removes you from every community.

<StepByStep>

<Step title="Open Account Settings and find Danger Zone" shot="del-zone">

Select your initials at the top right, then **Settings**, then **Account**. **Danger Zone** is at the bottom of the page.

</Step>

<Step title="Type DELETE and confirm" shot="del-confirm">

Select **Delete My Account**, type **DELETE**, select **Confirm Deletion**, then enter your password and select **Confirm**.

</Step>

<Step title="Wait out the 30 days, or cancel">

Your account keeps working for 30 days, and **Danger Zone** shows how many days remain. Select **Cancel Deletion** any time before then.

</Step>

</StepByStep>

If you are the root manager of a community, PropertyPro warns you first: nobody can manage billing or the subscription there until another property manager claims the role. Transfer the root role first if you can, or select **Delete anyway**.

After 30 days the account is turned off. Support can restore it for 6 months; after that your name, email and phone number are permanently erased.

<Callout type="warning" title="What your association keeps">

Records you created for the community, such as documents, announcements, signed legal documents and audit-log entries, stay in the community’s records. Once your personal details are erased, they show as “Deleted User”.

</Callout>
