<Callout type="warning" title="This is not an insurance claim">

A report creates a dated record for the association or property manager. It does not notify any insurer or start a claim. PropertyPro is not a public adjuster (§626.854).

</Callout>

Open **Storm Damage** in the sidebar. You see every resident’s report; residents see only their own. You can also file one yourself with **Report Damage**.

<Figure shot="st-list" alt="Storm damage reports with severity, status and the Status menu" />

Use the **Status** menu on each report to move it from **Logged** to **Reviewed by management** or **Archived**. Status is for internal tracking only. Dispatch repairs from [Operations](help:dispatch-work-order).

For anything life-threatening, call **911** first.
