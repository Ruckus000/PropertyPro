## Master policy

<StepByStep>

<Step title="Select Add Policy" shot="insm-add">

Open **Insurance** in the sidebar. **Add Policy** is at the top right of **Master Policy**.

</Step>

<Step title="Copy the declarations page" shot="insm-form">

Choose the **Policy type** and enter the **Carrier** and **Expiry date**. Type the **Coverage summary** and **Deductibles** exactly as written, including a percentage hurricane deductible. **Add the Agent email**; without it owners cannot request certificates. Select **Add Policy**.

</Step>

</StepByStep>

At renewal, select **Edit** on the policy card and enter the new dates and figures. Once a policy expires, owners see an expired-policy warning on it. The policy number is shown only to management.

## Wind-mitigation report

<StepByStep start={3}>

<Step title="Select Add Report and pick the uploaded PDF" shot="insm-wind">

[Upload the report to Documents](help:upload-document) first; the **Inspection report** list shows the **Inspection Reports** and **Insurance** categories by default. Choose the **Form**, **Form revision** and **Inspection date**. **Valid until** fills in five years out; change it if your inspector or insurer says otherwise. Confirm you removed residents’ personal information, then select **Add Report**.

</Step>

</StepByStep>

Management and board members are emailed when a report is 180 days and 30 days from expiring and when it expires, and when the master policy is 60 and 30 days out and when it lapses. To stop them, clear **Insurance alerts** in **Settings**.

<Callout type="warning" title="Use the right form">

Buildings of 1–3 stories generally use **OIR-B1-1802**; record whether it is the 2026 revision (effective April 1, 2026) or the pre-2026 form. For 4+ stories insured by Citizens, use **MIT-BT II** or **III**, which a home inspector cannot complete. Confirm the form with your insurer before ordering an inspection.

</Callout>
