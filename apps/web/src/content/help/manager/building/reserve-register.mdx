Open **Reserves** in the sidebar. Every owner and tenant can read the register; only management can change it.

<Figure shot="rv-list" alt="Reserve Register with component cards and years left">

Each component shows years left, **Due this year**, or **Past expected life**, based on its install year and expected life.

</Figure>

<StepByStep>

<Step title="Select Add Asset and enter the facts" shot="rv-add">

Enter the **Component name**, **Category**, **Year installed** and **Expected useful life (years)**. **Replacement cost**, **Currently reserved** and **Notes for members** are optional. Select **Add Asset**. When a component is replaced, select **Edit** on its card and update the install year.

</Step>

</StepByStep>

<Callout type="info" title="Not a reserve study">

The register is not a reserve study, a SIRS or a milestone inspection. Those are separate documents in **Documents**.

</Callout>
