## Overdue

Past the deadline, or a rolling 12-month record that has gone out of date. Do these first: select **Upload document** or **Upload current document** on the row, add the file and select **Upload & Link**. Uploading in Documents alone does not count; the record must be linked to the row.

## Action needed

Not yet linked, or a yearly record that is more than a year old. The **Action needed** chip narrows the list to rows due within 7 days, yearly records that need the current year's file, and rows marked **Needs board action**. If the record exists, upload it from the row now. If it is waiting on approval, such as minutes, put it on the next agenda and post it right after. Minutes uploaded as a file count for a meeting once you open the meeting in **Meetings** and select **Record minutes**.

## Not applicable

There is no control on the Compliance dashboard to mark a requirement not applicable. Records that are only needed in some cases, such as bids for association contracts or conflict-of-interest records, are listed in the **Records** section of **Website** (in the sidebar) once Florida’s website rule covers your association. Select **Doesn’t apply** on the record, choose a reason and select **Mark as not applying**. A row already marked **Not applicable** has **Mark applicable** to bring it back.

## The record does not exist

- If the community should have it, such as a current reserve study, hire the professional who prepares it.
- If it is lost for good, discuss it at the next board meeting and post those minutes.
- If you believe the requirement is wrong for your community, contact PropertyPro support.

<Callout type="tip" title="If the score does not change">

Check that the record is linked on the row, not just uploaded: the **Selected record** panel shows **Document on file**. Then check the date it was received or created: it was posted within 30 days of that date.

</Callout>

<Callout type="warning" title="Late records stay late">

A record posted more than 30 days after it was received or created stays **Overdue** for a year. Give each record type an owner and put known deadlines in your calendar 20 days ahead.

</Callout>
