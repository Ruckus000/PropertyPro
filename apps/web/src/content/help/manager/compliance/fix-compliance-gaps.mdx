## Overdue

Past the deadline, or a rolling 12-month record that has gone out of date. Do these first: select **Upload document** or **Upload current document** on the row, add the file and select **Upload & Link**. Uploading in Documents alone does not count; the record must be linked to the row.

## Action needed

Not yet linked. The **Action needed** chip narrows the list to rows due within 7 days or marked **Needs board action**. If the record exists, upload it from the row now. If it is waiting on approval, such as minutes, put it on the next agenda and post it right after.

## Not applicable

There is no control on the dashboard to mark a requirement not applicable. If one really does not apply to your community’s type or size, contact PropertyPro support. A row already marked **Not applicable** has **Mark applicable** to bring it back.

## The record does not exist

- If the community should have it, such as a current reserve study, hire the professional who prepares it.
- If it is lost for good, discuss it at the next board meeting and post those minutes.
- If you believe the requirement is wrong for your community, contact PropertyPro support.

## If the upload says Category mapping required

Some rows, such as contracts, bids, inspection reports and the SIRS, have no matching document category, so uploading from the row shows **Category mapping required**. Upload the file in **Documents** instead. If the row already has an older file and is overdue, select **Re-link or replace** and pick the new one; otherwise ask PropertyPro support to link it.

<Callout type="tip" title="If the score does not change">

Check that the record is linked on the row, not just uploaded: the **Selected record** panel shows **Document on file**. Then check that it was linked before the deadline.

</Callout>

<Callout type="warning" title="Late records stay late">

A record linked after its deadline stays **Overdue**. Give each record type an owner and put known deadlines in your calendar 20 days ahead.

</Callout>
