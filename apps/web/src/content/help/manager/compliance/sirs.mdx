<Step title="Upload the report under Inspection Reports">

In **Documents**, select **Upload Document** and choose **Inspection Reports**.

</Step>

<Step title="Link each document to its own row" shot="sirs-rows">

In **Compliance**, the inspection report goes on **Structural / Milestone Inspection Reports** and the reserve study on **Structural Integrity Reserve Study (SIRS)**. Do not swap them. If a row has an older report and is overdue, select **Re-link or replace** and pick the new document.

</Step>

</StepByStep>

<Callout type="info" title="Rows with nothing linked yet">

For these two rows, **Upload document** shows **Category mapping required**, because the requirement has no matching document category. Upload the report in Documents and ask PropertyPro support to link it.

</Callout>

## Milestone inspection timing

- Applies to condo buildings **three or more habitable stories**.
- First inspection by **December 31** of the year the building turns **30** (or **25** if the local agency requires it), then every **10 years**.
- **Phase 1** is a visual review. **Phase 2** (invasive testing) follows only if Phase 1 finds substantial deterioration.

<Callout type="florida-statute" title="Florida Statutes §553.899 and §718.112(2)(g)">

Milestone inspections and the SIRS are separate obligations with separate deadlines. Engineers book up early, so schedule well ahead.

</Callout>

<Callout type="warning" title="PropertyPro tracks records, not findings">

It shows whether each report is posted. Whether the building is sound or reserves are enough is for your engineer, reserve analyst and attorney.

</Callout>
