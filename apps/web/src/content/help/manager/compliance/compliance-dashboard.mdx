<Figure shot="cp-kpis" alt="Readiness, Posting windows, Overdue and Needs board action cards">

**Readiness** is your score. **Posting windows** counts records due within 7 days, **Overdue** is past the deadline, and **Needs board action** counts records, such as approved minutes, that are waiting on the board.

</Figure>

<StepByStep>

<Step title="Start with Action needed" shot="cp-chips">

The chips above the **Required records queue** filter the list: **Action needed**, **All**, **Overdue**, **Due ≤ 7 days** and **Satisfied**. **Action needed** shows everything overdue, due within 7 days, or waiting on the board.

</Step>

<Step title="Find the row" shot="cp-row">

Each row names the record, such as “Annual Budget” or “Current Insurance Policies”, with its status, deadline and statute. The **Selected record** panel shows whether a document is on file and its recent activity.

</Step>

<Step title="Upload and link the record" shot="cp-upload">

Select **Upload document** on the row. The category and title are filled in; add the file and select **Upload & Link**. The file goes into Documents and is linked to the row in one step.

</Step>

<Step title="Check the row" shot="cp-done">

It changes to **Satisfied** and Readiness goes up. A record linked after its deadline shows **Overdue** instead.

</Step>

</StepByStep>

If a row already has a record that is out of date, its button reads **Upload current document** or **Re-link or replace**. **Re-link or replace** opens **Link Document**, where you search for and pick a file already in Documents.

## Requirements that do not apply

There is no control on the dashboard to mark a requirement not applicable. A row already marked **Not applicable** is left out of the score and has a **Mark applicable** button. If you believe a requirement does not apply to your community, contact PropertyPro support.

## A weekly routine

- **Weekly:** clear everything under **Overdue**.
- **Monthly:** recheck every **Not applicable** row. A change in unit count can make a requirement apply again.
- **Quarterly:** capture the dashboard for the board packet. **Board view**, at the top right, shows how many records need board action.

<Callout type="florida-statute" title="Florida Statutes §718.111(12)(g) and §720.303">

Condos with **25+ units** and HOAs with **100+ parcels** must post specific records, generally within **30 days**. PropertyPro tracks timing and completeness; it does not give legal advice.

</Callout>
