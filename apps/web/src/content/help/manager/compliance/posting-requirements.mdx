- **Governing documents:** declaration, articles, bylaws, rules.
- **Financial:** annual budget and annual financial report.
- **Meetings:** approved minutes, and meeting notices and agendas, both over a rolling 12 months. Condos also post video recordings of virtual meetings and required affidavits.
- **Insurance:** current policies.
- **Contracts:** condos post the list of executory contracts and conflict-of-interest contracts; HOAs post current contracts.
- **Bids** received after bidding closes.
- **Condos only:** the question-and-answer sheet, inspection reports, and the SIRS.

These are requirements, not library categories. A requirement is satisfied only once a record is linked on its row in **Compliance**.

<OnlyFor types="condo_718">

<Callout type="florida-statute" title="Condo: Florida Statute §718.111(12)(g)">

Associations with **25+ units** must post specified records within **30 days** of creating or receiving them.

</Callout>

</OnlyFor>

<OnlyFor types="hoa_720">

<Callout type="florida-statute" title="HOA: Florida Statute §720.303">

Associations with **100+ parcels** must post specified records on a website or app, in an area only parcel owners and staff can reach.

</Callout>

</OnlyFor>

<Callout type="tip" title="When unit count changes">

PropertyPro does not change requirements when your unit or parcel count changes. If you cross 25 units (condo) or 100 parcels (HOA), check every row marked **Not applicable** and select **Mark applicable** where it now applies.

</Callout>
