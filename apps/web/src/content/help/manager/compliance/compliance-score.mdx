Readiness is the share of requirements that apply to you and are satisfied. Every requirement counts equally. The card shows it as a percentage and as “X of Y items satisfied”.

- **Satisfied** counts toward the score.
- **Action needed**, **Needs board action** and **Overdue** do not count. Overdue also appears in the **Overdue** card.
- **Not applicable** is left out entirely.

Readiness = Satisfied ÷ every requirement not marked Not applicable, rounded to a whole percent.

## When a linked record does not count

- It was linked after its deadline. The row stays **Overdue**.
- It is a rolling 12-month record, such as approved minutes or meeting notices and agendas, and the linked file was posted more than 12 months ago. Keep these current to avoid slow drift.

## What is not in the score

- The notice timing of each meeting. It is tracked on each meeting in **Meetings**.
- Financial health, reserve adequacy, and architectural review.

<Callout type="info" title="What 100% means">

A perfect score means every requirement that applies has a record linked on time and, for rolling records, within the last 12 months. It does not check what a record says. **Recent activity** on each record, and the activity feed below the list, show when records were linked.

</Callout>
