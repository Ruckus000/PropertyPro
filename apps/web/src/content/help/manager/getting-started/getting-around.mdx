<StepByStep>

<Step title="Use the sidebar" shot="gs-nav-m">

Everyone sees **Dashboard** and the **Community** group. As a manager you also see **Management**, with items such as **Payments** and **Website**, and **Admin**: **Compliance**, **Residents**, **Units**, **Contracts**, **E-Sign**, **Violations** and **Audit Trail**, depending on your community type and plan. Items marked **Pro** need a plan upgrade. On a phone, tap the menu icon at the top left.

</Step>

<Step title="Search your community" shot="gs-search">

The search bar at the top of every page finds pages, settings, documents, announcements, meetings, maintenance requests and residents. Press **⌘K** (Ctrl+K on Windows) to open it from anywhere.

</Step>

<Step title="Open help" shot="gs-help">

Select the **?** button at the top right, or press **?** on your keyboard. Help opens with guides for the page you are on.

</Step>

</StepByStep>

<Figure shot="gs-helppanel" alt="Help panel with articles relevant to the dashboard">

Under **Relevant to this page**, the help panel suggests articles for where you are. Its search covers all help articles and your community’s FAQs. Select **Visit Help Center** to see everything.

</Figure>

Your account menu is the circle with your initials at the top right. It has **Settings**, **Help Center**, **Data Export** and **Log out**.

<Callout type="florida-statute" title="Florida Statute §718.111(12)(g)">

Condominium associations with **25 or more units** must keep a website with specific records posted. See [Review the compliance dashboard](help:compliance-dashboard).

</Callout>
