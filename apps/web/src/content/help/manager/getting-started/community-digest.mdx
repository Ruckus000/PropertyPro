The digest is built from what is already in PropertyPro, so nobody has to write it. It skips any period with no new activity.

<StepByStep>

<Step title="Open Settings">

Select your initials at the top right, then **Settings**.

</Step>

<Step title="Turn on Send the digest to owners" shot="dig-board">

It is on the **Community digest** card, under **Notification Preferences**, and saves right away. Unit owners then get it weekly (on Mondays) and can switch to monthly, or turn it off, in their own settings. Tenants and staff do not receive it.

</Step>

</StepByStep>

<Callout type="info" title="Not an official notice">

Meeting notices, voting and other required notices are still sent separately. The digest does not replace them.

</Callout>
