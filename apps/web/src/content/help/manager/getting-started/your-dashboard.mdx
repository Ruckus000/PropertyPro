The dashboard is the first page after you sign in. It shows what is new and what needs you.

<OnlyFor types="condo_718 hoa_720">

<Figure shot="dash-m" alt="Dashboard with announcement, meeting and violation cards" />

<StepByStep>

<Step title="Recent Announcements" shot="dash-ann">

Up to five announcements, pinned ones first, then newest. Select **New announcement** to post one.

</Step>

<Step title="Upcoming Meetings" shot="dash-meet">

The next five meetings with type, date, time and place. Open **Meetings** for the agenda and notice.

</Step>

<Step title="Documents to Sign" shot="dash-sign">

Appears only when something is waiting for your own signature. Select a document to sign it.

</Step>

</StepByStep>

Where your community tracks violations, the **Violations** card shows open counts by status and the latest reports. Select **View All** to open the violations list.

</OnlyFor>

## Setup checklist

While a community is being set up, **Finish setting up your community** appears at the top, with a progress bar. Each item links to the page where you finish it. Select the **X** to hide it; when every item is done it shows a short congratulations you can dismiss.

<Figure shot="dash-check" alt="Setup checklist with a progress bar and setup steps" />

<OnlyFor types="apartment">

## Apartment communities

For apartment communities the dashboard opens on **Occupancy**, **Lease Expirations** (within 30 days, with 60- and 90-day counts), **Monthly Revenue** from active leases, and open **Maintenance** requests. Below them are **Recent Announcements** and **Quick Actions** such as **Post Announcement** and **New Maintenance Request**.

</OnlyFor>
