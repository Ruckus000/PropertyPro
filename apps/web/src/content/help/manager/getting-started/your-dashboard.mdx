The dashboard is the first page after you sign in. It tells you, in one sentence, whether you are on track, then lists what needs you.

<OnlyFor types="condo_718 hoa_720">

<Figure shot="dash-m" alt="Status card with a one-sentence summary and the next meeting" />

## The status line

The first card is a single sentence. It reads **Up to date**, **Needs attention** or **Late items**. Below it you see how many checklist documents are posted, for example "6 of 9 checklist documents are posted." When a meeting is coming up, **Next meeting** shows its date and time.

## What needs you

<StepByStep>

<Step title="Needs attention" shot="dash-attn">

Your to-do list, worst first. It collects late and upcoming compliance documents, meeting notices that still need posting, and violation hearing notices that still need sending. A small tag on each row says **Overdue**, **Due this week** or **Coming up**. Select the button on a row, such as **Post notice**, to go straight to the fix. The list shows six rows; select **View all** to see the rest in Compliance. When nothing is waiting you see **You're all set!**

</Step>

<Step title="This week" shot="dash-week">

The next seven days, starting today. It shows meetings, the dates by which notices must be posted, and hearings. Days with nothing on them say **Nothing scheduled**.

</Step>

</StepByStep>

## Quick actions

At the top right, next to **Welcome**, an **Upload document** button opens your documents. Under the cards above is a row of up to four buttons for common jobs: **New announcement**, **Upload document**, **Schedule meeting**, and, when you have signatures waiting, **Sign** with the number of documents. A button only appears if your plan includes that feature.

## The numbers

A row of counts links to the full lists. **Open violations** and **Open maintenance requests** appear only if your plan includes those features. **Upcoming meetings** counts meetings in the next 30 days. Select a count to open its page.

## Further down

<StepByStep start={3}>

<Step title="Documents to Sign" shot="dash-sign">

Appears only when something is waiting for your own signature. Select a document to sign it.

</Step>

<Step title="Recent Announcements" shot="dash-ann">

Up to five announcements, pinned ones first, then newest. Select **New announcement** to post one.

</Step>

</StepByStep>

**Recent Activity** at the bottom lists the latest compliance activity, with a link to the full history.

If the dashboard cannot load the to-do list or the week, it says so and asks you to refresh. It never shows an empty list in place of an error.

</OnlyFor>

## Setup checklist

While a community is being set up, **Finish setting up your community** appears at the top, with a progress bar. If your community website card is also showing, the checklist is titled **Strengthen your community** instead. Each item links to the page where you finish it. Select the **X** to hide it; when every item is done it shows a short congratulations you can dismiss.

## Apartment communities

For apartment communities the dashboard opens on **Occupancy**, **Lease Expirations** (within 30 days, with 60- and 90-day counts), **Monthly Revenue** from active leases, and open **Maintenance** requests. Below them are **Recent Announcements** and **Quick Actions** such as **Post Announcement** and **New Maintenance Request**.
