Help articles explain PropertyPro. FAQs answer questions about your community: quiet hours, trash pickup, who to call after hours. Residents see them in help search, next to help articles.

<StepByStep>

<Step title="Select Manage your community’s FAQs" shot="faq-link">

It is at the bottom of the help center, next to **Contact support**. Only managers see it.

</Step>

<Step title="Select a question, or use Add FAQ" shot="faq-list">

The list on the left shows every FAQ for this community. Select one to edit it, or fill in the **Add FAQ** form on the right to write a new one.

</Step>

<Step title="Write the question and answer" shot="faq-edit">

Phrase the **Question** the way a resident asks it: “When does the pool open?” is better than “Pool hours”. Keep the **Answer** to 2–4 sentences, and point to a document for anything longer.

</Step>

<Step title="Select Save changes" shot="faq-save">

For a new FAQ the button is **Add FAQ**. Residents can find it in search right away. **Delete FAQ** removes it immediately. Adding, editing and deleting are all recorded in the audit trail.

</Step>

</StepByStep>

<Callout type="warning" title="Review FAQs each quarter">

An FAQ that says “the pool opens May 1” in October does more harm than no FAQ. FAQs cannot be reordered here, so clear wording is what helps residents find them.

</Callout>
