Tenants cannot open documents in **Inspection Reports** until you turn this on. That includes milestone inspections and the structural integrity reserve study (SIRS). Owners and managers can always open them.

<StepByStep>

<Step title="Open Tenant access" shot="tia-card">

Select your initials at the top right, then **Settings**. Scroll to **Tenant access**.

</Step>

<Step title="Turn on Tenants can view inspection reports">

The change saves straight away and is recorded in the audit log. Turn it off again at any time.

</Step>

</StepByStep>

While it is off, tenants do not see the category, cannot find its documents in search, and are not notified when one is posted.

<Callout type="florida-statute" title="Inspection reports are owners' records">

Florida law gives the right to inspect the association's official records, which include these reports, to unit owners and their authorized representatives (§718.111(12)(g)). It does not require sharing them with tenants. Turn this on only if your association has decided to.

</Callout>
