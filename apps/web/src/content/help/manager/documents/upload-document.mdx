<StepByStep>

<Step title="Select Upload Document" shot="up-btn">

It is at the top right of the **Documents** page. The upload panel opens below it.

</Step>

<Step title="Add your files">

Drag files onto the panel, or select **choose files**. You can add several at once. PDF and Word (.docx) files can be up to 50 MB, and PNG or JPG images up to 10 MB. PDF is best: a photo of a paper page is hard to read on a phone. Files that can’t be posted, such as spreadsheets, are marked with the reason.

</Step>

<Step title="Check each file">

Each file has a **Title**, filled in from its file name, and a **Category**. The category organizes the library and decides who can see the file. If you opened the panel from a category, it is already chosen. Select **Add a description** to add one. “Annual budget 2026” is easier to find than “budget_final_v3”.

</Step>

<Step title="Post now, or save as a draft">

**Post 2 now** (the number counts your files) puts them in the library for residents straight away. **Save as draft** keeps them for managers only, until you open one and select **Post now**.

</Step>

</StepByStep>

<Callout type="info" title="Confirm redaction">

For categories that often hold personal information, such as **Financial Records**, **Meeting Records** and **Elections**, posting asks you to tick **Confirm redaction before uploading**. Check that the files have no social-security numbers, personal contact details, or medical or personnel information first. A draft doesn’t ask, because residents can’t see it yet.

</Callout>

<OnlyFor types="condo_718 hoa_720">

## Which category?

</OnlyFor>

<OnlyFor types="condo_718 hoa_720">

- Minutes, agendas and notices → **Meeting Records**
- Budget, financial report, reserve study, audit → **Financial Records**
- Declaration, articles, bylaws, rules → **Governing Documents**
- Vendor and management contracts → **Contracts**
- Certificates of insurance → **Insurance**
- Candidate sheets, ballots, results → **Elections**
- SIRS and milestone inspection reports → **Inspection Reports**
- Owner letters and official notices → **Correspondence**

</OnlyFor>

<OnlyFor types="apartment">

Apartment categories are Lease Agreements, Maintenance Records, Communications, Financials, Compliance, Rules, Community Handbook and Move In/Out Docs.

</OnlyFor>

## Replace a document

Open the document and select **Replace file**. The title, category and link stay the same. If you upload a file with the same name as one already in the library, the panel asks whether to **Replace the old one** or **Keep both**.

## Take a document off the site

Open it and select **Take off the site**. It becomes a draft again: residents can no longer see it.

<OnlyFor types="condo_718 hoa_720">

<Callout type="florida-statute" title="Posting deadlines">

Most records must be posted within **30 days** of being created or received. A file uploaded here does not satisfy a compliance requirement on its own. To upload and link in one step, open **Compliance** and select **Upload document** on the requirement’s row. See [Fix compliance gaps](help:fix-compliance-gaps).

</Callout>

</OnlyFor>
