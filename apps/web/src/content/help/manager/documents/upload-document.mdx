<StepByStep>

<Step title="Select Upload Document" shot="up-btn">

It is at the top right of the **Documents** page. The upload panel opens below it.

</Step>

<Step title="Choose a category" shot="up-cat">

Required: **Upload Document** stays unavailable until you choose one. The category organizes the library and decides who can see the file. If you had a category selected in the list, it is already chosen.

</Step>

<Step title="Add the file" shot="up-file">

Drag it onto the box or select **browse**. PDF, DOCX, PNG and JPG are accepted. PDF is best. Documents can be up to 50 MB, images up to 10 MB.

</Step>

<Step title="Check the title and add a description">

The **Title** and **Description (optional)** boxes appear once a file is added, with the title filled in from the file name. Residents see the title in the list, and search uses both. “Annual budget 2026” is easier to find than “budget_final_v3”.

</Step>

<Step title="Select Upload Document" shot="up-save">

The document is in the library right away.

</Step>

</StepByStep>

<Callout type="info" title="Confirm redaction">

For categories that often hold personal information, such as **Financial Records**, **Meeting Records** and **Elections**, a **Confirm redaction before uploading** box appears. Check that the file has no social-security numbers, personal contact details, or medical or personnel information, then tick the box to upload.

</Callout>

<OnlyFor types="condo_718 hoa_720">

## Which category?

</OnlyFor>

<OnlyFor types="condo_718 hoa_720">

- Minutes, agendas and notices → **Meeting Records**
- Budget, financial report, reserve study, audit → **Financial Records**
- Declaration, articles, bylaws, rules → **Governing Documents**
- Vendor and management contracts → **Contracts**
- Certificates of insurance → **Insurance**
- Candidate sheets, ballots, results → **Elections**
- SIRS and milestone inspection reports → **Inspection Reports**
- Owner letters and official notices → **Correspondence**

</OnlyFor>

<OnlyFor types="apartment">

Apartment categories are Lease Agreements, Maintenance Records, Communications, Financials, Compliance, Rules, Community Handbook and Move In/Out Docs.

</OnlyFor>

## Replace a document

Open the old document, select **Delete**, and upload the new one with the same title. **Version History** only tracks versions of documents written with **Author Document**.

<OnlyFor types="condo_718 hoa_720">

<Callout type="florida-statute" title="Posting deadlines">

Most records must be posted within **30 days** of being created or received. A file uploaded here does not satisfy a compliance requirement on its own. To upload and link in one step, open **Compliance** and select **Upload document** on the requirement’s row. See [Fix compliance gaps](help:fix-compliance-gaps).

</Callout>

</OnlyFor>
