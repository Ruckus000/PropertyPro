People who already have a PropertyPro account can ask to join your community with **Join another community**. Their requests appear on the **Join requests** tab of **Directory**, and the Directory item in the sidebar shows how many are waiting.

<StepByStep>

<Step title="Check the unit" shot="jr-queue">

Each request shows the unit they claimed, their name and email. **On your roster** means that unit number is in your Directory, and approving links them to it. **Not on your roster** means it is not: approving gives them access without a unit, and you can assign one in Directory afterwards. Either way, confirm the person against ownership or lease records.

</Step>

<Step title="Check the role">

Each request is marked **owner** or **tenant**. Owners should be on your ownership list; tenants should have a lease or be named by the owner.

</Step>

<Step title="Select Approve, or Deny with a reason" shot="jr-deny">

**Approve** gives them portal access straight away. **Deny** opens a **Reason for denying** box; the reason is sent to the person, then select **Confirm deny**. A reason like “The unit number does not match our records; please check your closing papers and resubmit” helps them fix it. They are notified in PropertyPro either way, and a denied person must wait 30 days before asking again.

</Step>

</StepByStep>

<Callout type="warning" title="The role cannot be changed later">

Approval grants the role they chose, and the Directory has no way to switch between owner and tenant. If a tenant picked owner, deny and ask them to request again as a tenant.

</Callout>
