<StepByStep>

<Step title="Select Add resident" shot="rs-add">

Open **Directory** from the sidebar and select the **Residents** tab. Before anyone is added, **Add resident** is in the middle of the tab; after that it is at the top right. You can also select a unit on the **Units** tab and choose **Add resident** in its panel, which starts the form on that unit.

</Step>

<Step title="Enter their details" shot="rs-form">

**Full name**, **Email** and **Unit** are required; **Phone** is optional. <OnlyFor types="condo_718 hoa_720">Choose **Owner** or **Tenant** as the **Role**.</OnlyFor><OnlyFor types="apartment">Residents are added as **Tenant**.</OnlyFor> Pick the **Unit** from the list of your units by its number. If the list is empty, add your units on the **Units** tab first. Leave **Send invitation email** checked to send a sign-up link, then select **Save resident**.

</Step>

<Step title="Resend if needed" shot="rs-resend">

Select the resident to open their details, then select **Send invite** (or **Resend invite** if they were invited and have not signed in yet).

</Step>

</StepByStep>

## Import from a spreadsheet

On the **Residents** tab, select **Import CSV** (before anyone is added) or **More actions** (**⋯**) then **Import from CSV**. Select **Download CSV template** (columns name, email, role, unit_number), fill it in with **owner** or **tenant** as the role, and upload it. Every row needs a unit_number that matches one of your units. You see a preview with any row errors before you select the **Import … Residents** button. Import does not send invitations; use **Send invite** afterward.

## Board and manager access

Only the community’s root manager can change access, in **Settings → Roles & Access**. It is where property managers are added or removed<OnlyFor types="condo_718 hoa_720"> and board designations are set</OnlyFor>.

## Removing a resident

Select the resident to open their details, then select **Remove from community** and confirm with **Remove resident**. You can also select **Remove** next to their name in a unit’s panel. They lose portal access to this community. Their PropertyPro account stays, so any other community they belong to is unaffected.<OnlyFor types="condo_718 hoa_720"> If they were the unit’s only owner, the unit is left with no owner on file.</OnlyFor>
