Roles are not set when you add a resident. Add the person as an owner or tenant first, then set their role in **Roles & Access**: open search, type **Roles**, and choose **Roles & Permissions**. Only the community’s root manager can open it.

<StepByStep>

<Step title="Assign a board seat" shot="ra-board">

In **Board**, find the person and select **President** or **Member**. Choosing **President** asks you to confirm, because it replaces any current president. If they are not an owner, you are asked to confirm they are eligible under your bylaws. Select **Clear** to remove the seat.

</Step>

<Step title="Grant manager access" shot="ra-pm">

In **Members**, select **Promote to property manager**. To remove it, select **Revoke** under **Property managers** and confirm. On Essentials, a plan limit caps the number of administrators.

</Step>

</StepByStep>

A board seat gives a resident election administration (from **Board → Elections**) and community export. Violations stay with managers. It does not give them management access. Property managers manage the community day to day but cannot change billing or roles; those stay with the root manager. To hand the root role to a property manager, use **Transfer root** under **Root manager**.

<OnlyFor types="apartment">

Apartment communities have no board, so the **Board** section is not shown.

</OnlyFor>

## When someone leaves

- Review their recent actions in the [audit trail](help:audit-trail), and hand off open work.
- Clear the board seat or revoke manager access. A revoked property manager goes back to being a resident.
- After an election, add the new board before removing the outgoing one.
- Review every admin each quarter. It takes about 30 minutes for 10 communities.
