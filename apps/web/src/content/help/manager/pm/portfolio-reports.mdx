Open **Reports** in the portfolio sidebar.

<StepByStep>

<Step title="Pick a report and filters" shot="rp-tabs">

Choose **Maintenance**, **Compliance**, **Occupancy**, **Violations** or **Delinquency**. Pick the communities (**All Communities** by default) and a date range, then select **Apply**. Compliance and Delinquency always show today’s position, whatever the dates.

</Step>

<Step title="Select Export CSV" shot="rp-export">

It is on the table below the chart. There is no PDF export. The community and date filters are saved in the page address, so a bookmark keeps them.

</Step>

</StepByStep>

<Callout type="tip" title="A routine">

Maintenance weekly, compliance and delinquency monthly, everything quarterly. Remove resident names before sharing outside the company.

</Callout>
