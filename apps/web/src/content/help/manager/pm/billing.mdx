In the community, open **Settings** and select **Billing & Subscription**. Everyone in management sees the plan and its status; only the community’s root manager can change anything.

<Figure shot="bl-page" alt="Billing page with the current plan, its status and the Change plan button" />

- **Essentials ($199/mo, condo and HOA):** compliance, meetings, announcements, the public website and transparency page, insurance, reserves and storm tools. Up to 3 administrators.
- **Professional ($349/mo, condo and HOA):** adds e-sign, violations, ARC, maintenance and work orders, finance, voting and polls, amenities, packages and visitors, and a custom domain. Unlimited administrators.
- **Operations Plus ($499/mo, apartments):** leases, packages, visitors, maintenance and work orders, finance, e-sign, and portfolio website templates. No condo/HOA compliance features.

<StepByStep>

<Step title="Select Change plan" shot="bl-plans">

Choose **Monthly** or **Annual**, pick the plan, and select **Review change**, then **Confirm change**. Stripe charges a prorated amount today and bills the new rate from then on. You are asked for your password. Only upgrades and billing-period changes are offered here.

</Step>

<Step title="Use Manage Subscription for everything else">

It, and **View Invoices**, **Update Payment Method** and **Cancel Subscription** under **Billing Actions**, open the Stripe billing portal after you re-enter your password. Cancellation has a 30-day grace period.

</Step>

</StepByStep>

<Callout type="warning" title="Before you cancel">

Make sure the new manager has access first. A failed payment shows **Past Due** and a **Payment failed** banner; select **Update Payment Method** to fix it.

</Callout>
