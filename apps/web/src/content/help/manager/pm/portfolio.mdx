**Communities** lays your portfolio out at a size that fits it. One or two communities each get a large card. Up to eight show as site cards. With nine or more you get a list grouped by status, and from 25 a table you can sort by any column. Switch between cards and list with the two buttons at the top right; your choice is remembered on this device.

Each community shows one status, worked out from what PropertyPro measures:

- **Needs attention**: a required document is past its posting deadline, or dues are overdue.
- **On track**: something is measured and nothing is wrong.
- **Not tracked**: nothing is measured. Apartments have no posting rules, so they are always here.

Open maintenance requests are shown as a number, not counted toward the status. **Unpublished edits** marks a community whose website has changes nobody has published yet.

<Figure shot="pm-table" alt="Communities in list view, grouped by status">

Use **All**, **Needs attention** and **Has overdue dues** to narrow the list, and **Type** to show one kind of community. With nine or more communities there is also **Search**; press the / key to jump to it.

</Figure>

<StepByStep>

<Step title="Open a community" shot="pm-row">

Select its card, its row, or its name. You work in that community with its management access, and its pages show only its data. The **Website** button on a large card opens that community's website.

</Step>

<Step title="Select Communities to return" shot="pm-back">

It is the first link in the breadcrumb at the top of every community page, and **PropertyPro** at the top left goes there from Reports and Templates. **Reports** and **Templates** are in the toolbar on Communities (on a phone, under **More portfolio tools**, ⋯).

</Step>

</StepByStep>

<Callout type="tip" title="Respect board boundaries">

You have management access in every community in your portfolio. Boards expect you to leave governance decisions to them.

</Callout>
