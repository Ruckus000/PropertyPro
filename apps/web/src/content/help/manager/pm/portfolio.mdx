**Communities** opens as cards. Select **List view** at the top right to see a table instead, and use **Type** and **Search** to narrow it.

<Figure shot="pm-table" alt="Portfolio table in list view">

Each row shows the type, units, residents, occupancy, open maintenance, compliance, outstanding balance and site status. Sort any column to find who needs attention.

</Figure>

<StepByStep>

<Step title="Open a community" shot="pm-row">

Select its card, or in list view open the row’s actions menu and select **View Dashboard**. You work in that community with its management access, and its pages show only its data.

</Step>

<Step title="Select Communities to return" shot="pm-back">

It is the first link in the breadcrumb at the top of every community page. On the portfolio, the sidebar has **Communities**, **Website**, **Templates** and **Reports**.

</Step>

</StepByStep>

<Callout type="tip" title="Respect board boundaries">

You have management access in every community in your portfolio. Boards expect you to leave governance decisions to them.

</Callout>
