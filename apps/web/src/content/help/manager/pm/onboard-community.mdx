## Day 1: create it

<StepByStep>

<Step title="Select Add Community" shot="ob-add">

It is at the top right of **Communities**. It is unavailable until your portfolio billing is set up.

</Step>

<Step title="Enter the basics" shot="ob-form">

Enter the **Community name**, **Community type** (condo, HOA or apartment), **Subdomain**, address and **Unit count**.

</Step>

<Step title="Choose the plan and pay" shot="ob-plan">

Pick the **Plan**: **Essentials** or **Professional** for a condo or HOA, **Operations Plus** for an apartment community. Any portfolio volume discount is shown below. Select **Continue to Payment** and complete the Stripe checkout in the same window. **Community added** confirms it is set up and its subscription is active.

</Step>

</StepByStep>

## The first month

- **Days 1–3:** [upload governing documents](help:upload-document), [add units](help:units), add the board and [assign their roles](help:roles-access), and [set the branding](help:website-branding).
- **Days 4–7:** [invite residents](help:invite-residents), owners first; [post a welcome announcement](help:post-announcement); train the local admin for an hour.
- **Week 2:** clear old gaps on the [compliance dashboard](help:compliance-dashboard), schedule the next board meeting, turn on the [transparency page](help:transparency-page) and [online payments](help:online-payments).
- **By day 30:** required records posted, dues billing, requests flowing through Operations.

<Callout type="warning" title="Upload documents on day 1">

Posting deadlines keep running while you set up. Post what you have right away and add the rest as it arrives.

</Callout>
