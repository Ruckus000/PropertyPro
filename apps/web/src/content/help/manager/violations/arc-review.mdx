<StepByStep>

<Step title="Open ARC Requests" shot="arcr-queue">

It is in the sidebar under **Admin**. The table lists title, project type, unit, status and submitted date. Use the tabs to show **Submitted**, **Under Review**, **Approved** or **Denied** requests.

</Step>

<Step title="Select Review on a request" shot="arcr-panel">

A panel opens with the status, description, dates and any notes. Select **Mark under review** if the committee needs time; the resident sees the status change on their request.

</Step>

<Step title="Write notes and decide" shot="arcr-decide">

Under **Record a decision**, select **Approve** or **Deny**. To deny, **Review notes** and **Rule or covenant cited** are both required. The notes are sent to the resident, who is notified of the decision, and the decision is recorded with who made it.

</Step>

</StepByStep>

<Callout type="florida-statute" title="Florida HB 1203">

A denial must state specific reasons. “Does not meet guidelines” is not enough; cite the section, such as “Architectural Guidelines §5.2 limits fences to 6 feet”, and suggest what would be approved.

</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="Approving with conditions">

Put the conditions (colors, materials, deadlines, an inspection) in **Review notes** and approve. The resident sees them on their request.

</Callout>

Review deadlines and extensions are not tracked in PropertyPro. Follow your bylaws.
