<StepByStep>

<Step title="Open Violations" shot="vi-inbox">

It is in the sidebar under **Admin**. Filter by status, severity or a date range. Statuses are **Reported**, **Noticed**, **Hearing Scheduled**, **Fined**, **Resolved** and **Dismissed**.

</Step>

<Step title="Select New violation" shot="vi-new">

Pick the **Resident’s Unit**, the **Category** and **Severity**, and describe what happened, when and where. **Cite the rule** in the description, and add up to 3 photos. Select **File Violation Report**. It is saved as **Reported**; the resident is not told yet.

</Step>

<Step title="Open it and select Send Notice" shot="vi-notice">

Select the violation in the list to open it, then **Send Notice**. Add optional notes and select **Send Violation Notice**. The status changes to **Noticed** and the notice date is recorded. PropertyPro emails and notifies every owner and tenant on the unit’s record. Still deliver the formal written notice your governing documents require.

</Step>

</StepByStep>

## Next steps

The actions offered depend on the status.

- **Noticed:** select **Schedule Hearing**, choose the **Hearing Date** and, optionally, the **Hearing Location**, which shows on the violation and the hearing notice. Or select **Impose Fine**.
- **Hearing Scheduled:** select **Impose Fine**, **Resolve** or **Dismiss**.
- **Fined:** select **Resolve** once it is fixed.
- **Resolve** needs resolution notes and **Dismiss** needs a dismissal reason. Both are final.
- **Fine:** enter the **Fine Amount ($)** and **Due Date**, tick at least three owners on the **Fining committee** who approved it, and confirm **The fining committee approved this fine**. If the community has fewer than three eligible owners, a warning appears and you must select **I accept this disclaimer** to continue; who accepted it and when is recorded in the audit log. It posts to the unit’s balance right away.

<Figure shot="vi-fine" alt="Impose Fine form with amount, due date and the fining committee">

**Impose Fine** only appears when fines are turned on for your community. Fines are off by default.

</Figure>

<Figure shot="vi-hear" alt="Hearing date inside 14 days, with a warning">

If the hearing is less than **14 days** away, a warning appears. You can still save it, but check your governing documents first.

</Figure>

<Callout type="florida-statute" title="Florida HB 1203 (2024)">

Every notice must state the specific rule and reason. PropertyPro does not check this; it is your responsibility. A fine must be approved by a committee of owners who are not officers, directors or employees (§718.303(3), §720.305(2)). The statute asks for at least three members. The committee list only offers owners without a board seat, and never you. PropertyPro also refuses a fine above $100, or one that takes the violation’s total over $1,000, unless your governing documents set other limits.

</Callout>
