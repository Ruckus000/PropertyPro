<StepByStep>

<Step title="Open E-Sign, then Templates, and select New Template" shot="tpl-btn">

**E-Sign** is in the sidebar under **Admin**. The template builder has four steps: Document, Signer roles, Place fields and Review.

</Step>

<Step title="Add the PDF">

Choose **Upload a PDF** (up to 50 MB) or **Use a document you have** to pick one from Documents. Merge any appendices into it first. Select **Next**.

</Step>

<Step title="Name it and add signer roles">

Enter a **Name**, for example “Annual insurance disclosure 2026”, a **Type** and an optional **Description**. Under **Signer roles**, enter a **Role** for each person who signs, such as owner, tenant or board president. Select **Add a role** for more. When you send, each role is matched to a person.

</Step>

<Step title="Place the fields" shot="tpl-build">

Choose a **Signer Role**, then a **Field Type**: **Signature**, **Initials**, **Date**, **Text** or **Checkbox**. Click the page where it goes. Make fields large enough to sign on a phone.

</Step>

<Step title="Review and select Save template">

Then send it to yourself first to check that every field is where you expect.

</Step>

</StepByStep>

## Changing a template

Open the template from the **Templates** tab. **Edit Fields** changes its fields; requests already sent keep the version they went out with. **Clone** makes a copy under a new name, and **Archive** retires the template.
