<StepByStep>

<Step title="Select Send Document" shot="snd-btn">

Open **E-Sign** in the sidebar. **Send Document** is at the top right. To start from a template, open it on the **Templates** tab and select **Send for Signing** instead; its PDF and fields are filled in for you.

</Step>

<Step title="Choose the document">

Choose **Upload a PDF** or **Use a document you have**, then select **Next**. Skip this when you start from a template.

</Step>

<Step title="Add the recipients and signing order" shot="snd-form">

Enter each recipient’s **Name**, **Email** and **Role**, including people outside the community. Select **Add a recipient** for more. Under **Order**, **Everyone at once** lets everyone sign in any order. **One after another, in order** makes later signers wait; they still get their email right away.

</Step>

<Step title="Add a message and expiry" shot="snd-exp">

The **Email subject** and **Message** go in the email. **Expires after** is 7, 14 or 30 days (14 by default), or **Does not expire**. Clear **Email each recipient their link** if you would rather copy the links yourself.

</Step>

<Step title="Place the fields, review, and select Send for signing">

On **Place fields**, give every recipient at least one field. On **Review**, check the details, then select **Send for signing**. Each recipient gets a personal link.

</Step>

</StepByStep>

## Track and follow up

Open a request from the **Requests** tab. Each signer shows **Pending**, **Opened**, **Completed** or **Declined**. Select **Send reminder** to email a signer again (up to 3 per signer), or **Copy signing link** to share it yourself. **Waiting on** lists everyone who still has to sign. When everyone has signed, select **Download Signed Document**.

<Figure shot="snd-track" alt="Request with its signers and their status" />

<Callout type="warning" title="Cancelling">

**Cancel Request** voids every pending signature and cannot be undone; signers are not told. There is no bulk send yet, so send one request per owner for community-wide forms.

</Callout>
