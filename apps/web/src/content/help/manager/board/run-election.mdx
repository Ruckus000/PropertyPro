<Callout type="warning" title="Elections must be turned on first">

Elections stay off until your attorney has reviewed e-voting and PropertyPro support enables them for your community. Until then **Board** has no **Elections** tab, and the elections page says attorney review is required. Elections and candidates are set up with PropertyPro support; you cannot create them in the app.

</Callout>

<StepByStep>

<Step title="Send the first notice (60 days before)">

Send it outside the election tools, for example with an [announcement](help:post-announcement). Candidates must give written notice of intent **40 days** before.

</Step>

<Step title="Take the eligibility snapshot">

In **Board → Elections**, select the draft election to expand it and select **Snapshot Eligibility** on or just before the second-notice date. It records which units may vote. Opening the election also takes a snapshot.

</Step>

<Step title="Send the second notice and ballot (14–34 days before)">

Include every candidate, any candidate information sheets, and instructions for electronic and paper voting.

</Step>

<Step title="Open and close the election" shot="el-actions">

Select **View Details** on the election. Under **Admin actions**, select **Open Election** and **Confirm**. Voting stops at the close time, but you still select **Close Election** to finish it. **Cancel Election** asks for a reason and cannot be undone.

</Step>

<Step title="Check quorum and select Certify Results" shot="el-results">

E-ballots are counted for you. Paper ballots are counted outside PropertyPro. The results show ballots cast against the quorum needed. PropertyPro lets you certify even when quorum is not met, so check it first: without quorum the election fails and must be noticed again. When certifying you can enter a results document ID.

</Step>

</StepByStep>

Record the results in the meeting minutes yourself. PropertyPro does not post them.

Residents with a board seat can run these same steps, and approve or reject proxies, from their own account. Only the owner who gave a proxy, or a manager, can revoke it.

<OnlyFor types="condo_718">

<Callout type="florida-statute" title="Condo: Florida Statutes §718.112(2)(d) and §718.128">

First notice at least **60 days** before; candidate intent at least **40 days** before; information sheets **35 days** before; second notice and ballot **14–34 days** before. Owners must opt in to e-voting, and paper ballots must stay available.

</Callout>

</OnlyFor>

<OnlyFor types="hoa_720">

<Callout type="florida-statute" title="HOA: Florida Statute §720.306(9)">

HOA elections follow §720.306 and your governing documents, not the condo two-notice schedule. Confirm the rules with your attorney.

</Callout>

</OnlyFor>

<Callout type="tip" title="If results are challenged">

Keep paper ballots with the official records and contact your attorney. Do not recount or change results outside your bylaws.

</Callout>
