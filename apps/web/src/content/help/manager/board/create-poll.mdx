<StepByStep>

<Step title="Open Board, then Polls, and select Create Poll" shot="pc-btn">
</Step>

<Step title="Write the question and options" shot="pc-form">

Put the question in **Title** and any background in **Description**. Add 2–20 options with **Add Option**.

</Step>

<Step title="Choose the poll type and end date" shot="pc-type">

**Single Choice** or **Multiple Choice**. A poll cannot be edited or closed early once it is created, so check it first. A week or less gets the most answers. Without an **End date** the poll stays open.

</Step>

<Step title="Select Create Poll, then announce it">

Creating a poll does not notify anyone. [Post an announcement](help:post-announcement) that explains the question and tells residents where to find it.

</Step>

</StepByStep>

<Callout type="warning" title="Not for binding decisions">

Board elections, amendments to governing documents, and anything else the law requires a vote on need a formal election, not a poll.

</Callout>
