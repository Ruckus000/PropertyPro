<StepByStep>

<Step title="Review requests" shot="wo-inbox">

Open **Operations** in the sidebar. The tabs are **All**, **Requests**, **Work Orders** and **Reservations**. **Requests** lists every resident request with its title, description and status.

</Step>

<Step title="Select Dispatch Work Order" shot="wo-btn">

It is at the top right on the **All** and **Work Orders** tabs. Work orders are separate from requests, so copy the details you need from the request.

</Step>

<Step title="Enter the work" shot="wo-form">

Enter a **Title**, a **Description** and the **Priority** (low, medium, high or urgent). If the work is in a unit, enter its **Unit ID**.

</Step>

<Step title="Choose a vendor and targets" shot="wo-vendor">

Pick a **Vendor**, or leave it on **(Assign later)**. Optionally set **SLA Response (hrs)** and **SLA Completion (hrs)**, and add internal **Notes**.

</Step>

<Step title="Select Dispatch Work Order" shot="wo-send">

The work order appears on the **Work Orders** tab.

</Step>

</StepByStep>

<Callout type="info" title="Vendors are contacted outside PropertyPro">

Vendors do not get email from PropertyPro and have no login. Call or email them as usual. Work orders cannot be marked complete in the app yet, and request statuses cannot be changed from **Operations**, so track progress in your own records.

</Callout>
