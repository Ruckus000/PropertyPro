Post minutes after the board approves them, usually at the next meeting. There are two ways to do it.

## Write them in PropertyPro

<StepByStep>

<Step title="Open the meeting and select Author Minutes" shot="min-author">

It is at the bottom of the meeting’s details. In the **Minutes** view, past meetings without minutes also have **Author minutes**. The editor opens with the title filled in. Write the minutes and select **Publish**.

</Step>

</StepByStep>

Published minutes are attached to the meeting, the meeting shows **Posted** in the **Minutes** view, and edits keep a version history.

<OnlyFor types="condo_718 hoa_720">

Publishing also links the minutes to the rolling 12-month minutes requirement in **Compliance**.

</OnlyFor>

## Or upload the approved file

<StepByStep start={2}>

<Step title="Upload it under Meeting Records" shot="min-upload">

In **Documents**, select **Upload Document** and choose **Meeting Records**. See [Upload a document](help:upload-document).

</Step>

</StepByStep>

An uploaded file is not attached to the meeting, and the **Minutes** view still counts the meeting as owed.

<OnlyFor types="condo_718 hoa_720">

To count it toward compliance, upload it from **Compliance** instead, on the **Approved Meeting Minutes (Rolling 12 Months)** row, which uploads and links in one step.

</OnlyFor>

<Figure shot="min-list" alt="Minutes view showing which meetings have minutes posted">

The **Minutes** view shows which past meetings still need minutes and their post-by date.

</Figure>

## What to include

- Type, date, time and place, who attended, and whether there was a quorum.
- Every motion, who made and seconded it, and the result.
- A short summary of discussion, not a transcript. Leave out medical, HR and attorney-client matters.

<OnlyFor types="condo_718">

<Callout type="florida-statute" title="Florida Statute §718.111(12)(g)">

Approved minutes must be posted within **30 days** of the association having them. **Target post by** counts 30 days from the meeting as a safe reminder. Video recordings of meetings are a separate record that must be kept for **12 months**.

</Callout>

</OnlyFor>

<Callout type="tip" title="Correcting posted minutes">

Approve the correction at the next meeting. For minutes written in PropertyPro, open them in **Documents** and select **Edit** to publish a new version; **Version History** keeps the earlier one. For an uploaded file, upload the corrected file and delete the old one.

</Callout>
