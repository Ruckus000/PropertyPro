<StepByStep>

<Step title="Open Meetings and pick a view" shot="mt-views">

**Calendar** shows the month. **Schedule** lists **Upcoming** and **Past** meetings with their notice window and status. **Minutes** lists past meetings and whether their minutes are posted; its tab shows how many are owed.

</Step>

<Step title="Select a day" shot="mt-day">

The meetings on that day are listed below the calendar, with type, time, place and how many documents are attached.

</Step>

<Step title="Open the meeting" shot="mt-detail">

Select **Details**, or select the meeting’s title in **Schedule**. You see the time, location, its **Compliance deadlines** and any **Attached documents**, such as the agenda. Select a document to open it. **Edit**, **Author Minutes** and **Delete** are at the bottom.

</Step>

</StepByStep>

## Past meetings

Select **Schedule**, then **Past**. A meeting shows **Posted** in the **Minutes** view once its minutes are published with **Author Minutes**. See [Post approved minutes](help:post-minutes).

<Figure shot="mt-past" alt="Schedule view with the Past list selected" />

Meeting notice emails follow each member’s [notification settings](help:notifications).

<OnlyFor types="condo_718 hoa_720">

<Callout type="florida-statute" title="Notice periods">

Florida law sets minimum notice before meetings: generally **14 days** for owner meetings and **48 hours** for board meetings. The **Schedule** view shows when each notice was posted, or the date it must be posted by.

</Callout>

</OnlyFor>
