<OnlyFor types="condo_718 hoa_720">

<Callout type="info" title="Meeting notices go in Meetings">

Board and owner meeting notices have legal timing rules. [Schedule the meeting](help:schedule-meeting) instead of announcing it.

</Callout>

</OnlyFor>

<StepByStep>

<Step title="Select New announcement" shot="an-new">

It is at the top right of **Announcements**.

</Step>

<Step title="Write the title and message" shot="an-compose">

Fill in **Title** and **Message**. Be specific: “Storm prep: three things to do this weekend” works better than “Important announcement”. To share a file, link to it in Documents.

</Step>

<Step title="Choose the audience" shot="an-aud">

In **Audience**, choose **All residents**, **Owners only**, **Board members only** or **Tenants only**. When unsure, send to everyone. **Board members only** is emailed to members with a board seat, but does not show on their **Announcements** page.

</Step>

<Step title="Pin it if it is urgent" shot="an-pinbox">

Check **Pin announcement** to keep it at the top of the list until you unpin it. To take it down automatically, set **Stop showing on (optional)**.

</Step>

<Step title="Select Publish announcement" shot="an-publish">

It appears on dashboards and the **Announcements** page right away, and is emailed based on each person’s settings. There is no preview, so proofread first.

</Step>

</StepByStep>

<Callout type="warning" title="Edits do not recall email">

Editing a published announcement with **Edit** does not change the email already sent. For a real mistake, publish a short correction. To remove one, select **Delete** on it and confirm; you can bring it back with **Show deleted**, then **Restore**.

</Callout>
