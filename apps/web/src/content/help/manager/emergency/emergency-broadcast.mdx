<Callout type="warning" title="Real emergencies only">

Alerts reach people at any hour. Using them for events or rule reminders teaches residents to ignore them. Use a [pinned announcement](help:post-announcement) instead.

</Callout>

<StepByStep>

<Step title="Open Send Emergency Alert" shot="em-btn">

There is no sidebar link. Press **Ctrl+K** (**⌘K** on a Mac), type “emergency” and choose **Send Emergency Broadcast**. The alert has four steps: Template, Compose, Recipients and Confirm.

</Step>

<Step title="Pick a template" shot="em-tpl">

Hurricane Preparation, Water Shutoff, Gas Leak, Evacuation Order, Elevator Outage, Fire Alarm, Power Outage or Security Alert, or **Start from scratch**.

</Step>

<Step title="Check the message">

Set the **Severity** (Emergency, Urgent or Info) and edit the **Title** and **Email body**. If your community sends texts, edit the short **SMS body** too. Select **Next: Recipients**.

</Step>

<Step title="Choose recipients and channels" shot="em-rec">

Under **Audience**, choose **All residents** or **Owners only**. Under **Channels**, choose **SMS**, **Email**, or both. If text messaging is not enabled for your community, the alert goes by email only. Select **Next: Confirm**.

</Step>

<Step title="Confirm and send" shot="em-confirm">

Check the counts of recipients, SMS-eligible residents and email recipients. Check **I understand this will immediately alert…** and select **Send Emergency Alert**.

</Step>

<Step title="Undo within 10 seconds if needed">

A countdown runs before anything goes out. Select **UNDO - Cancel Broadcast** to stop it.

</Step>

</StepByStep>

Texts only reach residents who verified their phone number and agreed to text alerts in **Settings**, and most of them only get texts for **Emergency** severity. Everyone with an email address gets the email.

<Figure shot="em-stats" alt="Delivery counts: total, sent, delivered, failed">

Select **View Delivery Report** after sending. Counts update as they come in. Reach anyone who shows as failed by phone or in person.

</Figure>

When it is over, send an all-clear with what happened. Look at the templates now, before you need them.
