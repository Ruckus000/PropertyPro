A template sets the overall layout. A colour set changes the colours and fonts, and works with any template.

<StepByStep>

<Step title="Open Design">

Select **Design** in the tool rail on the left.

</Step>

<Step title="Pick a template">

Under **Template**, select one. **Show all templates** lists the rest. Your words, photos and sections stay the same when you switch.

</Step>

<Step title="Pick colours">

Under **Colours & fonts**, select a colour set. On the Professional plan you can also set your own colours and body font under **Your own colours**.

</Step>

</StepByStep>

Design changes are saved as a draft. They reach your site when you [publish](help:publish-website).
