A look sets how your whole website is laid out and what your home page starts with. There are four: **Bayfront**, **Heritage**, **Palm Grove** and **Plain & Simple**. A colour set changes the colours and fonts, and works with any look.

<StepByStep>

<Step title="Open Design">

Select **Design** in the tool rail on the left.

</Step>

<Step title="Pick a look">

Under **Look**, select one of the four. The page beside the panel changes right away, so you can look it over.

Sections you have edited or hidden stay just as you left them, and so do your other pages. Sections you haven’t edited follow the new look, and the new look may add a section your page didn’t have. To go back, select the look you started with.

</Step>

<Step title="Pick colours">

Under **Colours & fonts**, select a colour set. On the Professional plan you can also set your own colours and body font under **Your own colours**.

</Step>

</StepByStep>

Your choice of look and colours is saved as a draft. Nothing changes on your website until you [publish](help:publish-website).
