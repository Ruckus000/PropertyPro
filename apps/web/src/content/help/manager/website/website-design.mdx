A look sets how your whole website is laid out and what your home page starts with. There are four: **Bayfront**, **Heritage**, **Palm Grove** and **Plain & Simple**. You choose one in the editor. Colours and fonts are separate: they are in **Pages & design**, and they work with any look. Logos are on the **Settings** tab of your Website page.

<StepByStep>

<Step title="Open the looks">

On your Website page select **Edit website**, then **Look**. In Advanced, the looks are at the top of **Pages & design**, on the **Design** tab.

</Step>

<Step title="Pick a look">

Select one of the four. Your page beside the choices changes to that look straight away, but only in the editor: your website does not change until you publish.

Sections you have edited or hidden stay just as you left them, and so do your other pages. Sections you haven’t edited follow the new look, and the new look may add a section your page didn’t have.

</Step>

<Step title="Publish it, or keep what you have">

Select **Publish** in the top bar to put it on your website, along with any other changes you made. To go back, select the button under the looks that keeps your current one: **Keep** with your look’s name, or **Keep my current look**.

</Step>

<Step title="Pick colours and fonts">

In Advanced, select **Pages & design** in the top bar and open the **Design** tab. Under **Colours & fonts**, select a colour set. On the Professional plan you can also set your own colours and body font under **Your own colours**.

</Step>

</StepByStep>

A colour set or your own colours are saved as a draft. Nothing changes on your website until you [publish](help:publish-website). Logos are the exception: they go live as soon as they upload. See [Brand the community website](help:website-branding).
