A look sets how your whole website is laid out and what your home page starts with. There are four: **Bayfront**, **Heritage**, **Palm Grove** and **Plain & Simple**. You choose a look on the look picker. Colours, fonts and logos are separate: they are in **Site settings**, and they work with any look.

<StepByStep>

<Step title="Open the look picker">

Select **Website** in the sidebar, then **Change look**. From the editor, select **Site settings** in the top bar, open the **Design** tab, and select **Change look**.

</Step>

<Step title="Pick a look">

Select one of the four. A preview of your site in that look appears beside the choices, so you can look it over before anything changes.

Sections you have edited or hidden stay just as you left them, and so do your other pages. Sections you haven’t edited follow the new look, and the new look may add a section your page didn’t have.

</Step>

<Step title="Publish it, or keep what you have">

Select **Publish this look** to put it on your website. **Keep my current look** leaves your site as it is. If you have other unpublished edits, they publish with the look.

</Step>

<Step title="Pick colours and fonts">

In the editor, select **Site settings** in the top bar and open the **Design** tab. Under **Colours & fonts**, select a colour set. On the Professional plan you can also set your own colours and body font under **Your own colours**.

</Step>

</StepByStep>

A colour set or your own colours are saved as a draft. Nothing changes on your website until you [publish](help:publish-website). Logos are the exception: they go live as soon as they upload. See [Brand the community website](help:website-branding).
