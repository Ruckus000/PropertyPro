You can export your community’s records at any time, including after a subscription has lapsed. Board members can export too; other residents cannot.

<StepByStep>

<Step title="Open Data Export" shot="exp-page">

Select your initials at the top right, then **Data Export**.

</Step>

<Step title="Select Download CSV export, then enter your password" shot="exp-auth">

Under **Quick CSV export**, select **Download CSV export**. Enter your password and select **Confirm**. The ZIP downloads right away as community-export-(number).zip.

</Step>

</StepByStep>

## What is in the quick export

- Four spreadsheets (CSV): residents, documents (titles, categories and dates, not the files), maintenance requests and announcements.
- Not included: the uploaded document files, payments, violations, or other communities.
- Each spreadsheet stops at 10,000 rows. For complete records, use the full archive.

## Get everything with the full archive

Under **Full archive**, select **Prepare full archive**. It includes every record and the uploaded document files. It is prepared in the background, and you get an email when it is ready. Come back to **Data Export** and select **Download part 1** (large archives come in several parts). The files are deleted on the date shown; you can prepare a new one any time.

<Callout type="tip" title="A snapshot, not a backup">

The export does not update afterward. Download a new one when you need current records. Exports contain residents’ contact details, so store and share them with care.

</Callout>
