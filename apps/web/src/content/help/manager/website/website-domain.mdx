Every site has a PropertyPro address. On the Professional plan you can also connect a domain you own.

<StepByStep>

<Step title="Open Address & domain">

Select **Settings** in the top bar, then **Address & domain**. **Your PropertyPro address** is shown first, with **Copy**. It keeps working after you connect your own domain.

</Step>

<Step title="Enter your domain">

Under **Use your own domain**, type it in **Your domain**, such as www.yourcommunity.com, and select **Connect**. If you don’t have one yet, **Find one** checks whether a name is free and links to registrars that sell it.

</Step>

<Step title="Add the record at your registrar">

The panel shows the record to add, with its **Type**, **Name** and **Value**. Add it where you bought the domain, exactly as shown.

</Step>

<Step title="Select Check status">

Registrars can take a while to pass the change on, so check again later if it isn’t ready. When the domain works, it shows **Live**, with **View site**.

</Step>

</StepByStep>

**Remove** disconnects the domain. Your PropertyPro address keeps working either way.

<Callout type="info" title="This doesn’t wait for Publish">

Connecting or removing a domain takes effect straight away.

</Callout>
