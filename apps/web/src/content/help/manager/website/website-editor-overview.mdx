The editor has two parts: the bar across the top, and your page below it. Everything about your site that isn’t a section on a page is in **Site settings**.

<Figure shot="ov-editor" alt="The website editor: the top bar with Website, Site settings and Publish, the page in the middle with the Announcements section selected and its toolbar showing, and that section's settings on the right" />

## The top bar

- **Website** and **Site settings** switch between building your pages and your site’s settings. See below.
- **Editing page** shows which page you are working on. Open it to switch pages.
- The three screen buttons show your page at the width of a computer, a tablet or a phone.
- For condos and HOAs, a label shows whether the sections Florida law asks for are on your site. Select it to fix one.
- **Draft saved** appears after each change.
- **Help** opens these guides beside your page.
- **Preview** shows the page as visitors will see it once you publish. **Publish** puts your changes live.

## Site settings

Select **Site settings** in the top bar. Its tabs are:

- **General**: the footer and your site icon.
- **Pages**: add a page, rename it, change the menu, or remove it.
- **Design**: colour sets, your own colours, fonts and logos. You choose a look on the look picker; **Change look** is on this tab.
- **Notice**: post an [urgent notice](help:urgent-notice).
- **Address & domain**: your PropertyPro address and your own domain.
- **Search & sharing**: how your site shows in search results and when shared.
- **Access & visibility**: who can see what.

Each tab says at the top whether its changes save immediately or wait for Publish. Select **Website** to go back to your page.

## Your page

Click a section to select it. Its settings open on the right, and a toolbar appears on it to move, hide, copy or remove it. See [Change the words and photos](help:edit-words-and-photos).

<Callout type="info" title="What waits for Publish">

Changes to sections, pages you add, and the look of your site are saved as a draft. Residents see them only after you [publish](help:publish-website). Some things go live as soon as you save them instead: most of **Site settings**, a published page’s name and place in the menu, and an [urgent notice](help:urgent-notice).

</Callout>
