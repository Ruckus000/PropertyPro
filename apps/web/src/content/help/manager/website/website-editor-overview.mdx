The editor opens from **Edit website** on your Website page. It has two modes, switched at the top: **Simple** for the welcome and the look, and **Advanced** for pages and sections. Changes you make in either mode come with you to the other.

## Simple

Your home page, with two things beside it:

- **Welcome**: the headline, subtitle and photos at the top of your home page. See [Change the words and photos](help:edit-words-and-photos).
- **Look**: how your whole site is laid out. See [Choose a look](help:website-design).

Simple works on a phone too. Phone numbers, email and office hours come from your community profile, not the editor.

## Advanced

- **Page** and **Pages & design** switch between building a page and your site’s pages and look. See below.
- **Editing page** shows which page you are working on. Open it to switch pages.
- On a wider screen, the three screen buttons show your page at the width of a computer, a tablet or a phone.
- For condos and HOAs, a button shows whether the sections Florida law asks for are on your site. Select it to fix one.

Advanced needs a laptop or tablet. On a phone, use Simple.

## The rest of the top bar

- **Website** takes you back to your Website page.
- For condos and HOAs, when records are missing from your site, a link with a warning sign shows how many.
- **Draft saved** appears after each change.
- **Help** opens these guides beside your page.
- **Publish** puts your changes live, and takes you back to your Website page.

## Pages & design

In Advanced, select **Pages & design** in the top bar. Its tabs are:

- **Pages**: add a page, rename it, change the menu, or remove it.
- **Design**: the looks, colour sets, your own colours and fonts.

Each tab says at the top how its changes save. Select **Page** to go back to your page.

Settings that go live as soon as you save them are not in the editor. They are on the **Settings** tab of your Website page: an [urgent notice](help:urgent-notice), your site icon, footer and logos, your address and [your own domain](help:website-domain), [search and sharing](help:website-search), and who can see what.

## Your page

Click a section to select it. Its settings open on the right, and a toolbar appears on it to move, hide, copy or remove it. See [Change the words and photos](help:edit-words-and-photos).

To add a section, select **Add a part** between two sections, or at the end of the page. See [Add, move or remove a section](help:website-sections).

<Callout type="info" title="What waits for Publish">

Changes to sections, the welcome, pages you add, and the look of your site are saved as a draft. Residents see them only after you [publish](help:publish-website). One thing in the editor goes live as soon as you save it instead: a published page’s name and place in the menu.

</Callout>
