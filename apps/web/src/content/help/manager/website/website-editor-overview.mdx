The editor has three parts: the bar across the top, the tools down the left, and your page in the middle.

<Figure shot="ov-editor" alt="The website editor: the top bar with Website, Settings and Publish, the tool rail on the left, the page in the middle with the Announcements section selected and its toolbar showing, and that section's settings on the right" />

## The top bar

- **Website** and **Settings** switch between building your pages and your site’s settings, such as its address and how it shows in search results.
- **Editing page** shows which page you are working on. Open it to switch pages.
- The three screen buttons show your page at the width of a computer, a tablet or a phone.
- For condos and HOAs, a label shows whether the sections Florida law asks for are on your site. Select it to fix one.
- **Draft saved** appears after each change.
- **Help** opens these guides beside your page. On narrower screens it is in the tool rail instead.
- **Preview** shows the page as visitors will see it once you publish. **Publish** puts your changes live.

## The tools

**Add**, **Pages**, **Sections**, **Design**, **Documents** and **Notice** each open a panel next to your page. Select the same tool again, or the close button, to put the panel away. **Help**, at the bottom of the rail, opens these guides.

## Your page

Click a section to select it. Its settings open on the right, and a toolbar appears on it to move, hide, copy or remove it. See [Change the words and photos](help:edit-words-and-photos).

<Callout type="info" title="What waits for Publish">

Changes to sections, pages you add, and the look of your site are saved as a draft. Residents see them only after you [publish](help:publish-website). Some things go live as soon as you save them instead: everything in **Settings**, a published page’s name and place in the menu, and an [urgent notice](help:urgent-notice).

</Callout>
