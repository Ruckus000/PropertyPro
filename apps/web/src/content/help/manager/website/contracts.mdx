Open **Contracts** in the sidebar, under **Admin**. The page is called **Contracts & Vendors**.

<Figure shot="ct-alerts" alt="Expiration Alerts listing contracts that expire soon">

**Expiration Alerts** lists contracts ending within **90**, **60** and **30** days, and ones that have expired. Select a contract's title, there or in the table, to open its own page.

</Figure>

<StepByStep>

<Step title="Select Add contract" shot="ct-add">

It opens the **New Contract** page.

</Step>

<Step title="Enter the terms" shot="ct-form">

**Title**, **Vendor name** and **Start date** are required. Add a **Description**, a **Contract value ($)** written as a plain amount such as 12500 or 12500.00, and an **End date**, which cannot be before the start date. Set the **Status**: Draft, Active, Expired or Terminated. Check **Conflict of interest declared** if a director or officer has a financial interest, and say who and what in the **Conflict of interest note**.

</Step>

<Step title="Link the signed copy">

Upload the PDF in **Documents** first, then enter its ID in **Linked Document ID**. Select **Save contract**. The contract's page opens, and shows the linked document by its title.

</Step>

</StepByStep>

## Renewals and bids

To renew, open the contract, select **Edit**, extend the **End date** and select **Save changes**. To retire a contract, set its **Status** to **Terminated**; contracts are not deleted. For contracts you put out to bid, set **Bidding closes at**. Until that time the contract's page shows only how many bids have come in; each bid's vendor, amount and notes stay sealed for everyone, managers included. To record a bid, open the contract, select **Add bid** in the **Bids** section, enter the **Vendor name** and **Bid amount ($)**, and select **Save bid**. Record the board’s choice in the minutes.

<Figure shot="ct-bids" alt="Bids recorded on a contract" />

<Callout type="florida-statute" title="Florida Statutes §718.111(12)(g) and §720.303">

Contracts above the statutory threshold must be available to members. This contract list is only visible to managers, and the transparency page only shows whether required records are posted. Upload the signed copy to a member-visible category in **Documents**.

</Callout>
