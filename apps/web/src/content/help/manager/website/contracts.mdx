Open **Contracts** in the sidebar, under **Admin**. The page is called **Contracts & Vendors**.

<Figure shot="ct-alerts" alt="Expiration Alerts listing contracts that expire soon">

**Expiration Alerts** lists contracts ending within **90**, **60** and **30** days, and ones that have expired.

</Figure>

<StepByStep>

<Step title="Select Add Contract" shot="ct-add">
</Step>

<Step title="Enter the terms" shot="ct-form">

**Title**, **Vendor Name** and **Start Date** are required. Add a **Description**, **Contract Value ($)**, **End Date**, and check **Conflict of interest declared** if a director or officer has a financial interest.

</Step>

<Step title="Link the signed copy">

Upload the PDF in **Documents** first, then enter its ID in **Linked Document ID**. Select **Create**.

</Step>

</StepByStep>

## Renewals and bids

To renew, select **Edit** on the contract, extend the **End Date** and select **Update**. For contracts you put out to bid, set **Bidding Closes At**; bid details stay sealed until then. To record a bid, select **Bids** on the contract, then **+ Add Bid**, and enter the **Vendor Name** and **Bid Amount ($)**. Record the board’s choice in the minutes.

<Figure shot="ct-bids" alt="Bids recorded on a contract" />

<Callout type="florida-statute" title="Florida Statutes §718.111(12)(g) and §720.303">

Contracts above the statutory threshold must be available to members. This contract list is only visible to managers, and the transparency page only shows whether required records are posted. Upload the signed copy to a member-visible category in **Documents**.

</Callout>
