Select **Settings** in the top bar, then **Search & sharing**.

## Search results

- **Page title** is the headline in search results and browser tabs, up to 60 characters. Leave it empty to use your community name.
- **Description** is the line or two under it, up to 160 characters.
- The preview below shows roughly how the result will look.
- **Let search engines list this site** is on unless you turn it off. Turning it off asks search engines to leave your site out of their results. Your site stays online for anyone with the address.

Select **Save search settings**.

## Sharing image

The picture shown when someone shares a link to your site in a text, email or social post. Choose **Add an image**. A wide photo of your community works best: it is cropped to 1200 × 630. Without one, shared links show only your title and description.

## Page by page

Each page other than your home page can have its own **Title** and **Description**. Pick the **Page**, fill in either box, and select **Save page settings**. Leave a box empty to use the wording shown in grey. Your home page uses the settings at the top.

<Callout type="info" title="This doesn’t wait for Publish">

These settings go live as soon as you save them. Search engines can take a few days to show the change.

</Callout>
