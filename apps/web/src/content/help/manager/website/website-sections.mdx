Each page is a stack of sections, like a welcome message, meeting notices or a photo gallery.

## Add a section

- Select **Add a part** between two sections, or at the end of the page, then pick a section from the list. The new section goes where you selected.
- Besides text, images and a gallery, the list includes **Quick links**, a row of shortcuts to your meetings, news, owner documents and contact details, and **Board members**, the people on your board by name and title.

**Quick links** and **Board members** fill in by themselves from your community’s own records. In **Quick links**, the meetings, news and contact shortcuts appear only when your site has something to show there. In **Board members** you choose only the heading. You can move either section afterwards.

## Change a section

Select the section. Its toolbar appears on its top edge:

- **Move up** and **Move down** change its place. With the section selected, **Alt+Up** and **Alt+Down** do the same.
- **Hide** keeps it on the page for you but not for visitors. Select **Show again** to bring it back.
- **Duplicate** makes a copy.
- **Remove** asks you to confirm, then takes it off the page. You have 10 seconds to select **Undo**, and that includes a **Payments** section.

The welcome section at the top of your home page has no toolbar.

## Sections Florida law asks for

The **Documents** and **Meetings** sections post your official records and meeting notices. When Florida law applies to your association, you can move or hide them, but you can’t remove the only one on your site. See [What Florida law requires](help:florida-website-rules).
