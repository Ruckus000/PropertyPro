Residents don’t see changes to your welcome, sections, look or new pages until you publish. In the editor, Simple and Advanced both have **Publish** at the top right. If you left changes unpublished, your Website page says so and offers **Review and publish**.

<StepByStep>

<Step title="Select Publish">

It is at the top right. The number on it counts your changes.

</Step>

<Step title="Review your changes">

**Review and publish** lists what was added, edited, removed or moved. Anything listed under **stopping this publish** must be fixed first: select **Fix this** to go to it.

</Step>

<Step title="Publish now or later">

Select **Publish changes**. You go back to your Website page, which says your website was updated. To publish at a set time instead, tick **Publish later instead**, choose the date and time, and select **Schedule publish**.

</Step>

</StepByStep>

<OnlyFor types="condo_718 hoa_720">

<Callout type="florida-statute" title="Florida’s website rules">

Publishing checks the sections Florida law asks for. A hidden or missing **Documents** or **Meetings** section is flagged, but doesn’t stop you publishing. A button in the top bar shows these at any time. See [What Florida law requires](help:florida-website-rules).

</Callout>
