Your site has several pages. The menu at the top of your site links to them.

<StepByStep>

<Step title="Switch to another page">

Open the **Editing page** menu in the top bar and pick a page. **Add or manage pages** at the bottom of that menu opens the **Pages** tool.

</Step>

<Step title="Add a page">

In **Pages**, select **Add a page**, type a **Page name**, and select **Add page**. The web address fills in from the name. A new page is a draft: it isn’t on your site until you publish. A site can have up to 20 pages.

</Step>

<Step title="Change the order or the menu">

Use the up and down arrows next to a page to change the order. With the keyboard, focus the handle and press the arrow keys. In a page’s settings (the gear next to it), **Show in navigation** decides whether the page is in your site’s menu. A page left out of the menu is still online at its own address. To take it off your site, remove it.

</Step>

<Step title="Rename or remove a page">

Select the gear next to a page to open its settings, where you can change its **Page name**. You can change its **Web address** only before it is first published. **Remove page** takes it off your site when you next publish, and you can cancel until then. A page that was never published has **Delete page** instead, which deletes it straight away and can’t be undone. Your home page can’t be removed.

</Step>

</StepByStep>

<Callout type="info" title="Some page changes go live right away">

On a page that is already published, its name, its place in the menu and whether it shows in the menu change on your site straight away. Changes to its sections wait for **Publish**.

</Callout>
