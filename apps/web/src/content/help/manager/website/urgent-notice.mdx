Use a notice for things residents need to know today, like a pool closure or storm preparations.

<StepByStep>

<Step title="Open Notice">

Select **Notice** in the tool rail on the left.

</Step>

<Step title="Write the notice">

Type one or two sentences in **Notice text**, up to 240 characters.

</Step>

<Step title="Choose when it comes down (optional)">

Set **Take it down automatically**. Leave it empty and the notice stays up until you remove it.

</Step>

<Step title="Select Post notice">

The notice is live on every page of your website straight away. It does not wait for **Publish**.

</Step>

</StepByStep>

To take it down early, open **Notice** and select **Remove notice**. You have a few seconds to undo.

<Callout type="info" title="Your website must be published first">

A notice appears on your live website, so the panel asks you to publish your site once before you can post one. To reach residents by text or email instead, send an [emergency broadcast](help:emergency-broadcast).

</Callout>
