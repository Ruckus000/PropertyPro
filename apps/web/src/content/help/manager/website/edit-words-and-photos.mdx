You change a section’s words and photos in a panel next to your page. The page shows the result as you type.

<StepByStep>

<Step title="Select a section on your page">

Click any section. A label in its corner shows which one you picked, and its settings open on the right.

</Step>

<Step title="Change the words">

Type in the boxes in the settings panel. For the welcome section that is **Headline**, **Subtitle**, **Button label** and **Button link**. A text section has a **Heading** and a **Body**. Your changes save as a draft on their own.

</Step>

<Step title="Add photos to the welcome section">

Under **Add photos**, choose up to 8 photos from your computer. Describe each photo in its **Alt text** box, or tick **Decorative** if it adds nothing a visitor needs to know. Then select **Add photos to hero**. Use the arrows to change their order.

</Step>

<Step title="Add a photo elsewhere on the page">

Add an **Image** or **Gallery** section from the **Add** tool. Select **Upload a photo**, or **Choose from your photos** to reuse one already on your site.

</Step>

</StepByStep>

Press **Esc** to close the settings panel.

<Callout type="info" title="Nothing is live until you publish">

The top bar shows **Draft saved** after each change. Residents don’t see your changes until you [publish](help:publish-website).

</Callout>
