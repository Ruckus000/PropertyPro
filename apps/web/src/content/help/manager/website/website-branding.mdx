<StepByStep>

<Step title="Set your own colours (Professional and up)" shot="wb-colors">

Select **Design** in the tool rail on the left. Under **Your own colours**, turn on only the colours you want to change (**Use my own main colour**, **Use my own secondary colour** or **Use my own accent colour**); the others keep your colour set’s. Type a hex code or use the picker, and turn on **Use my own body font** to pick a font. Select **Save colours**.

Your colours are saved as a draft. They reach your site when you [publish](help:publish-website). To change the template or colour set instead, see [Change the look](help:website-design).

</Step>

<Step title="Connect a custom domain (Professional and up)" shot="wb-domain">

Select **Address** in the tool rail. Under **Your domain**, enter the hostname, such as www.yourcommunity.com, and select **Connect**. Add the DNS record shown at your registrar, then select **Check status**. The status reads **Live** once it works.

</Step>

</StepByStep>

On the Essentials plan, **Your own colours** and **Address** explain the upgrade instead, and your site uses the colours from your colour set.

<Callout type="tip" title="Choose readable colours">

The main colour needs **4.5:1** contrast with white text for buttons to be readable. Check the result on a phone.

</Callout>

On **Operations Plus**, **Templates** in the portfolio view saves one community’s branding and applies it to many communities.
