The audit trail records actions that matter for compliance or security, such as documents posted, viewed or deleted, meeting notices, announcements sent, invitations, settings changes and FAQ edits. Entries cannot be edited or deleted. Only managers can open it.

<StepByStep>

<Step title="Filter the list" shot="au-filters">

Select **Audit Trail** in the sidebar, under **Admin**. Narrow by **Action**, **Start Date** and **End Date**, or search a name or unit under **User**.

</Step>

<Step title="Open the details" shot="au-meta">

Each row shows the action, what it affected, when, and who did it. Expand **Metadata** on a row to see what was recorded.

</Step>

<Step title="Select Export CSV" shot="au-export">

The file has the same filters as your view and opens in a new tab. It stops at 10,000 rows, so narrow the dates first for large communities.

</Step>

</StepByStep>

<Callout type="warning" title="Treat exports as sensitive">

Exports can contain personal details. Share them only with the board and people authorized to see official records.

</Callout>
