The audit trail is a permanent record of what happened in your community and who did it: documents posted, opened or deleted, notices sent, access changes, website changes and more. Each event reads as a sentence, such as "Maria Lopez deleted the document 2025 Reserve Study.pdf". Entries cannot be edited or deleted. Only managers can open it.

<StepByStep>

<Step title="Find what you're looking for" shot="au-filters">

Open **More** at the top of the page and select **Audit Trail**, under **Admin**. To look into one person or one thing, type a name, document title or unit in the search box and pick it from the list. You can also select any bold name in the list.

Use the buttons under the search to narrow the list: **Important** (the default), **Everything**, **Deleted**, **Downloads**, **Access changes** or **Notices**. Choose a time period on the right.

</Step>

<Step title="Open an event for the details" shot="au-meta">

Select any event to open its details: the exact time, what changed (old value → new value), and why it matters when there's something to act on. From there you can see everything that happened to the same document, or everything the same person did.

</Step>

<Step title="Print the record or download the list" shot="au-export">

When you've narrowed the list to one person or document, select **Print record** for a version laid out for paper. Choose **Save as PDF** in the print window to keep a copy. Without a selection, **Download** saves the current list as a spreadsheet (CSV), up to 10,000 entries.

</Step>

</StepByStep>

<Callout type="info" title="Why some events are hidden">

**Important** leaves out routine events, like each time someone opens a document or a charge is posted automatically. The bottom of the list says how many it hid. Select **Everything** to see them.

</Callout>

<Callout type="warning" title="Treat printouts and downloads as sensitive">

They can contain personal details. Share them only with the board and people authorized to see official records. Printing a record is itself recorded in the audit trail.

</Callout>
