The transparency page shows the public **whether** records are posted, not the records themselves. It updates as you link records on the compliance dashboard.

<StepByStep>

<Step title="Open Transparency Settings">

Press **⌘K** (Ctrl+K on Windows), type **Transparency**, and select **Transparency Settings**.

</Step>

<Step title="Check Enable public transparency page" shot="tp-settings">

The public address is shown under it. It uses your community’s subdomain. Select **Preview what your transparency page will look like** to check it first.

</Step>

<Step title="Check the acknowledgment and select Save Settings" shot="tp-ack">

The first time, you must check the box that says the page shows factual data and is not a legal certification. The status then reads **Page is live**. To take it down, clear **Enable public transparency page** and save.

</Step>

</StepByStep>

<Figure shot="tp-public" alt="Public transparency page with document posting status">

Four sections: **Document Posting Status**, **Meeting Compliance Records**, **Minutes Tracking** and **Access Controls**.

</Figure>

<Callout type="warning" title="It does not replace owner access">

The law also requires a protected area for owners and staff. The transparency page never shows owner details, balances, open violations or privileged material. Ask counsel what to redact.

</Callout>
