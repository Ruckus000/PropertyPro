Use **Resident gave notice** when a resident tells you they are leaving at or before the end of the lease. For a resident who stayed after the lease ended, use **Record move-out**. A declined renewal offer also schedules a move-out. See [Send a renewal offer](help:renewing-a-lease).

## Record a notice to vacate

<StepByStep>
<Step title="Open the unit">
Select the unit number, or anywhere on its row.
</Step>

<Step title="Select Resident gave notice">
It is with the other buttons near the top of the unit panel, next to **End early**. The **Record notice to vacate** dialog opens, with the residents' names and the lease end date.
</Step>

<Step title="Enter the dates">
**Notice received on** starts at today. It can't be a future date. **Move-out date** starts at the lease end date, and can't be later than it. The hint under it says when the unit becomes available to lease. A note shows the deposit deadlines counted from the move-out date.
</Step>

<Step title="Select Record move-out">
Add anything useful in **Notes**, such as a forwarding address. Select **Record move-out**. A confirmation says **Unit 101 moving out** with the date, and includes **Undo**. The row's **Renewal** column shows **Moving out** with the date, and the link changes to **Pre-lease**. The unit is listed in the **Moving out** group under the **Vacant** tile. The unit panel shows the deposit deadlines.
</Step>
</StepByStep>

Recording the move-out also starts a move-out checklist on the Move In/Out page, if the primary resident has a PropertyPro account. See [Move-in and move-out checklists](help:move-in-out).

The resident stays in the unit, and the unit counts as occupied, until the move-out date. The next day, the lease moves to **Lease history** and the unit shows **Vacant**, or **Pre-leased** if you added the next lease. To pre-lease it, see [Add a lease or pre-lease a unit](help:adding-a-lease).

If the move-out date has already passed, the hint says so. The lease closes now, and the unit shows as vacant from the day after that date.

**Resident gave notice** is not shown once a move-out, early end or transfer is recorded, once a renewal is signed, while the unit is pre-leased, or for a holdover.

## Cancel a move-out

If the resident decides to stay, open the unit and select **Cancel move-out**. There is no dialog. A confirmation says the unit stays on its current term, and includes **Undo**. The row goes back to its earlier status. The move-out checklist, if one was started, stays on the Move In/Out page.

If the unit has been pre-leased, **Cancel move-out** is not shown. Cancel the upcoming lease first with **Cancel before move-in** (see [Edit or cancel an upcoming lease](help:upcoming-lease)), then select **Cancel move-out**.

## Month-to-month tenancies

For a month-to-month lease, **Move-out date** starts empty. Enter the resident's last day. The notice is counted from **Notice received on**. If the two dates are less than 30 days apart, a warning appears. You can still select **Record move-out**, for example when both sides agreed to the shorter date.

## Holdovers

A unit shows **Holdover** when its lease end date has passed, the resident is still in the unit, and no renewal or move-out has been recorded. The **Lease end** column shows how long ago it ended, in red, for example **6 days past end**. The **Renewals due** tile adds a red line such as **1 past end date**. Holdovers are listed first when the list is sorted by **Lease end**.

<StepByStep>
<Step title="Select Resolve holdover">
A holdover's row ends with **Resolve holdover**. It opens the unit panel, which shows **Holdover** and the date the lease ended.
</Step>

<Step title="Choose what happens next">
If the resident is staying, select **Convert to month-to-month**. There is no dialog. The lease keeps its terms with no end date, and a confirmation says the unit is now month-to-month, with **Undo**. The unit shows **Month-to-month** and leaves **Renewals due**. To agree a new fixed term later, see [Offer a fixed term](help:renewing-a-lease).

If the resident is leaving or has left, select **Record move-out**. The **Record notice to vacate** dialog opens and says when the lease ended. **Notice received on** starts at today. **Move-out date** starts empty. Enter the actual or planned last day. It can be after the old end date.
</Step>

<Step title="Select Record move-out in the dialog">
If the date has passed, the lease closes right away and the unit shows **Vacant** from the next day. If the date is in the future, the row shows **Moving out** with the date until then, and the panel offers **Cancel move-out**. This also starts the move-out checklist.
</Step>
</StepByStep>

After the resident leaves, record the deposit refund or claim. See [Record a security deposit and its notice](help:deposit-notice).

<Callout type="florida-statute" title="Florida Statutes §83.49(3) and §83.57">
After you record a move-out, the unit panel shows two dates: return the deposit within 15 days of the move-out date, or send a claim notice by certified mail within 30 days (§83.49). For a month-to-month tenancy, the dialog warns when the notice is shorter than 30 days, the written notice Florida requires before the end of a monthly period (§83.57).
</Callout>
