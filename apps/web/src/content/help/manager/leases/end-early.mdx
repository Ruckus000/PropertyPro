<StepByStep>

<Step title="Select End lease early" shot="end-1">

Open the unit. The link is at the bottom left of the panel. For a month-to-month lease, it says **End tenancy**.

</Step>

<Step title="Choose a reason" shot="end-2">

The reason is saved with the lease record. If you choose **Other**, describe the reason in Notes.

</Step>

<Step title="Enter the last day" shot="end-3">

It must be before the scheduled end date. The message below the date shows when the unit becomes available.

</Step>

<Step title="Select End lease early" shot="end-4">

The row shows **Ending** with the last day. The unit stays occupied until then, and you can pre-lease it.

</Step>

<Step title="To undo it, select Cancel early end" shot="end-5">

You can do this from the unit panel until the last day.

</Step>

</StepByStep>

If the resident is moving to another unit here, use [Transfer](help:transfer) instead so the deposit carries over.

## Month-to-month tenancies

Also enter the date written notice was given. If the notice date and the last day are less than 30 days apart, a warning appears. To continue, check **Both parties agreed to this date in writing**, or choose a later date.

<Figure shot="end-6" alt="End tenancy dialog with notice date, last day, and the 30-day notice warning" />

<Callout type="florida-statute" title="Month-to-month: Florida Statute §83.57">

Ending a month-to-month tenancy requires at least **30 days’** written notice before the end of a monthly period.

</Callout>

<Callout type="florida-statute" title="Security deposits: Florida Statute §83.49">

Deposit deadlines start from the last day: **15 days** to return it, or **30 days** to send a claim notice by certified mail.

</Callout>
