<StepByStep>

<Step title="Select Transfer" shot="tr-1">

Open the resident’s current unit. **Transfer** is at the bottom of the panel, next to **End lease early**.

</Step>

<Step title="Choose the new unit" shot="tr-2">

**Move to** lists vacant units and units with a recorded move-out. Rent fills in with the new unit’s market rent.

</Step>

<Step title="Set the move-in date and term" shot="tr-3">

The current lease ends the day before. The date must be on or after the new unit is free, and no later than the day after the current lease ends.

</Step>

<Step title="Check rent and the deposit" shot="tr-4">

The deposit carries over. If you raise it, the §83.49 notice fields appear for the difference.

</Step>

<Step title="Select Record transfer" shot="tr-5">

The new unit’s panel opens. The old unit shows **Transfer** with the last day, and you can pre-lease it. To undo, select **Cancel transfer** on either unit.

</Step>

</StepByStep>

<Callout type="info" title="Deposits on a transfer">

Because the deposit moves with the residents, no refund or claim notice is due for the old unit.

</Callout>
