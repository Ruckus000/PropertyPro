An offline unit is not offered for new leases or transfers, and is left out of the vacancy count and the occupancy rate. Only an empty unit can go offline: one with no current or upcoming lease.

## Take a unit offline

<StepByStep>
<Step title="Open an empty unit">
Select the row of a vacant unit that has no upcoming lease. The unit panel shows **New lease** and **Take offline**.
</Step>

<Step title="Select Take offline">
The **Take unit offline** dialog opens.
</Step>

<Step title="Choose a reason and dates">
Choose a **Reason**: **Storm or casualty damage**, **Renovation or repairs**, **Model unit**, **Staff or office use** or **Other**. **Offline since** starts at today, and can't be a future date. **Expected back** is optional. It shows on the unit so the team knows when to expect it back. **Note** is optional, except with **Other**.
</Step>

<Step title="Select Take offline">
A confirmation says the unit is offline and no longer counts as vacant, and includes **Undo**. The row shows **Offline**, the reason (for example **Offline · storm damage**), and **Back** with the expected date. The **All units** tile counts it as offline, and the **Vacant** tile lists it in the **Offline** group.
</Step>
</StepByStep>

## Bring a unit back online

<StepByStep>
<Step title="Open the offline unit and select Bring back online">
**Bring back online** is the only button in the unit panel.
</Step>

<Step title="Check the details and select Bring back online">
The dialog shows **Reason**, **Offline since**, **Expected back** and any **Note**. Select **Bring back online**. A confirmation says the unit is back online and can be leased, and includes **Undo**. The unit shows **Vacant** again.
</Step>
</StepByStep>

To change the expected date or the reason, bring the unit back online, then take it offline again with the new details.

## If the unit is occupied

A unit with a current or upcoming lease can't go offline, and its panel doesn't show **Take offline**. If damage forces a lease to end, first [end the lease early](help:ending-a-lease-early) with the reason **Casualty damage (§83.63)**. After the last day, take the unit offline.
