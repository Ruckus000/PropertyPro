<StepByStep>

<Step title="Select Take offline" shot="off-1">

Open a vacant unit with no upcoming lease. **Take offline** is at the bottom of the panel.

</Step>

<Step title="Choose a reason and dates" shot="off-2">

Pick the reason and the date it went offline. **Expected back** is optional. If you choose **Other**, explain in Notes.

</Step>

<Step title="Bring it back online" shot="off-3">

The panel shows the reason and dates. Select **Bring back online** when work is done, or **Edit details** to change the return date. If the date passes, it turns amber.

</Step>

</StepByStep>

<Figure shot="off-4" alt="Row for Unit 402 showing Offline, storm damage, back Nov 12">

Offline units stay in the list with their reason and return date. They are not offered in New lease or Transfer.

</Figure>

If damage forces an occupied unit’s lease to end, first [end the lease early](help:end-early) with the reason **Casualty damage (§83.63)**. Take the unit offline after the last day.

<Callout type="florida-statute" title="Florida Statute §83.63">

If damage makes a unit unlivable, the resident may be able to end the lease or reduce rent. Record what was agreed in Notes.

</Callout>
