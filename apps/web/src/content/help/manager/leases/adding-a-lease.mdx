<StepByStep>

<Step title="Select New lease" shot="add-1">

It is at the top of the page. A vacant unit’s row also ends with a **New lease** link.

</Step>

<Step title="Pick the unit" shot="add-2">

The list shows vacant units and units with a recorded move-out date. Offline units and units that already have an upcoming lease are not listed.

</Step>

<Step title="Add the residents" shot="add-3">

Type at least two letters of a name, then select the person. The first person you add is the primary resident. Add co-tenants the same way. For someone new, select **Add “name” as a new resident**.

</Step>

<Step title="Choose the term and start date" shot="add-4">

Pick 6, 12 or 18 months and the end date fills in. Choose **Custom** to enter any end date up to 36 months out. For a unit that is moving out, the lease must start after the current resident’s last day.

</Step>

<Step title="Enter rent and the deposit" shot="add-5">

Both start at the market rent for the floor plan. If you collected a deposit, choose how it is held and enter the bank’s name and Florida address. Enter 0 if there is no deposit.

</Step>

<Step title="Attach the signed lease" shot="add-8">

Optional. Select **Choose a file** to upload the PDF. You can also attach it later from the unit panel.

</Step>

<Step title="Select Create lease" shot="add-6">

A confirmation appears with **Undo**, and the unit panel opens. A lease that starts today shows the unit as **Leased**. A later start shows **Pre-leased** until move-in.

</Step>

</StepByStep>

## Residents without an email

When you add a new resident, check **No email address** if they do not have one. Notices go to the unit by mail or hand delivery, and no resident portal invite is sent.

<Figure shot="nr-1" alt="New resident form with No email address checked" />

## Staff and rent-free units

Enter 0 for monthly rent. A **Why is there no rent?** field appears. Choose staff, courtesy officer or a rent-free agreement. If you choose **Other**, explain in Notes.

## Pre-lease a unit that is moving out

After you record a move-out or an early end, open the unit and select **Pre-lease this unit**. The start date is set to the day after the last day. These units are also listed under the **Vacant** tile, in the **Moving out** group.

<Figure shot="add-7" alt="Unit panel buttons: Start move-out checklist and Pre-lease this unit" />

To change or cancel a pre-lease, see [Edit or cancel an upcoming lease](help:upcoming-lease).

<Callout type="florida-statute" title="Deposit notice: Florida Statute §83.49(2)">

Within **30 days** of receiving a deposit, you must tell the resident in writing how and where it is held. Choose **Send it now** when you create the lease. See [Send the deposit notice](help:deposit-notice).

</Callout>
