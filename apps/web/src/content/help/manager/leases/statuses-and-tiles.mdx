The Leases page has one row per unit. Each row shows the lease in effect today. The last column shows the next step, if there is one.

## Summary tiles

<Figure shot="st-1" alt="All units and Renewals due tiles">

Select a tile to filter the list. Select it again, or select **All units**, to clear the filter.

</Figure>

- **All units** shows how many units are occupied today. Occupancy leaves out offline units. Holdovers and units moving out count as occupied until the resident leaves.
- **Renewals due** counts leases that end inside your outer alert window, plus holdovers, that still need a renewal decision.
- **Vacant** counts empty units, including pre-leased ones, but not offline units. Its list also shows units that are moving out, so you can pre-lease them, and offline units in their own group.
- **Month-to-month** counts current leases with no end date.

## Unit statuses

- **Leased**: a current lease with more than 90 days left.
- **Expiring**: the lease ends inside your outer alert window.
- **Holdover**: the end date has passed and nothing was recorded.
- **Month-to-month**: a current lease with no end date.
- **Moving out** or **Ending**: a move-out or early end is recorded.
- **Pre-leased**: vacant or moving out, with an upcoming lease signed.
- **Vacant** and **Offline**: no current lease, or taken out of service.

## Next-step links

<Figure shot="st-2" alt="Row for Unit 212 ending with a Record response link">

Rows with nothing to do end with an arrow that opens the unit.

</Figure>

Renewed, expired, ended-early, transferred and cancelled leases are kept as records. Select **Past leases** in the list header to see them.
