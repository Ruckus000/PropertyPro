<StepByStep>

<Step title="Open the unit">

Select the unit’s row.

</Step>

<Step title="Select Resident is moving out" shot="mo-1">

It is under the renewal buttons in the unit panel.

</Step>

<Step title="Enter the move-out date" shot="mo-2">

The date starts as the lease end date. It must be today or later, and no later than the lease end. Select **Record move-out**.

</Step>

<Step title="Start the move-out" shot="mo-3">

The panel shows the move-out date and your deposit deadlines. Select **Start move-out checklist** to open it in Move In/Out, or **Pre-lease this unit**. **Cancel move-out**, below these, undoes the move-out.

</Step>

<Step title="Or start from the row" shot="mo-4">

Until the checklist is started, rows for units moving out show **Start move-out**. After that they show **Pre-lease**.

</Step>

</StepByStep>

## Holdovers

A unit shows **Holdover** when its end date has passed and nothing has been recorded. The row shows how many days past the end date it is.

<Figure shot="mo-5" alt="Row with a red Holdover status, 6 days past end" />

If the resident has left or is leaving, open the unit and select **Record move-out**, then enter the actual date. A past date closes the lease right away, and the unit is vacant from the next day. If the resident is staying, [send an offer](help:renewal-offer) or [record a signed renewal](help:record-renewal).

<Figure shot="mo-6" alt="Holdover unit panel with Send renewal offer, Record signed renewal, and Record move-out" />

<Callout type="florida-statute" title="Security deposits: Florida Statute §83.49">

After move-out, you have **15 days** to return the deposit if you are not making a claim. If you are making a claim, send written notice by certified mail within **30 days**. The unit panel shows both dates.

</Callout>

<Callout type="florida-statute" title="Holdovers: Florida Statute §83.58">

Section 83.58 covers residents who stay after the lease ends without the landlord’s consent. How it applies depends on your lease and on whether you accepted rent after the end date.

</Callout>
