<StepByStep>

<Step title="Open the unit" shot="edit-1">

Select the unit number or anywhere on its row.

</Step>

<Step title="Select Edit lease" shot="edit-2">

It is at the bottom of the unit panel.

</Step>

<Step title="Make the correction" shot="edit-3">

You can change residents, monthly rent, the security deposit and how it is held, the move-out notice period, and notes. The dates of a current lease are shown, but you cannot edit them here.

</Step>

<Step title="Select Save changes" shot="edit-4">

A confirmation appears with **Undo**. Select it within a few seconds to restore the old values.

</Step>

</StepByStep>

## Attach the signed lease

Leases without a signed copy show **Not attached** in amber. Select **Attach signed lease** under the lease details and choose the PDF. To swap in a new copy, select **Replace signed lease**.

<Figure shot="doc-1" alt="Signed lease: Not attached, with an Attach signed lease button" />

## Changing dates or the term

- To change when the lease ends, [record a renewal](help:record-renewal) or [end it early](help:end-early).
- Upcoming leases can be edited in full until move-in. See [Edit or cancel an upcoming lease](help:upcoming-lease).
- Rent normally changes at renewal. Edit rent here only to fix a mistake.
