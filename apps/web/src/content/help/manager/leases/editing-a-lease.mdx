Use **Edit lease** to fix a mistake in the lease record. Rent normally changes at renewal, not here.

<StepByStep>
<Step title="Open the unit" shot="step-1">
Select the unit number, or anywhere on its row. The unit panel opens.
</Step>

<Step title="Select Edit lease" shot="step-2">
**Edit lease** is with the other buttons near the top of the panel. The **Edit lease** dialog opens and shows the lease dates. The dates can't be changed here.
</Step>

<Step title="Make the correction" shot="step-3">
You can change **Monthly rent**, **Notice to leave at term end (days)** (not shown for a month-to-month lease) and **Notes**. If you change the rent to 0, choose a reason under **Why is there no rent?**.
</Step>

<Step title="Select Save changes" shot="step-4">
A confirmation says **Lease for Unit 101 updated.** The **Current lease** section of the unit panel shows the new values.
</Step>
</StepByStep>

If the panel says **Rent is not recorded for this lease. Edit the lease to add it.**, use these steps to enter the rent.

<Callout type="info" title="This lease changed since you opened it">
If someone else saved a change to the lease while your dialog was open, your change is not saved, and the dialog shows this message with a **Reload** button. Select **Reload**. The dialog closes and the page loads the latest version. Open **Edit lease** again and make your change.
</Callout>

## What Edit lease can't change

- **Dates.** To change when a lease ends, [send a renewal offer](help:renewing-a-lease), [record a move-out](help:move-out-and-holdovers) or [end it early](help:ending-a-lease-early). A lease that has not started can have its term changed. See [Edit or cancel an upcoming lease](help:upcoming-lease).
- **Residents.** Residents change at renewal: the renewal offer lists everyone on the new term.
- **The deposit.** Use **Deposit** in the unit panel. See [Record a security deposit and its notice](help:deposit-notice).
- **The signed lease.** When a signed copy is on file, **Current lease** shows **Signed lease: Attached**. The signed lease can't be attached from this page.

<Callout type="florida-statute" title="Florida Statute §83.575">
**Notice to leave at term end (days)** is how much notice the resident must give to move out when the term ends. The hint says it must match the signed lease and that Florida caps it at 60 days (§83.575). The unit panel uses it to show the notice deadline for the lease.
</Callout>
