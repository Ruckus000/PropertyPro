Record the renewal once the resident has accepted the offer and signed. First [send the offer and record the acceptance](help:renewing-a-lease).

<StepByStep>
<Step title="Select Record renewal">
After you record an acceptance, the row shows **Accepted · not signed** and **Record renewal**. In the unit panel, the button is also **Record renewal**. The **Record renewal** dialog opens and says the resident signed the renewal.
</Step>

<Step title="Check the terms">
The dialog shows the accepted offer: **Rent**, **Term**, **Starts**, **Deposit**, **Sent** and **Expires**. Below it, it says when the new term starts, and that the current lease stays as it is until then. If the new term has an end date, check **Notice to leave at term end (days)**. It starts with the current lease's notice period. Enter what the signed renewal says, from 0 to 60.
</Step>

<Step title="Select Record renewal">
A confirmation says **Renewal recorded for Unit 101. The new term starts** and the date. The row shows **Renewed · starts** with the date, and the unit leaves **Renewals due**. The unit panel shows a **Signed renewal** section with the new term and rent.
</Step>
</StepByStep>

The terms, residents and deposit come from the offer. If the terms are wrong, select **Change response** instead, choose **Withdraw offer**, and send a new offer. See [Send a renewal offer](help:renewing-a-lease). After you record the renewal, **Edit** on the **Signed renewal** section can still change the rent, the notice period and the notes.

## Until the new term starts

The current lease stays in effect until its end date. On the renewal's start date, the renewal becomes the current lease, and the old lease moves to **Lease history** and **Past leases**.

Before then, you can change or cancel the renewal from the **Signed renewal** section: **Edit** changes the rent, notice period and notes, and **Cancel before move-in** cancels it. See [Edit or cancel an upcoming lease](help:upcoming-lease).

If the deposit did not change, the renewal keeps the deposit of the lease it renews, and the **Deposit (§83.49)** section keeps showing it. If the offer changed the amount, the renewal gets a new deposit record. Select **Deposit** under **Signed renewal** (or, once the renewal has started, with the buttons at the top), choose **Notice or how it is held**, and record when the written notice went out. See [Record a security deposit and its notice](help:deposit-notice).

<Callout type="florida-statute" title="Florida Statutes §83.575 and §83.49(2)">
The **Notice to leave at term end (days)** hint says it must match the signed lease and that Florida caps it at 60 days (§83.575). A changed deposit restarts the 30-day written deposit notice (§83.49(2)).
</Callout>
