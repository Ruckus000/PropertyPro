The Leases page has one row per unit, not one row per lease. Each row shows who lives in the unit today, the lease status, when the lease ends, the rent, and where the renewal stands. When a unit needs something from you, its row ends with a link for that next step.

<FeatureGate feature="apartment communities only">
Lease tracking is available in communities set up as apartments. Condos and HOAs don't see the Leases page.
</FeatureGate>

## Find a unit and open it

<StepByStep>
<Step title="Filter with a tile">
The four tiles at the top are also filters: **All units**, **Renewals due**, **Vacant** and **Month-to-month**. Select one to filter the list. The list heading changes to match, for example **Renewals due** or **Vacant and moving out**. Select the same tile again to go back to **All units**.
</Step>

<Step title="Search or sort the list">
Type in **Search unit, resident or email** to narrow the list. Select **Unit** or **Lease end** to change the order. When the full list is sorted by **Unit**, it is grouped by floor, and each group heading shows how many units are occupied. Select a group heading to collapse it. A filtered or searched list shows 50 units at a time, with a **Show … more** button at the bottom.
</Step>

<Step title="Open the unit">
Select the unit number, or anywhere on its row. The unit panel opens on the right with the unit as its title, for example **Unit 101**. It shows the unit's status, any dates or warnings, the buttons for what you can do now, and the sections **Residents**, **Renewal**, **Current lease**, **Upcoming lease**, **Deposit (§83.49)** and **Lease history** when they apply.
</Step>
</StepByStep>

In the **Residents** section, **View resident** opens the Residents page.

## Where each task starts

**Start a lease**

- [Add a lease or pre-lease a unit](help:adding-a-lease): **New lease** in the list header, or **Pre-lease** on a unit that is moving out.
- [Edit or cancel an upcoming lease](help:upcoming-lease): **Edit** or **Cancel before move-in** in the unit panel.
- [Record a security deposit and its notice](help:deposit-notice): **Deposit** in the unit panel.
- [Correct a lease](help:editing-a-lease): **Edit lease** in the unit panel.

**Renew**

- [Send a renewal offer and record the answer](help:renewing-a-lease): **Send offer**, **Record response**, and **Offer a fixed term** for a month-to-month lease.
- [Record a signed renewal](help:recording-a-renewal): **Record renewal**.

**End a lease**

- [Record a move-out or a holdover](help:move-out-and-holdovers): **Resident gave notice**, or **Resolve holdover** for a lease past its end date.
- [End a lease early](help:ending-a-lease-early): **End early**.
- [Transfer residents to another unit](help:transferring-units): **Transfer to another unit**.

**Settings and reference**

- [Take a unit offline](help:unit-offline): **Take offline** and **Bring back online**.
- [Set expiry alerts](help:expiry-alert-windows): the **Expiry alerts** menu in the list header.
- [Unit statuses, tiles and next steps](help:lease-statuses): what every label on the page means.

## Past leases

Leases that ended, were renewed, were transferred or were cancelled leave the main list. Select **Past leases** in the list header to see them. The button shows how many there are. Each row says how the lease ended, for example **Ended Sep 30, 2026**, **Ended early**, **Transferred** or **Cancelled before move-in**. Select a row to open that unit. Select **Back to all units** to return.

## Undo a change

After a change, a confirmation appears at the bottom of the screen for 10 seconds. Some confirmations include **Undo**:

- **Renewal offer sent:** **Undo** withdraws the offer.
- **Notice to vacate or early end recorded:** **Undo** clears the move-out date.
- **Move-out or early end cancelled, or holdover converted to month-to-month:** **Undo** puts it back.
- **Unit taken offline, or brought back online:** **Undo** reverses it.
- **Deposit refund or claim recorded:** **Undo** clears it.
- **Expiry alerts changed:** **Undo** restores the previous setting.

After the confirmation closes, or for changes without **Undo**, use the unit panel:

- **A recorded move-out or early end:** select **Cancel move-out** or **Cancel early end**. If the unit is already pre-leased, cancel the upcoming lease first. See [Record a move-out](help:move-out-and-holdovers).
- **A transfer:** cancel the upcoming lease on the new unit, then select **Cancel move-out** on the old unit. See [Transfer residents](help:transferring-units).
- **A new lease or a signed renewal that has not started:** select **Cancel before move-in**. See [Edit or cancel an upcoming lease](help:upcoming-lease).
- **An accepted offer:** select **Change response**. See [Send a renewal offer](help:renewing-a-lease).
- **Lease details:** select **Edit lease** and change them back.

## Who can use the Leases page

Property managers can see and change every lease in the community. Only the root manager can change the settings in the **Expiry alerts** menu: the alert windows and **Allow household members on leases**. Everyone else sees the current settings.

If some details fail to load, a banner says **Some lease details are unavailable.** Lease dates and statuses are still complete, but resident names or renewal offers may be missing. Refresh the page to try again.
