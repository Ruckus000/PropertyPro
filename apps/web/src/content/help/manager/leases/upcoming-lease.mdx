Units with a signed future lease show an **Upcoming lease** or **Renewal term** card in the unit panel.

## Edit before move-in

<StepByStep>

<Step title="Select Edit on the card" shot="edit-next">

You can change residents, dates, term, rent and the deposit for an upcoming lease. For a renewal term the dates follow the current lease, so only the other fields can change.

</Step>

<Step title="Select Save changes">

A confirmation appears with **Undo**.

</Step>

</StepByStep>

## Cancel an upcoming lease

<StepByStep>

<Step title="Select Cancel upcoming lease" shot="can-1">

It is on the Upcoming lease card. For a renewal, the link is **Cancel renewal**.

</Step>

<Step title="Choose a reason and the deposit outcome" shot="can-2">

If a deposit was collected, choose **Refund in full** or **Keep part or all**. Each option shows its §83.49 deadline.

</Step>

<Step title="Select Cancel lease" shot="can-3">

The lease moves to **Lease history** as **Cancelled**, with the reason and deposit outcome. The unit becomes available again.

</Step>

</StepByStep>

<Callout type="warning" title="Entered by mistake">

Choosing **Entered by mistake** deletes the record instead. Use it only if the lease was never signed and no deposit was collected.

</Callout>

<Callout type="florida-statute" title="Florida Statute §83.49(3)(a)">

Return the deposit within **15 days**, or send a written claim notice by certified mail within **30 days**. The resident then has 15 days to object.

</Callout>
