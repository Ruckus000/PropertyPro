<StepByStep>

<Step title="Select Renewals due" shot="ren-1">

The tile shows leases that end inside your outer alert window, plus holdovers. The list sorts by lease end, with holdovers first.

</Step>

<Step title="Select Send offer" shot="ren-2">

Rows that say **No offer yet** end with **Send offer**.

</Step>

<Step title="Check the terms" shot="ren-3">

Pick a term, then confirm the start date and rent. Under the rent field you can see the change from the current rent and the market rent.

</Step>

<Step title="Send the offer" shot="ren-4">

Leave **Send offer to resident** selected. Set **Offer expires**, then select **Send offer** at the bottom of the dialog. The resident reviews it in the resident portal.

</Step>

<Step title="Check the row" shot="ren-5">

The row now shows **Offer sent today** and a **Record response** link. The current lease does not change.

</Step>

</StepByStep>

## Change residents or the deposit

The offer starts with the current residents. Remove anyone who is leaving, or add a co-tenant, the same way as on a new lease.

<Figure shot="ren-res" alt="Residents field in the renewal dialog with two residents" />

The deposit starts at the current amount. The hint under it shows the change. A higher deposit needs a new written notice, and a lower one means refunding the difference.

<Figure shot="ren-dep" alt="Security deposit raised, with the hint about a new §83.49 notice" />

## Offer expiry

**Offer expires** defaults to 14 days out, and must be before the new term starts. After that date the resident can no longer accept in the portal. If the date is after the lease’s notice deadline, a warning appears.

<Figure shot="ren-expf" alt="Offer expires date field" />

Expired offers show **Offer expired** in amber, and the row link changes to **Resend offer**. In the unit panel, select **Send a new offer**. If the resident accepted on paper, you can still select **Mark accepted**.

<Figure shot="ren-exp" alt="Row showing Offer expired Sep 27 with a Resend offer link" />

## Record the resident’s answer

<StepByStep start={6}>

<Step title="Select Record response" shot="ren-6">

The unit panel opens.

</Step>

<Step title="Mark the answer" shot="ren-7">

Select **Mark accepted** or **Mark declined**. **Revise offer** lets you change the terms and resend them.

</Step>

<Step title="If declined, enter the move-out date" shot="ren-8">

Enter the resident’s last day, then select **Record decline**. The unit shows **Declined**, counts as moving out, and can be pre-leased.

</Step>

</StepByStep>

After you select **Mark accepted**, the row shows **Record renewal**. See [Record a signed renewal](help:record-renewal).

## Month-to-month leases

Open the unit and select **Offer a fixed term** at the bottom of the panel. The dialog works the same way. The month-to-month tenancy ends the day before the new term starts.

<Figure shot="ren-9" alt="Unit panel footer with End tenancy, Transfer, Edit lease, and Offer a fixed term" />

## Holdovers

For a **Holdover** row, also select **Send offer**. Until you record something, the old lease’s terms still apply. If the resident is leaving, see [Record a move-out](help:move-out).

<Callout type="florida-statute" title="Florida Statute §83.575">

A fixed-term lease can require the resident to give notice before moving out at the end of the term, but no more than **60 days**. The unit panel shows each lease’s notice deadline. Send offers, and set expiry dates, before that deadline. A 90-day outer [alert window](help:expiry-alerts) helps.

</Callout>
