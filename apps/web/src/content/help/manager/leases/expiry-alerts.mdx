<StepByStep>

<Step title="Select Expiry alerts" shot="al-1">

It is above the summary tiles and shows the current setting.

</Step>

<Step title="Choose an option" shot="al-2">

**30, 60, 90 days** suits 12-month leases. **60, 90, 120 days** gives more lead time when leases require 60 days’ notice. **30, 60 days** suits short terms.

</Step>

<Step title="Select Apply for everyone" shot="al-3">

The setting applies to every manager of this community.

</Step>

</StepByStep>

<Figure shot="al-4" alt="Confirmation: Alert windows set to 60, 90, 120 days for everyone, with Undo">

A confirmation with **Undo** appears.

</Figure>

Managers get a reminder at each mark. Leases appear in **Renewals due** from the outer mark. End dates turn amber within 30 days and are flagged within 7.
