Documents holds the records your association maintains for the community — governing documents, budgets, meeting minutes, insurance, and more. Everyone in your community has read access; uploads and edits are admin-only.

## Open the document library

<StepByStep>
<Step title="Click Documents in the sidebar">
The library opens with the most recently posted documents at the top.
</Step>

<Step title="Use the category filter">
A horizontal pill bar above the list lets you filter by category: **All**, Governing Documents, Financial Records, **Meeting Records**, Contracts, and more. Click a pill to narrow the list.
</Step>

<Step title="Search by keyword">
Click **Search** to open the search panel. Search matches document titles, descriptions, and extracted text. For a fuzzier match across documents and announcements, use the main search in the top bar.
</Step>

<Step title="Open or download">
Click a row to preview the file inline. Use the Download icon on the row to save a copy locally.
</Step>
</StepByStep>

## Access levels

Who can see a document depends on its category and your role — PropertyPro applies category-based access policies automatically. If you expect to see a document that isn't visible, ask your community contact — admins can verify your role assignment and the document's category.

## If a file won't open

- Most documents are PDFs and should open in the browser preview.
- Files are limited to 50 MB at upload; preview should work for any file within that limit.
- If a specific file won't render, download it and open in your preferred PDF reader.

## What's next

- Admins: read [uploading documents](/help/documents/uploading-documents) for the upload flow and category selection.
- Residents: use Documents together with [meetings](/help/meetings/viewing-meetings-and-notices) to follow your community's record-keeping in context.
