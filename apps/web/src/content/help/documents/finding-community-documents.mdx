Documents holds the records your association maintains for the community — governing documents, budgets, meeting minutes, insurance, and more. Everyone in your community has read access; uploads and edits are admin-only.

## Open the document library

<StepByStep>
<Step title="Click Documents in the sidebar">
The library opens with the most recently posted documents at the top.
</Step>

<Step title="Use the category filter">
The left-side filter groups documents by category: Governing Documents, Financial Records, Meeting Materials, Contracts, and more. Click a category to narrow the list.
</Step>

<Step title="Search by filename or keyword">
The search bar at the top of the library matches filenames, descriptions, and uploaders. For a fuzzier match — "what did the budget say about landscaping?" — use the main search in the top bar, which searches across documents and announcements.
</Step>

<Step title="Open or download">
Click a row to preview the file inline. Use the Download icon on the row to save a copy locally.
</Step>
</StepByStep>

## The 30-day posting indicator

Each row shows when the document was posted relative to its statutory deadline. You'll see one of:

- A green check mark — posted on time
- A yellow timer — posted late but still satisfying the requirement
- A red flag — the requirement remains outstanding

<Callout type="tip" title="What the indicator tells you">
The indicator is mostly useful for admins managing compliance. As a resident, you can still view every file regardless of posting status — the icon just reflects your association's posting history.
</Callout>

## Pinned and featured documents

Your board or manager can pin certain documents to appear at the top of the library. Pinned items often include the current fiscal-year budget, the rules and regulations, and the most recent meeting minutes. Pinning is a quick way to find the "always-used" records without filtering.

## Access levels

Some documents may be marked **Admin only** or **Owners only** and won't appear in everyone's list. If you expect to see a document that isn't visible, ask your community contact — admins can verify your role assignment.

## If a file won't open

- Most documents are PDFs and should open in the browser preview.
- Large files (over 50 MB) may take a moment to preview; the Download button still works immediately.
- If a specific file won't render, download it and open in your preferred PDF reader.
