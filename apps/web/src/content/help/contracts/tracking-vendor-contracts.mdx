The contracts module is where every active and historical vendor agreement lives — landscaping, security, elevator service, insurance, management. Florida statute requires associations to post material vendor contracts; PropertyPro keeps them indexed and surfaces expirations before they bite.

<Callout type="florida-statute" title="Posting threshold">
Both §718.111(12)(g) (condos with 25+ units) and §720.303 (HOAs with 100+ parcels) require associations to make vendor contracts above the statutory dollar threshold available to members. Verify the current threshold with your attorney; statute amendments adjust it periodically.
</Callout>

## What belongs in contracts

- Vendor service agreements (landscaping, pool, security, elevator)
- Management contracts (with your management company)
- Insurance policies and certificates of insurance
- Banking and reserve-investment agreements
- Professional services (attorney, accountant, engineer)

Anything time-bounded with payment terms belongs here. One-off purchases (a single repair) do not — those live under the related work order.

## Logging a new contract

<StepByStep>
<Step title="Open Contracts">
Click Contracts in the sidebar. The list shows every contract sorted by next-action date.
</Step>

<Step title="New contract">
Click **New contract**. Pick the vendor from your directory or add a new one inline.
</Step>

<Step title="Enter the terms">
- **Effective date** — when the contract starts.
- **Expiration date** — end of the current term.
- **Renewal type** — auto-renew, manual renewal, or fixed.
- **Notice period** — how many days before expiration you must give notice to non-renew. This drives the alert window.
- **Amount** — total contract value or recurring amount.
</Step>

<Step title="Upload the signed PDF">
Attach the executed contract. PropertyPro stores it under Documents → Contracts automatically.
</Step>

<Step title="Mark statutorily public if applicable">
If the contract exceeds your statutory threshold, mark it for the transparency page so it auto-publishes to the public records section.
</Step>
</StepByStep>

## Expiration alerts

PropertyPro flags contracts as they approach their notice period:

- **Calm** — more than 90 days to expiration
- **Aware** — within the notice period but not yet inside the no-go window
- **Urgent** — past the notice period; renewal or non-renewal needs an immediate decision
- **Expired** — past expiration without recorded renewal

Work the Aware bucket every month. Once a contract goes Urgent, your options narrow — the contract may auto-renew at unfavorable terms if no action is taken.

## Renewal workflow

<StepByStep>
<Step title="Open the expiring contract">
The Contracts dashboard surfaces upcoming expirations.
</Step>

<Step title="Choose renew or non-renew">
- **Renew**: enter the renewed terms (often a price change) and upload the renewal addendum.
- **Non-renew**: send the non-renewal notice within the notice window. PropertyPro records the notice date.
</Step>

<Step title="If non-renewing, plan the transition">
Start an RFP for replacement vendors well before expiration. Use the bulk-document feature to circulate the RFP to candidates.
</Step>
</StepByStep>

## RFPs and bid comparison

For large contracts (management, insurance, elevator service), document the RFP process:

1. Upload the RFP document.
2. Track which vendors received it.
3. Attach each vendor's response.
4. Record the board's selection rationale in a meeting minute.

This creates an audit trail satisfying both transparency requirements and any future challenge to the board's selection.

## Insurance certificates

A common pattern: a vendor's main contract is multi-year, but their insurance certificate renews annually. Track them separately:

- **Contract record** — the underlying multi-year agreement.
- **Insurance attachment** — replaced each year. The contract page shows the certificate's expiration so a lapsed cert is visible at a glance.

<Callout type="warning" title="Lapsed insurance is high-risk">
A vendor working on association property without current insurance leaves the association exposed. PropertyPro flags lapsed insurance prominently in the vendor directory and on the contract record — don't dismiss those alerts.
</Callout>

## Public posting on the transparency page

For contracts above the statutory threshold, the public transparency page shows:

- Vendor name
- Contract type
- Effective and expiration dates
- A redacted PDF if any sensitive fields exist (rate cards, pricing schedules)

The full unredacted contract is available to members through the normal records-request process.

## Retention

Contracts are retained as long as the relationship is active and for the retention period afterward per Florida statute (typically 7 years for financial records). PropertyPro does not auto-delete — the audit history is preserved.

## Common pitfalls

- **Auto-renewal without notice review** — many vendors set aggressive auto-renew terms. Set the notice period correctly in PropertyPro so you get the alert in time.
- **Missing signatures** — if the signed copy isn't uploaded, the contract is unenforceable. Track the signature status on every record.
- **Verbal modifications** — any change to a contract must be in writing. Verbal agreements with vendors are not enforceable; route changes through a contract amendment.
