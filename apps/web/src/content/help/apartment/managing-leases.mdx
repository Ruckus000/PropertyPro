Lease management is an apartment-specific feature. Condos and HOAs don't typically track leases at the association level.

<FeatureGate feature="apartment communities only">
Lease tracking is available in communities configured as apartments. Condos and HOAs won't see this feature.
</FeatureGate>

## What the leases module tracks

For each unit that's under a lease:

- **Current tenant** — one resident per lease record
- **Lease term** — start date, end date (optional for month-to-month)
- **Rent** — monthly amount
- **Status** — Active, Expiring Soon (within 60 days), Expired, Renewed, Terminated, or Vacant (no active lease on the unit)

## Creating a lease record

<StepByStep>
<Step title="Open Leases">
Click Leases in the sidebar, then **New lease**.
</Step>

<Step title="Pick the unit">
Select the unit from the dropdown.
</Step>

<Step title="Select the resident">
Select the resident (one per lease record). If they aren't listed, add them on the Residents page first.
</Step>

<Step title="Enter lease details">
Fill in Start Date, End Date (optional — leave blank for month-to-month), Monthly Rent, and Notes.
</Step>

<Step title="Save">
The lease becomes active as of the start date.
</Step>
</StepByStep>

## Lease lifecycle

Leases use these statuses:

- **Active** — currently in term
- **Expiring Soon** — within 60 days of the end date
- **Expired** — past end date
- **Renewed** — replaced by a renewal lease
- **Terminated** — early termination recorded
- **Vacant** — unit has no active lease (shown on the Leases list)

## Renewals

<StepByStep>
<Step title="Open the expiring lease">
Leases entering the expiring phase appear in a dashboard card or the Expiring Soon filter.
</Step>

<Step title="Click Renew">
Click **Renew** on the lease row to open the **Renew Lease** dialog.
</Step>

<Step title="Enter new terms">
Set New Start Date, New End Date (optional — leave blank for month-to-month), and Monthly Rent.
</Step>

<Step title="Confirm">
Click **Renew Lease**. The current lease is marked **Renewed** immediately and a new lease record is created.
</Step>
</StepByStep>

## Terminations

Terminations can happen mid-lease for various reasons (mutual agreement, breach, buyout). To record:

1. Open the active lease.
2. Click **Terminate**.
3. Enter the termination date and notes.

The tenant's access to the community ends on the termination date (or the move-out date, whichever you prefer).

## Staff view

Staff see every lease for the community in the Leases list. Lease data is sensitive — rent amounts and legal agreements — and access is gated accordingly.

## Compliance

<Callout type="warning" title="Florida landlord-tenant law applies">
Florida Statutes Chapter 83 governs residential leases. PropertyPro records the agreement but does not enforce statutory requirements (notice periods, security deposit handling, habitability). Work with your legal counsel to ensure lease terms comply with Chapter 83.
</Callout>

## Common patterns

- **Month-to-month after expiration** — leave End Date blank; the lease appears in the Month-to-Month filter.
- **Rent escalations** — record the new rent on renewal. Until the renewed lease is active, the prior rent applies.
