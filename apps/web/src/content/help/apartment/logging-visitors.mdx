Visitor logging keeps an auditable record of who entered the community — useful for security investigations, insurance claims, and resolving disputes between residents and their guests.

<FeatureGate feature="apartment and condo communities">
Visitor logging is part of the apartment and condo concierge toolkit. HOAs typically don't run centralized guest logs.
</FeatureGate>

## Who gets logged

Every non-resident entering the community:

- **Pre-registered passes** — residents or staff create a pass with guest type, host unit, and a passcode or QR code
- **Service personnel** — contractors, delivery beyond packages, cleaning services
- **Revoked passes** — use **Revoke Pass** with a required reason instead of checking someone in

Pass statuses include expected, checked in, checked out, expired, overstayed, revoked, and revoked on site.

## Checking in a visitor

<StepByStep>
<Step title="Open Visitors → Register Visitor">
From the Visitors page, click **Register Visitor** (or use a pass a resident already created).
</Step>

<Step title="Set guest type and host unit">
Choose guest type (One-Time, Recurring, Vendor, or Permanent), enter visitor name, purpose, and host unit.
</Step>

<Step title="Check In">
For an expected pass, click **Check In**. If the visitor matches the denied-visitor list, PropertyPro shows a warning before you proceed.
</Step>

<Step title="Check Out or Revoke Pass">
When the visit ends, **Check Out** the pass. To deny entry or cancel a pass, use **Revoke Pass** and enter a required reason.
</Step>
</StepByStep>

## Resident self-registration

Residents can register their own visitors — including recurring and permanent passes — and share a passcode or QR code. Staff can also register visitors with **Register Visitor** when a guest arrives without a pre-created pass.

## Denied entry

<Callout type="warning" title="Document every denial">
If you deny entry, add notes on the visitor record and **Revoke Pass** with a specific reason. Denials without documentation can look arbitrary later if the visitor complains.
</Callout>

## Denied-visitor list

Communities can maintain a denied-visitor list — individuals who are never to be admitted (domestic incidents, prior trespass, court order):

- Added by staff only
- Checked automatically against every incoming visitor at check-in
- Entries are private to staff, not visible to other residents

## Resident view

Residents see their own visitor history:

- Who visited (name)
- When
- Pass status

They don't see visitors who came for other residents.

## Privacy

Visitor data is sensitive. Policy guidelines:

- **Don't share** visitor history outside staff, the resident, and law-enforcement requests with a warrant
- **Retention** — follow your community's retention policy (typically 12-24 months for routine entries)
- **Incidents** — preserve any visit records tied to an ongoing incident until resolved

## Emergency situations

If a visitor becomes threatening or refuses to leave:

1. Call 911 if there's any safety concern.
2. Add notes on the visitor record and **Revoke Pass** for follow-up.
3. Consider adding them to the denied-visitor list after the fact.

## Staffing patterns

- **Peak hours** — weekends, evenings, holiday periods. Staff accordingly.
- **Off-hours** — if the front desk is unstaffed, consider a keypad or intercom system that logs entry even without staff on site.
- **Events** — for large resident gatherings (holiday parties), have residents pre-register guests or create recurring passes in advance.
