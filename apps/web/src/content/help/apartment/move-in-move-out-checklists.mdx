Move-in and move-out checklists track lease onboarding and offboarding tasks. Checklists are created automatically when a lease is created (move-in) or terminated (move-out).

<FeatureGate feature="apartment communities only">
Move-in/move-out checklists are an apartment-community feature. Condos and HOAs don't track unit condition at this level.
</FeatureGate>

## What a checklist captures

Each checklist has eight fixed task steps with a completed flag and optional notes. A progress bar shows how many steps are done.

**Move-in steps:** Lease signed, Security deposit recorded, Move-in inspection scheduled, Keys/access cards assigned, Parking assigned, Resident portal account created, Welcome packet sent, Utilities transfer confirmed.

**Move-out steps:** 30-day notice received, Move-out inspection scheduled, Move-out inspection completed, Security deposit disposition calculated, Keys/access cards returned, Parking assignment cleared, Portal access deactivated, Security deposit refund processed.

## Running a move-in checklist

<StepByStep>
<Step title="Open the checklist">
When a lease is created, a move-in checklist appears on the Move In/Out page. Open the card to view it.
</Step>

<Step title="Complete each step">
Mark each step complete and add notes where helpful.
</Step>

<Step title="Finish when all steps are done">
Completion means marking all steps complete. There is no electronic sign-off in the app.
</Step>
</StepByStep>

## Move-out checklist

When a lease is terminated, a move-out checklist is created automatically. It is independent of the move-in checklist — there is no side-by-side comparison in the app.

<StepByStep>
<Step title="Open the move-out checklist">
Find it on the Move In/Out page after the lease is terminated.
</Step>

<Step title="Complete each step">
Mark steps complete and add notes as you work through move-out tasks.
</Step>
</StepByStep>

## Normal wear vs. damage

<Callout type="warning" title="Know the distinction">
Florida law distinguishes normal wear (not deductible) from damage (deductible). Small nail holes, minor carpet wear, and faded paint generally fall on the "wear" side. Large holes, pet stains, and broken fixtures fall on "damage." Train staff to mark conditions honestly — over-claiming damage leads to deposit disputes you'll lose.
</Callout>

## Security deposit handling

<Callout type="florida-statute" title="Chapter 83 deposit timelines">
Under Chapter 83 of Florida Statutes, you have specific windows to return or itemize a deposit after the tenant vacates. Missing the window can result in forfeiting your right to claim any deductions. Consult your attorney for the exact timelines — they depend on whether the deposit is held in an interest-bearing account and whether the tenant gives written notice.
</Callout>

## Disputes

If a tenant disputes move-out findings:

1. Review the move-out checklist notes and any off-platform documentation you kept.
2. If the dispute escalates, consult your attorney.

## Common mistakes

- **Rushing the move-in checklist** — leads to missed steps at move-out.
- **Assuming checklists compare automatically** — move-out is a separate checklist; keep your own records if you need a before/after reference.
