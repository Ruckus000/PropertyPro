The board forum is a private space for board members and approved staff to discuss community topics asynchronously, between formal meetings. It's not a substitute for meetings — but it helps preparation go faster.

<Callout type="warning" title="Open-meeting laws apply">
Florida's open-meeting rules generally prohibit the board from taking substantive action via private communication that would otherwise require a meeting. The forum is fine for information-gathering and discussion; it is **not** a place to vote or make binding decisions. Keep decisions for meetings with proper notice.
</Callout>

## Who can see the forum

- **Owners and tenants** — can read and post threads.
- **Board members, CAM, and site managers** — can read and post.
- **Admins** — can moderate (pin, lock, and delete threads and replies). Only admins moderate.
- **Platform admins** — have audit access for support purposes but don't participate.

## Creating a thread

<StepByStep>
<Step title="Open Board → Forum">
From the sidebar, click Board, then the Forum tab.
</Step>

<Step title="New thread">
Click **New thread**. Enter a clear **Title** and **Body**.
</Step>

<Step title="Write the opening post">
Frame the question or topic. Keep it focused — long meandering opening posts discourage engagement.
</Step>

<Step title="Publish">
Click **Create Thread**. The thread becomes visible to all forum participants.
</Step>
</StepByStep>

## Replying to a thread

Click any reply box. Replies are plain text (8,000-character limit).

## Deleting replies

You can delete replies you wrote. Forum moderators can delete any reply when a thread needs cleanup or sensitive content is posted in the wrong place.

Deleted replies are hidden from the conversation and replaced with a placeholder so later readers can still follow the thread. Deletion is recorded in the audit trail and does not erase the association's record-retention obligations.

## Pinning threads

Important ongoing topics — annual meeting planning, open audit issues — can be pinned to the top of the forum. Unpin when the topic is resolved.

## What belongs in the forum

- **Context gathering** — "What do we know about the pool deck?"
- **Pre-meeting prep** — "Here's the vendor quote summary for next week's meeting."
- **Policy questions** — "Has anyone seen this issue before?"
- **Vendor coordination** — scheduling, quote comparisons.

## What doesn't belong

- **Votes or decisions** — must happen at a meeting.
- **Substantive debate on decisions** — per open-meeting rules, hash it out at the meeting.
- **Attorney-client privileged content** — use a secure channel directly with the attorney.
- **Personal disputes or HR matters** — use the right channel with appropriate confidentiality.
- **Gossip about residents** — can be discoverable; keep it professional.

## Record retention

Forum posts are part of the community's records for as long as the association exists. They may be discoverable in litigation. Write every post as though it might be read aloud in court — because occasionally it is.

## Etiquette

- **Respond within a few business days** — silence on a thread delays everyone.
- **Summarize long threads** before a meeting — members skimming in advance need a summary.
- **Don't vent** — if you're frustrated, cool off before posting.
- **Summarize resolved threads** — post a final summary reply so future readers can skim the outcome without reading every reply.

## Searching past discussions

Forum search isn't available yet. Scan pinned threads before starting a new topic — often the subject has been discussed and the prior resolution applies.
