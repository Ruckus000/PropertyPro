The reserve register is a plain, factual list of your association's major components — roof, elevators, pool, paving, mechanical systems — with the year each was installed and its expected useful life. Owners see the register and a remaining-useful-life countdown for each component.

<Callout type="info" title="Facts only, not a reserve study">
This register shows the data the association entered. It is **not** an assessment of reserve adequacy or a reserve study, and PropertyPro does not provide engineering, financial, or legal advice.
</Callout>

## Add a component

1. Go to **Reserves** in the sidebar and choose **Add Asset**.
2. Enter the **component name** and pick a **category**.
3. Enter the **year installed** and the **expected useful life** in years.
4. Optionally add the **replacement cost** and the **amount currently reserved**.

Owners see the component immediately, with a remaining-useful-life badge.

## How the countdown works

Remaining useful life is a simple calculation: the install year plus the expected useful life, compared to the current year. It reflects only the numbers you entered — not the component's actual condition, and not whether reserves are adequate.

| Badge | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Years left | Whole years until the entered useful life elapses |
| Due this year | The entered useful life ends this year |
| Past expected life | The entered useful life has elapsed |

## Keep it factual

Enter facts about the component only. Do not enter personal information about owners or residents in the notes field. When you replace a component, update its install year so the countdown resets.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is this a reserve study?**
No. It is a register of the data you entered. It does not replace a reserve study, a structural integrity reserve study (SIRS), or a milestone inspection.

**Does the countdown say whether we've saved enough?**
No. It is a neutral time-remaining figure and makes no judgment about funding or adequacy.
