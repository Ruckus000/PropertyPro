Once you submit a request, you can check its status on the Maintenance page or in Operations (Requests tab). You don't need to call to check in.

## The request lifecycle

Requests move through these statuses, roughly in order:

- **Submitted** — received, waiting in the triage queue.
- **Acknowledged** — staff have seen it and are assigning it.
- **In progress** — work is underway (in-house or with a vendor).
- **Resolved** — the work is complete from the staff's side.
- **Closed** — final state after confirmation.

You'll get an email on every status change.

## Checking your requests

On web, open **Operations** (Requests tab) or **Maintenance**. On mobile, open **Maintenance**.

Each request appears as a card showing its title and status badge. There is no detail page, timeline, comment box, or way to upload additional photos after submitting.

Email notifications carry status updates from staff.

## If a resolved request isn't really fixed

PropertyPro does not let residents reopen a resolved request or add comments after submission. If staff marked something Resolved but the issue continues, submit a new request describing what's still wrong.

Staff can move a request from **Resolved** to **Closed**, but not back to **In progress**.

## If you see no updates

<Callout type="tip" title="Silent doesn't mean forgotten">
Some updates happen behind the scenes — a vendor being contacted, parts being ordered. If you haven't seen movement in a few days for a non-emergency request, contact your management team.
</Callout>

## Urgent vs. routine handling

High- and Urgent-priority requests should get faster attention from your management team. Routine (Normal, Low) items follow your community's scheduling process — PropertyPro does not queue them automatically. Setting priority honestly helps everyone.

## What staff see

Staff see the same request list you do (title, description, status badge) plus internal notes on their admin views. Email notifications go to residents when status changes.
