When a resident submits a maintenance request, staff triage it and dispatch work. Work orders are created separately in Operations — they do not convert from requests and do not inherit request details automatically.

## The Operations inbox

Open **Operations** from the sidebar (the legacy `/maintenance/inbox` route redirects here). Tabs are **All**, **Requests**, **Work Orders**, and **Reservations**.

The **Requests** tab shows cards with:

- Title
- Description
- Status badge

Rows are not clickable — there is no request detail page. Filter by status or priority using the URL parameters (`?status=...&priority=...`).

## Dispatching a work order

<StepByStep>
<Step title="Open Dispatch Work Order">
From Operations, click **Dispatch Work Order**.
</Step>

<Step title="Enter work details">
Fill in **Title**, **Description**, **Priority**, and optionally **Unit ID**. Work orders are created standalone — re-enter details from the resident request manually.
</Step>

<Step title="Assign a vendor (optional)">
Pick a vendor from your directory, or leave unassigned for in-house staff.
</Step>

<Step title="Set SLA and notes">
Optionally enter **SLA Response (hrs)**, **SLA Completion (hrs)**, and internal **Notes**.
</Step>

<Step title="Dispatch">
Click **Dispatch Work Order** to create the work order.
</Step>
</StepByStep>

## Vendor directory

PropertyPro lets you maintain a vendor directory with:

- Name and company
- Phone and email
- Specialties
- Active or inactive status

Vendors do not receive email from PropertyPro and have no vendor login. Coordinate with vendors outside the app — by phone, email, or your usual dispatch process.

## Priority and SLA

Each priority level can have target response windows — your community sets the policy. SLA hours are recorded on the work order for reference. PropertyPro does not surface SLA breaches on a dashboard.

## Completing work

After verifying the work offline, staff mark the work order complete from Operations. There is no vendor completion flow, resident confirmation step, or way to transfer a work order to another vendor in PropertyPro.

## Working with the resident

Residents see their requests in a list (title and status badge) and receive email notifications on status changes. There is no resident-facing comment thread on requests.

## Common patterns

- **Recurring vendor issues** — same vendor, same problem repeating. Demote them to back-up status or replace.
- **Resident escalation** — when a resident calls instead of submitting a request, log the outcome in your internal notes on the work order.
- **Emergency dispatch** — for Urgent items outside business hours, use your on-call vendor list outside PropertyPro.
