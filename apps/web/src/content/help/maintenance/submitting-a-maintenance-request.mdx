## Describe the issue clearly

When you create a maintenance request, include the location, the problem you are seeing, and any details that help a manager or vendor understand urgency.

## Add photos when they help

Photos are especially helpful for leaks, visible damage, or recurring issues. They reduce back-and-forth and give staff more context before they arrive.

## Track status updates

After you submit a request, return to Maintenance to see whether it is new, in progress, waiting on a vendor, or resolved.
