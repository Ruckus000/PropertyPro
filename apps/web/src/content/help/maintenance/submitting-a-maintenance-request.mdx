A maintenance request is how you tell your management team about something that needs fixing — a leak, a broken fixture, a common-area issue. The better the details, the faster it gets resolved.

## When to submit

Submit a request when something tangible in your unit or a common area needs attention. Examples:

- A plumbing leak, running toilet, or water damage
- A broken door lock, intercom, or package locker
- Elevator, gate, or HVAC issues in common areas
- Landscaping or pest issues that are beyond what a resident should handle

<Callout type="warning" title="Emergencies">
For a gas leak, flood, fire, or safety threat, call 911 and your association's emergency contact first. Submit a request afterward for the record — don't wait for the ticket to be seen.
</Callout>

## How to submit

<StepByStep>
<Step title="Open Maintenance">
Click Maintenance in the sidebar, then **New request** (or **Submit request** on mobile).
</Step>

<Step title="Pick a category">
Choose the category that best fits — Plumbing, Electrical, HVAC, Appliance, Common Area, Pest, Other. Categories route to the right vendor or staff member.
</Step>

<Step title="Describe the issue">
Include the location (which room, which common area), what you're seeing, and when it started. For example: "Leak under kitchen sink, dripping steadily since this morning, about a cup of water an hour."
</Step>

<Step title="Add photos">
Attach photos whenever possible. A single photo often saves a diagnostic visit. Photos are especially helpful for leaks, visible damage, and recurring issues.
</Step>

<Step title="Set urgency honestly">
- **Emergency** — active damage or safety issue (should be paired with a phone call)
- **Urgent** — needs attention within 24 hours
- **Normal** — can be scheduled in the next week

The category and urgency together determine how quickly staff see and act on the request.
</Step>

<Step title="Submit">
Click Submit. You'll see a confirmation and a request ID. Any staff or vendor updates appear on the same page.
</Step>
</StepByStep>

## What happens next

Your request moves through these statuses:

- **New** — submitted, awaiting triage
- **In Progress** — assigned and being worked on
- **Waiting on vendor** — out for an outside quote or visit
- **Resolved** — work completed; you can confirm or reopen
- **Closed** — final confirmation; no further action

You'll get an email when the status changes. You can also visit Maintenance any time to see the current state.

## Adding information later

If you notice something new after submitting — the leak got worse, you took better photos — open the request and add a comment. Comments are append-only, so the history is preserved for everyone.

<Callout type="tip" title="Help the technician">
Tell staff what access they'll need: can they enter when you're not home? Is there a key fob at the front desk? The faster they can get in, the faster you have working plumbing again.
</Callout>

## What this isn't

Maintenance is for things your association or a vendor should fix. It isn't the right place for:

- Rule violations by other residents — use Report Violation instead.
- Payment questions — use Contact Management.
- Architectural changes you're requesting — use the ARC / ACC submission flow.
