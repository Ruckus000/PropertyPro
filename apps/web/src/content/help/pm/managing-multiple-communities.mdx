Property managers often oversee a dozen or more communities at once. PropertyPro's PM dashboard keeps portfolio-level work in one place while still letting you drop into any single community's full interface.

## The PM dashboard

Open `/pm/dashboard/communities` to see every community in your portfolio. The table shows:

- **Community** — name with type badge (Condo, HOA, or Apartment)
- **Units**
- **Residents**
- **Occupancy**
- **Open Maintenance**
- **Compliance %**
- **Outstanding Balance**
- **Site** — public-site status (Customized, Draft saved, or Default)

Sort any column to prioritize communities that need attention.

## Switching into a community

<StepByStep>
<Step title="Click a community">
From the PM dashboard, clicking any community drops you into that community's full dashboard — exactly what a local admin would see.
</Step>

<Step title="Work in context">
Every page you visit is now scoped to that community. URLs use `/dashboard?communityId=N` on the same host; data (documents, residents, meetings) is that community's data.
</Step>

<Step title="Return to the portfolio">
Click the **Portfolio** breadcrumb link to return to the multi-community view. Your session remains authenticated across the portfolio.
</Step>
</StepByStep>

## Visual cues you're in PM mode

When you're working at the portfolio level:

- The breadcrumb shows **Portfolio** linking back to the communities list.
- Navigation shows **Communities**, **Website**, **Templates**, and **Reports**.

When you're inside a specific community:

- The community's name appears in the top bar.
- The community's branding (colors, logo) applies if configured.
- Navigation matches what local admins see.

## Adding a community

To add a new community to your portfolio, click **Add Community** on the dashboard. The modal collects:

1. Community name
2. Plan (Essentials, Professional, or Operations Plus)
3. Community type (condo / HOA / apartment)
4. Address (line, city, state, zip)
5. Subdomain
6. Unit count
7. Timezone

After you submit, embedded Stripe checkout completes payment. The new community is provisioned within a few minutes. Invite board members and CAMs via **Residents → Add Resident**.

## Search scope

Inside a community, the top-bar search is scoped to that community's data. When you need to compare across communities, run a portfolio report (see [Running portfolio reports](/help/pm/running-portfolio-reports)) — it aggregates data where the search bar doesn't.

## Permission model

<Callout type="tip" title="Property managers get full admin access in their communities">
Once you add a community to your portfolio, you have full admin powers there. Exercise them carefully — board members expect CAMs and property managers to respect governance boundaries.
</Callout>

## Watching the portfolio score

The portfolio dashboard shows an overall compliance score — the weighted average across your communities. Dips usually reflect one community slipping rather than a broad decline. Use the per-community breakdown to find the source.

## Handoffs

When a community leaves your portfolio (change of management company, owner takeover):

1. Archive your PM-specific records (branding, bulk announcements).
2. Verify the new management is added as admin before you lose access.
3. Remove yourself only after the new team confirms they're set up.

The community's data (documents, residents, records) stays with the community — it doesn't belong to your management company.
