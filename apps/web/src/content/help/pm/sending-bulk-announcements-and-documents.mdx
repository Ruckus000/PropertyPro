When the same thing needs to happen in many communities — a management-company-wide announcement, a new insurance certificate, a policy update — the bulk operations save hours of clicking.

## Bulk announcements

<StepByStep>
<Step title="Open the PM dashboard → Bulk announcements">
Available only to property-manager-admin roles.
</Step>

<Step title="Compose the announcement">
Write the headline and body as you would for a single community. You can include placeholders — `{{community.name}}`, `{{community.manager}}` — that expand per community.
</Step>

<Step title="Select communities">
Check each community that should receive the announcement. For "all of them," click the Select all toggle.
</Step>

<Step title="Preview one">
Click Preview to see the announcement as it will appear in the first selected community. Confirm placeholders resolved correctly.
</Step>

<Step title="Publish">
PropertyPro creates one announcement in each selected community with the right context. Residents see their community's version on their dashboards and in email.
</Step>
</StepByStep>

<Callout type="warning" title="Bulk announcements are visible immediately">
There's no scheduled send for bulk operations today. Double-check the content before publishing — editing a single community's copy is possible, but undoing a multi-community publish takes coordinated effort.
</Callout>

## Bulk document uploads

For documents that apply across the portfolio — your management company's service agreement, shared insurance certificate, policies — upload once to multiple communities:

<StepByStep>
<Step title="Bulk documents tool">
From the PM dashboard, open Bulk documents.
</Step>

<Step title="Upload the file">
Pick the PDF. The file uploads once; PropertyPro replicates the reference across communities.
</Step>

<Step title="Set category and date">
The category (e.g., Contracts, Insurance) and finalization date apply to every copy.
</Step>

<Step title="Select target communities">
Same picker as bulk announcements. Choose the communities that should have this document.
</Step>

<Step title="Publish">
Each community's document library gets the new record; residents see it in their library.
</Step>
</StepByStep>

## What not to do in bulk

- **Community-specific content** — if the message only applies to one community, post it there natively. Bulk announcements should be genuinely portfolio-wide.
- **Private records** — don't bulk-upload a single community's meeting minutes; keep those in that community's library.
- **Anything requiring customization beyond placeholders** — some communications need hand-crafted language per community (disputes, sensitive topics). Write those individually.

## Cadence

Most PM teams use bulk operations monthly or quarterly — for a company-wide policy update, an annual insurance refresh, a seasonal reminder (hurricane prep, winter maintenance). Use sparingly; residents can tell when a message is templated and may disengage if every communication feels impersonal.

## Tracking

The PM dashboard keeps a log of every bulk operation — who sent it, when, which communities were targeted, how many messages were sent per community. Use this for your own records and for any management committee reporting.

## Undoing a bulk send

You can't undo a bulk announcement in one click. To retract:

1. Open the community list you targeted.
2. Unpublish the announcement in each community.
3. Send a follow-up explaining the retraction.

Because retractions are painful, pause and preview twice before every bulk publish.
