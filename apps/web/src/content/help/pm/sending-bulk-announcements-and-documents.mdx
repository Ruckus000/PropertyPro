When the same thing needs to happen in many communities — a management-company-wide announcement, a new insurance certificate, a policy update — bulk operations save hours of clicking.

<Callout type="info" title="Not available in the UI yet">
Bulk announcements and bulk document uploads are not reachable from the PM dashboard today. Until the feature is wired in, post announcements and upload documents per community using the standard community tools.
</Callout>

## Bulk announcements (planned)

When bulk announcements ship, the flow will work like this:

<StepByStep>
<Step title="Compose the announcement">
Enter a **Title** and **Body**. Choose an **Audience** (All Members, Owners Only, Board Only, or Tenants Only). Optionally pin the announcement. The same literal text goes to every selected community — there are no per-community placeholders.
</Step>

<Step title="Select communities">
Check each community that should receive the announcement.
</Step>

<Step title="Review and send">
Click **Review & Send**, confirm the target community list, then click **Confirm & Send**. PropertyPro creates one announcement in each selected community.
</Step>
</StepByStep>

<Callout type="warning" title="Bulk announcements are visible immediately">
There's no scheduled send for bulk operations today. Double-check the content before publishing — editing a single community's copy is possible, but undoing a multi-community publish takes coordinated effort.
</Callout>

## Bulk document uploads (planned)

For documents that apply across the portfolio — your management company's service agreement, shared insurance certificate, policies:

<StepByStep>
<Step title="Select files">
Choose one or more files to upload.
</Step>

<Step title="Add an optional description">
Enter a **Description (optional)** if you want a note on the upload.
</Step>

<Step title="Select target communities">
Choose the communities that should receive the document.
</Step>

<Step title="Confirm and upload">
Click **Confirm & Upload**. Each community's document library gets the new record. Set the document category per community afterward — bulk upload does not assign a category.
</Step>
</StepByStep>

## What not to do in bulk

- **Community-specific content** — if the message only applies to one community, post it there natively. Bulk announcements should be genuinely portfolio-wide.
- **Private records** — don't bulk-upload a single community's meeting minutes; keep those in that community's library.
- **Anything requiring per-community wording** — some communications need hand-crafted language per community (disputes, sensitive topics). Write those individually.

## Cadence

Most PM teams use bulk operations monthly or quarterly — for a company-wide policy update, an annual insurance refresh, a seasonal reminder (hurricane prep, winter maintenance). Use sparingly; residents can tell when a message is templated and may disengage if every communication feels impersonal.

## Undoing a bulk send

You can't undo a bulk announcement in one click. To retract:

1. Open the community list you targeted.
2. Archive or delete the announcement in each community.
3. Send a follow-up explaining the retraction.

Because retractions are painful, double-check content carefully before every bulk publish.
