Admin roles in PropertyPro have real consequences — admins can post statutorily required records, run elections, and access sensitive resident data. Granting access carefully and offboarding cleanly is part of the management responsibility.

## Admin role tiers

Five role tiers carry admin capabilities:

- **`board_president`** — full community admin including governance authority (close elections, sign meeting minutes electronically).
- **`board_member`** — same as president without governance-specific actions.
- **`cam`** — community association manager. Day-to-day operations, document posting, maintenance triage.
- **`site_manager`** — on-site staff. Visitor logging, package handling, maintenance dispatch.
- **`property_manager_admin`** — portfolio-level admin (PMs and their staff).
- **`pm_admin`** — alias for property_manager_admin.

Each role has a defined permission set codified in `packages/shared/src/manager-permissions.ts`.

## Adding a CAM or board admin

<StepByStep>
<Step title="Open Add Resident">
On the community's **Residents** page, click **Add Resident** and enter the person's name, email, and phone.
</Step>

<Step title="Pick the admin role">
In the **Role** dropdown, choose the admin role directly — **Community Association Manager**, **Board Member**, **Board President**, or **Site Manager**. The role is set as you add them. Keep **Send invitation email** checked so they get portal access.
</Step>

<Step title="Confirm immediately">
Once they accept the invitation, the new admin sees admin features on their next page load. Walk them through the dashboard to verify they have what they need (and only what they need).
</Step>
</StepByStep>

<Callout type="info" title="Setting a role vs. changing it later">
You choose a member's role when you add them. PropertyPro doesn't yet offer an in-app control to change or remove an existing member's role from the resident list — that capability is on the roadmap. For now, assign the correct role when you add someone; anywhere this guide describes promoting, demoting, or removing an existing member, that change currently goes through PropertyPro support.
</Callout>

For property-manager admins (your own team), use the PM dashboard's team-management area rather than the per-community resident list — that grants portfolio-level access in one step rather than per-community.

## Multi-community admins

A CAM often covers several communities. Add them to each:

- For 2-3 communities, invite and promote per community.
- For 5+ communities, add them as a property_manager_admin so they get portfolio access in one assignment. Use community-specific role caps in your management agreement to constrain what they can do per community if needed.

<Callout type="tip" title="Least privilege">
Don't grant property_manager_admin to a junior CAM "just to make access easier." That role can change billing, modify branding, and onboard new communities. Use community-scoped roles for staff whose scope is intentionally limited.
</Callout>

## Onboarding a new board after an election

When a board changes hands after an election:

<StepByStep>
<Step title="Promote the new board">
For each new board member, change their role from owner (or tenant) to board_member. Promote the president to board_president.
</Step>

<Step title="Demote the outgoing board">
For each outgoing member who's no longer on the board, change their role back to owner (or tenant). Their admin features disappear immediately.
</Step>

<Step title="Notify outgoing members">
Send a courteous note thanking them for their service and explaining their admin access has ended. They keep their resident access unchanged.
</Step>

<Step title="Update auto-generated content">
The transparency page, meeting templates, and any communications referencing the board automatically reflect the new roster. Verify with a preview.
</Step>
</StepByStep>

## Offboarding a CAM

When a CAM leaves your company or moves to a different account:

<StepByStep>
<Step title="Audit their recent actions">
Before removing access, open the [audit trail](/help/audit/reviewing-the-audit-trail) and review the CAM's recent activity. Anything in progress (open work orders, draft announcements) needs handoff.
</Step>

<Step title="Reassign in-progress work">
Reassign open work orders, transfer ownership of draft documents, and update any community contacts that pointed to this CAM.
</Step>

<Step title="Remove their admin access">
To revoke a departing CAM's access, contact PropertyPro support to change their role or remove them from the community (existing-member role changes aren't self-serve yet — see the note above). Their PropertyPro account stays (they can use it elsewhere); their access to your portfolio ends.
</Step>

<Step title="Communicate the change">
Inform the board and post a community announcement introducing the replacement CAM.
</Step>
</StepByStep>

## Permission audit cadence

<Callout type="warning" title="Stale admin access is a security risk">
Admins who shouldn't have access anymore are a primary cause of data leaks and unintended actions. Audit admin roles quarterly.
</Callout>

Every quarter:

1. List the admins in each community (open the community's **Residents** page and note who holds an admin role — CAM, board member, board president, or site manager).
2. Verify each one is still in their role (CAM still works on this account; board member still on the board).
3. Demote or remove anyone whose access is outdated.

The audit takes 30 minutes for a 10-community portfolio and prevents the worst-case "former employee deleted records" scenario.

## Two-factor authentication

Admin accounts should have 2FA enabled. PropertyPro supports authenticator apps (Authy, Google Authenticator) and SMS codes. Encourage every admin to enable 2FA during their first session; the prompts appear in their account settings.

For communities subject to higher-security regimes (HOAs handling material reserves, condos in litigation), make 2FA mandatory for admins via your management policy.

## Cross-community admin patterns

Some admins are intentionally cross-community:

- **Your CAM team** — typically property_manager_admin at the portfolio level.
- **Association attorney** — usually granted admin temporarily for litigation reviews, then removed.
- **Auditors** — granted read-only board_member access during audit windows, then demoted.

Track grants and revocations in the audit trail. For temporary grants, set a calendar reminder to revoke when the engagement ends — temporary access easily becomes permanent if no one remembers.

## Permission troubleshooting

If an admin reports they can't do something they should:

1. Confirm their role on their profile.
2. Check the resource permission matrix (`packages/shared/src/manager-permissions.ts`) — they may genuinely lack permission.
3. If they should have it, escalate to support; a stuck role assignment occasionally needs a manual fix.

If an admin can do something they shouldn't:

1. Demote immediately.
2. Audit their recent actions.
3. Investigate how they were granted that role.
4. Strengthen the policy or training that allowed the mistake.

## Common patterns

- **CAM rotation** — a regular pattern in larger companies. Document the handoff cleanly each time so the next rotation is faster.
- **Board member resignation** — sometimes mid-term. Demote immediately upon resignation acceptance, not at next election.
- **Vendor needing temporary access** — rare. Grant the narrowest possible role (often just a non-admin role) and revoke promptly when the project ends.
- **Shadow account problems** — when an admin uses a personal email instead of their work email, role hygiene gets messy. Require work emails for admin accounts.
