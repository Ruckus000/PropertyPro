Portfolio reports aggregate data across the communities you manage. They're the fastest way to prepare for a monthly owner review, a quarterly management committee, or your own performance tracking.

## Available reports

Open `/pm/reports` to see the available report types:

- **Compliance** — compliance status and score per community, with the gaps that need attention.
- **Maintenance** — maintenance request and work-order volume across your communities.
- **Occupancy** — occupancy trends over the selected date range.
- **Violations** — a summary of violations per community.
- **Delinquency** — delinquency aging: who's behind on assessments, and by how long.

Each report type has the same shape: you pick the date range and the communities to include.

## Generating a report

<StepByStep>
<Step title="Pick a report type">
Click the report type on the Reports index.
</Step>

<Step title="Configure">
Choose the date range and which communities to include. By default, all of your communities are selected.
</Step>

<Step title="Generate">
Click Generate. Most reports render in a few seconds. Very large portfolios or long date ranges may take longer.
</Step>

<Step title="Export">
Each generated report has a download button — usually PDF and CSV. The CSV is handy for further analysis in Excel; the PDF is ready to share.
</Step>
</StepByStep>

## Example uses

- **Monthly management report** — maintenance + delinquency reports for your management committee.
- **Owner transparency** — the compliance report for communities where owners are asking for accountability data.
- **Collections review** — the delinquency report to see who needs follow-up across the portfolio.
- **Year-end review** — all five reports across the full fiscal year.

## Going deeper

When a report row surfaces something worth investigating, open the community directly from the PM dashboard to get into its detail pages — compliance, maintenance inbox, residents. The report tells you where to look; the community dashboard is where you dig in.

## Re-running with the same settings

For frequently-used configurations (monthly compliance review, quarterly delinquency recap), bookmark the URL after you generate the report — PropertyPro encodes the filters in the URL so the bookmark reproduces the same view next time.

## Limits

- **Data freshness**: reports reflect the state when you generated them. Re-run for fresh data; PropertyPro does not live-refresh a rendered report.
- **Historic reach**: reports go back as far as PropertyPro has data for each community, which is usually the community's onboarding date.
- **Custom fields**: if your portfolio tracks data beyond PropertyPro's schema, export the CSV and add context manually.

## Cadence

- **Weekly**: the maintenance report — catch maintenance backlogs early.
- **Monthly**: compliance + delinquency reports — material for your management committee.
- **Quarterly**: full portfolio review — spot trends before they become problems.
- **Annually**: year-over-year comparison — useful for proposing changes.

<Callout type="tip" title="Share wisely">
Reports contain sensitive operational data. When sharing externally, redact specific resident names and financial details unless the recipient already has a right to see them.
</Callout>
