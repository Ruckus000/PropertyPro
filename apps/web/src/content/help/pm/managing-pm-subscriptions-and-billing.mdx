PropertyPro bills per community. As a property manager, you typically manage billing centrally — you (or your company) pay for every community in your portfolio, then pass costs through per your management agreement.

## Plan tiers

Each plan tier unlocks a defined set of features:

- **Essentials — $199/mo** — compliance dashboard, meetings, announcements, public website, transparency page, and site editor. Up to 3 admins.
- **Professional — $349/mo** — everything in Essentials plus e-sign, violations, ARC, maintenance, finance, polls, work orders, amenities, calendar sync, accounting connectors, custom domain, and site polish blocks. Unlimited admins.
- **Operations Plus — $499/mo** — full operations suite for apartments and larger portfolios: maintenance, finance, violations, lease tracking, package and visitor logging, portfolio site templates, and unlimited admins. Does not include condo/HOA statutory compliance features.

Specific feature mappings are documented at the plan selection screen. Confirm with the board which features they expect before choosing.

<Callout type="tip" title="Don't over-buy on day one">
Communities often start on Essentials and upgrade as they discover features they want. Upgrades are seamless — features unlock immediately. Downgrades go through the Stripe billing portal and are more disruptive (features disappear), so start lower and grow into the higher tier rather than the reverse.
</Callout>

## Setting up billing on a new community

<StepByStep>
<Step title="During community creation">
The **Add a Community** modal asks for community name, plan, type, address, subdomain, unit count, and timezone. After you submit, embedded Stripe checkout collects payment.
</Step>

<Step title="Verify billing contact in Stripe">
Receipts and dunning notifications go to the email on file in Stripe. Update the billing email in the Stripe billing portal — there is no separate billing-email field in PropertyPro.
</Step>
</StepByStep>

## Changing plans

<StepByStep>
<Step title="Open Settings → Billing → Change plan">
From within the community, open the plan-change flow at `/settings/billing/change-plan`.
</Step>

<Step title="Pick the new plan and billing interval">
The screen shows what's included and what changes vs. your current plan. Choose monthly or annual billing here.
</Step>

<Step title="Review proration">
PropertyPro prorates upgrades on the spot (you pay the difference today).
</Step>

<Step title="Confirm">
Upgrades take effect immediately. Only upgrades are supported in-app — use the Stripe billing portal to downgrade or cancel.
</Step>
</StepByStep>

## Updating the payment method

<StepByStep>
<Step title="Open the billing portal">
Visit `/billing/portal`. This redirects you to the Stripe-hosted billing portal.
</Step>

<Step title="Add or update a card / bank account">
Manage saved methods, set defaults, and update card-on-file details.
</Step>

<Step title="Verify the change">
Return to PropertyPro. The billing page shows the updated method.
</Step>
</StepByStep>

Multiple methods can be on file; only the default is used for autopay.

## Invoices and receipts

The billing page at `/settings/billing` shows your current plan and subscription status. Click **View Invoices** to open the Stripe-hosted portal where you can download invoices and receipts as PDFs.

## Portfolio-level billing

If your management company centralizes billing across communities:

- **Single payer model** — one card/account on file pays for every community in the portfolio. Configure during onboarding.
- **Per-community model** — each community's board sets up their own billing. You see the status across all communities but don't manage payments.

Most PM relationships use the single-payer model with cost pass-through in the management agreement.

## Failed payments and dunning

If a charge fails, the community's subscription status changes to **Past Due** and admins see a warning banner with a link to the Stripe billing portal. Stripe handles payment retries automatically.

Resolve quickly — update the payment method in the Stripe portal and Stripe will retry the charge.

## Cancelling a subscription

If a community leaves your management portfolio:

<StepByStep>
<Step title="Coordinate handoff first">
The new management company should be added as admin before you cancel. Otherwise the community loses access.
</Step>

<Step title="Cancel via Settings → Billing">
Click **Cancel Subscription** to open the Stripe billing portal. Cancellation includes a 30-day grace period. The community remains active until the end of the paid period.
</Step>

<Step title="Confirm data retention">
Data retention after cancellation depends on your subscription terms and archive policy — confirm with PropertyPro support before assuming a specific window.
</Step>
</StepByStep>

## Refunds

PropertyPro refunds aren't automatic. To request:

1. Open Help → Contact Management with the invoice number and reason.
2. Support reviews — most refund requests are for billing errors or service issues.
3. Approved refunds return to the original payment method; timing depends on your payment provider (check your Stripe billing portal or contact support).

## Tax and record-keeping

PropertyPro invoices contain the company name, billing address, and an invoice number suitable for accounting. Your finance team can pull invoices automatically via the Stripe-hosted billing portal.

Consult your accountant about how to treat subscription fees. Pass-through to communities is a separate arrangement governed by your management agreement.

## Common questions

- **"Can a community switch plans monthly?"** Yes, but try not to. Frequent plan changes confuse residents (features appearing and disappearing). Pick the right plan and stick with it.
- **"What if the board wants a feature we're not paying for?"** Upgrade the plan. The cost is typically small relative to the management fee and the feature value to the board.
- **"Does the resident pay PropertyPro directly?"** No — communities are billed at the association level. Residents pay assessments to the association; the association covers PropertyPro from its budget.
