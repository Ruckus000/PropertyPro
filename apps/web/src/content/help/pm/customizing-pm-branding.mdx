PropertyPro's PM tier supports per-community branding — when residents see the public site and app, they see your management company's look, not generic PropertyPro chrome.

## What you can customize

Per community, in the website editor:

- **Main, secondary, and accent colors** — drive buttons, links, and accent elements.
- **Body font** — chosen from a curated list of Google Font families.
- **Logo** — square logo for the app avatar and auth screens.
- **Site logo** — horizontal wordmark for the public-site header.
- **Custom email footer** — plain-text footer appended to outbound emails.

Colors and the body font are **Professional** features. On other plans the
Colours tool stays visible but locked, and your site uses the colors from its
selected preset.

## Setting up branding

<StepByStep>
<Step title="Open the website editor">
From the PM dashboard, open a community and go to **Website**.
</Step>

<Step title="Open the Colours tool">
In the editor's tool tabs, choose **Colours**.
</Step>

<Step title="Turn on the colors you want to set">
Each color has its own switch. Leave a switch off to keep the preset's color for
that slot. Turning one on starts from the color your site uses today, so you are
only changing what you mean to change.
</Step>

<Step title="Pick colors and a font">
Use the picker or type a six-digit hex code. Choose a body font from the list.
</Step>

<Step title="Save">
Choose **Save colours**. These go live on your website right away — they are not
part of Publish. Open your public site to verify.
</Step>
</StepByStep>

To push branding across multiple communities at once, use **Templates** at `/pm/portfolio/templates` — available on the **Operations Plus** plan only.

## Per-community overrides

Individual communities can have their own logo and colors if their board prefers. When that happens:

- The community's branding takes precedence on that community's public site and app pages.
- Your PM-level templates can still seed defaults for new communities.

This lets large associations maintain their identity while your company still manages the portfolio.

## Custom domain

A custom public-site domain is available on the **Professional** plan and above (on Essentials the **Custom Domain** card is shown as a locked upsell). Each eligible community can configure one via the **Custom Domain** card in the site editor:

1. Enter your hostname and click **Add domain**.
2. Add the DNS records PropertyPro shows at your registrar.
3. Click **Check status** to verify propagation.

This configures the community's public website domain — not a separate app login domain.

<Callout type="tip" title="Test email deliverability after a domain change">
Changing the sender domain can affect email deliverability temporarily. Send a test announcement to yourself after the change to confirm emails aren't being filtered. If they are, check SPF / DKIM / DMARC records with our support team.
</Callout>

## Brand consistency

When onboarding new communities to your portfolio, apply branding per community in the site editor — or use portfolio templates to seed defaults. Existing communities keep their current branding until you update them.

## Removing PM branding

If your company stops managing a community (or the community changes management):

1. Remove the community from your portfolio.
2. The incoming management company applies their branding, or the community reverts to default PropertyPro styling until branding is configured.

Your PM branding doesn't linger on a community you no longer manage.

## Design tips

- **Logos need to work on light backgrounds.** A dark or colored logo with a transparent background is safest.
- **Primary color should have 4.5:1 contrast against white text.** Very light colors will look washed out on buttons.
- **Test on mobile.** Some logos look great on desktop and pixellate on phone screens.
- **Keep it simple.** A clean brand usually outperforms a busy one, especially when residents see it on every page they visit.
