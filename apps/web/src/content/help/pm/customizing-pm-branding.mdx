PropertyPro's PM tier supports white-label branding — when residents see the app, they see your management company's look and contact details, not the generic PropertyPro chrome.

## What you can customize

- **Logo** — appears in the top bar, login screen, and email templates.
- **Primary color** — drives buttons, links, and accent elements.
- **Contact details** — your management company's email and phone appear on the Help → Contact Management page for every community you manage.
- **Email sender name** — outgoing emails come from your company name, not generic PropertyPro.
- **Custom domain** (if enabled on your plan) — residents access the app at your company's domain instead of the shared PropertyPro domain.

## Setting up branding

<StepByStep>
<Step title="Open PM Settings → Branding">
Visit `/pm/settings/branding` from the PM dashboard.
</Step>

<Step title="Upload your logo">
Use a transparent-background PNG at least 256 pixels wide. PropertyPro resizes automatically for different placements.
</Step>

<Step title="Pick your primary color">
Enter a hex code. PropertyPro generates hover and active states automatically from the primary color. Pick something with enough contrast against white backgrounds.
</Step>

<Step title="Enter contact info">
Your management company's phone and email. This appears on every community's Help contact page.
</Step>

<Step title="Save and preview">
Changes apply to every community in your portfolio immediately. Navigate to a community to verify the branding applied cleanly.
</Step>
</StepByStep>

## Per-community overrides

Individual communities can override the PM brand with their own logo and colors if their board prefers. When that happens:

- The community's branding takes precedence inside that community's pages.
- Your PM branding applies at the portfolio level (PM dashboard, cross-community reports).
- Contact info defaults to your PM-level contact unless the community overrides.

This lets large associations maintain their identity while your company still manages the portfolio.

## Custom domain

If custom domain is on your plan:

1. Configure DNS at your registrar — add a CNAME pointing your subdomain (e.g., `residents.acmemgmt.com`) to the value PropertyPro provides.
2. Wait for DNS to propagate (up to 24 hours).
3. Enable the domain in PM Settings → Branding → Custom domain.
4. Test by visiting the new URL — you should see your branded login screen.

<Callout type="tip" title="Test email deliverability after a domain change">
Changing the sender domain can affect email deliverability temporarily. Send a test announcement to yourself after the change to confirm emails aren't being filtered. If they are, check SPF / DKIM / DMARC records with our support team.
</Callout>

## Brand consistency

When onboarding new communities to your portfolio, the PM branding applies automatically — the community sees your logo and colors from day one. Existing communities keep their current branding unless you override.

## Removing PM branding

If your company stops managing a community (or the community changes management):

1. Remove the community from your portfolio.
2. The incoming management company applies their branding, or the community reverts to default PropertyPro styling until branding is configured.

Your PM branding doesn't linger on a community you no longer manage.

## Design tips

- **Logos need to work on light backgrounds.** A dark or colored logo with a transparent background is safest.
- **Primary color should have 4.5:1 contrast against white text.** Very light colors will look washed out on buttons.
- **Test on mobile.** Some logos look great on desktop and pixellate on phone screens.
- **Keep it simple.** A clean brand usually outperforms a busy one, especially when residents see it on every page they visit.
