A submission is one instance of a template being sent to specific signers. This guide walks sending, tracking, and finalizing a submission.

## Creating a submission

<StepByStep>
<Step title="Send Document">
From E-Sign in the sidebar, click **Send Document**.
</Step>

<Step title="Pick a template">
Choose the template that matches what you're sending. Every submission starts from a template.
</Step>

<Step title="Assign signers">
For each signer role defined in the template, type each signer's full name and email address. External signers (non-residents) can be added the same way.
</Step>

<Step title="Set signing order">
Choose **Parallel** so all signers can sign at the same time, or **Sequential** so signing links unlock in order. In sequential mode, all signers still receive their email at creation — the signing page shows **Waiting for another signer** until earlier signers finish.
</Step>

<Step title="Add a message">
Write a short note for the signers. This appears in the invitation email — useful for context ("This is your annual insurance acknowledgement; due by April 30").
</Step>

<Step title="Set expiration">
Choose **Expires after** (7, 14, 30, 60, or 90 days; default 30). When the expiration passes, signing links stop working and signers see **Request expired**.
</Step>

<Step title="Send">
Each signer receives an email with a personalized signing link.
</Step>
</StepByStep>

## Tracking progress

Open the submission record to see real-time status per signer:

- **Pending** — email sent; signer hasn't completed signing
- **Opened** — link clicked; review in progress
- **Completed** — their portion is complete
- **Declined** — signer chose not to sign (with optional reason)

For sequential submissions, all signers are emailed at creation. Sequencing blocks the signing page (**Waiting for another signer**), not the email delivery.

## Sending reminders

Haven't heard back from a signer?

1. Open the submission.
2. Click **Send reminder** next to their row.
3. They receive another email with the same link.

You can send up to 3 manual reminders per signer. PropertyPro does not send automatic reminders.

## Cancelling a submission

If you sent to the wrong person, or the submission is no longer needed:

1. Open the submission.
2. Click **Cancel Request**.
3. Outstanding signing links stop working. Signers are not notified — if they open their link, they see **Request cancelled**.

<Callout type="warning" title="Can't cancel partial signatures">
If any signer has already completed their signature, you cannot delete the signatures they already provided. You can mark the submission Cancelled, but the partial record remains. Be deliberate about who you send to.
</Callout>

## Completed submissions

Once every signer has signed:

- A final combined PDF is generated with every signer's signatures and field values. Timestamps, IP addresses, and consent records are stored in the audit log, not embedded in the PDF.
- Signers download the signed PDF from their signing page. Admins download it from the submission record (**Download Signed Document**). No completion email is sent, and the PDF is not archived to the Documents library.

## Common patterns

- **Send to one person** — a lease, a unit-specific amendment.
- **Send to every owner** — annual disclosures, major bylaw amendments. Send one submission per owner manually (bulk e-sign is not yet available).
- **Multi-party submissions** — three-way contracts with association, vendor, and board president as signers in order.

## Audit trail

Every submission's lifecycle — sent, opened, signed, completed — is captured in the compliance audit log. If a signer later disputes that they signed, you have timestamped evidence of the signing event including IP address.

## What if a signer asks for changes?

The signer must decline the current submission with a reason ("Paragraph 3 is unclear"). You then edit the template or this specific submission, cancel the current request, and resend with the changes.
