Announcements are how admins push community-wide information — newsletters, maintenance alerts, policy reminders. They differ from meeting notices, which have statutory timing rules and belong in Meetings.

## When to use an announcement

Use announcements for:

- Informational updates (pool reopened, trash day change)
- Reminders (annual fire-inspection week, parking rules)
- Community events (social, meet-and-greet)
- Non-statutory alerts (maintenance outage, weather prep)

Use **Meetings** instead for board or owner meeting notices — those carry statutory 14-day / 48-hour timing requirements.

## Composing an announcement

<StepByStep>
<Step title="Open Announcements">
Click Announcements in the sidebar, then **New announcement** in the top right.
</Step>

<Step title="Write the headline and body">
Keep the headline specific — "Hurricane Idalia prep: three things to do this weekend" beats "Important announcement." The body supports basic formatting and can include links.
</Step>

<Step title="Attach files">
Drag in PDFs, images, or other documents — a pool-closure notice with a photo of the new hours is far more useful than plain text.
</Step>

<Step title="Pin if urgent">
Check **Pin to top** for announcements that should stay visible until resolved — storm prep, major outages. Unpin when the situation is over.
</Step>

<Step title="Preview">
Use the preview to see exactly what residents will receive in email and see on their dashboards.
</Step>

<Step title="Publish">
Click Publish. The announcement appears on every resident's dashboard instantly and fans out to email based on each resident's notification preferences.
</Step>
</StepByStep>

## Targeting an audience

By default, announcements go to every member of the community. You can narrow the audience to specific roles (Owners only, Tenants only) or a custom recipient list. Targeted announcements are useful for:

- **Owner-only updates** — assessment previews, reserve discussions
- **Board-only** — internal planning reminders
- **Ad-hoc groups** — residents affected by a specific outage

<Callout type="tip" title="Keep residents in the loop">
When in doubt, address the whole community. Residents who feel excluded from updates often escalate privately; broad communication usually prevents that.
</Callout>

## Scheduling

If your community has scheduled delivery enabled, you can pick a future send time. Drafts save automatically as you work. Scheduled announcements show on the Announcements list with a Scheduled badge and can be edited up to send time.

## Editing or retracting

<Callout type="warning" title="Edits aren't silent">
Editing a published announcement doesn't recall the email that already went out. If you're correcting a substantive error, consider publishing a short follow-up announcement acknowledging the correction rather than quietly editing the original.
</Callout>

To retract an announcement entirely, open it and click **Unpublish** (or the trash icon). Residents who already received email still have the text in their inbox — but the announcement disappears from the dashboard and the Announcements list.

## Analytics

Open an announcement to see:

- How many residents have viewed it (in-app)
- Delivery status per channel (email sent / failed / bounced)
- Any resident comments

Use this to gauge whether a follow-up reminder is warranted.
