Announcements are the way your association pushes community-wide updates — maintenance notices, policy changes, upcoming events. Unlike meeting notices (which have statutory timing), announcements are informational.

## Where announcements appear

- **Dashboard** — the three newest announcements are on your dashboard.
- **Announcements page** — click **Announcements** in the sidebar for the full list.
- **Email** — based on your notification preferences.

Each announcement shows the title, the sender, when it was posted, and any attachments.

## Marking announcements read

Announcements track read/unread state per user. Opening an announcement marks it read automatically. You can also:

- **Mark all read** — the button at the top of the Announcements page clears the unread count.
- **Re-open** — click an announcement after marking it read to re-read it; the read state stays marked.

## Attachments

Announcements may include attached files — event flyers, rule PDFs, survey results. Click the attachment to preview or download.

## Emailed announcements

If your notification preferences include announcement emails, you'll also get each new announcement in your inbox. The email links back to the web version so you can open attachments or see updates.

<Callout type="tip" title="Too much email?">
Go to Settings → Notifications and dial back announcement emails to a weekly digest. You still see announcements on your dashboard in real time, you just stop getting every one in email.
</Callout>

## Pinned announcements

Important announcements — hurricane prep, major maintenance outages — can be pinned to the top of the list by admins. Pinned items have a small pin indicator and stay visible until unpinned.

## If an announcement seems wrong

PropertyPro doesn't edit announcements from the resident side. If you believe an announcement contains an error or affects your lease/ownership incorrectly, open Help → Contact Management and message the board with the announcement URL. They can update or retract it.

## Who can post announcements?

Only community admins (board members, CAMs, and property managers) can publish announcements. If you're wondering who sent a specific one, the sender's name appears on the post itself.
