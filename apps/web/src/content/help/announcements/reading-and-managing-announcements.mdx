Announcements are the way your association pushes community-wide updates — maintenance notices, policy changes, upcoming events. Unlike meeting notices (which have statutory timing), announcements are informational.

## Where announcements appear

- **Dashboard** — up to five announcements, with pinned items shown first.
- **Announcements page** — click **Announcements** in the sidebar for the full list.
- **Email** — based on your notification preferences.

Each announcement shows the title, posted date, and a **Pinned** badge when applicable.

## Marking notifications read

Announcements do not track read/unread state on the Announcements page. Read state lives on the **Notifications** page (bell icon in the top bar). There you can:

- **Mark all read** — clears the unread count for your notifications.
- Open individual notifications to jump to the related item.

## Emailed announcements

If your notification preferences include announcement emails, you'll also get each new announcement in your inbox. The email links back to the web version.

<Callout type="tip" title="Too much email?">
Go to Settings and use the **Notification Preferences** section to set Email frequency to Daily digest or Weekly digest, or uncheck Announcements. You still see announcements on your dashboard in real time — you just stop getting every one in email.
</Callout>

## Pinned announcements

Important announcements — hurricane prep, major maintenance outages — can be pinned to the top of the list by admins. Pinned items have a small pin indicator and stay visible until unpinned.

## If an announcement seems wrong

PropertyPro doesn't edit announcements from the resident side. If you believe an announcement contains an error or affects your lease/ownership incorrectly, open Help → Management Contact and message the board with the announcement URL. They can update or retract it.

## Who can post announcements?

Only community admins (board members, CAMs, and property managers) can publish announcements.
