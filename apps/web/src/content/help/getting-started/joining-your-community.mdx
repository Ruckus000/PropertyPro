PropertyPro ties every account to one or more communities. The first step after signing up is linking your account to the association you belong to.

## Two ways to join

Most communities use one of these paths:

- **Invitation from your management team** — you receive an email with a link. Click it while signed in (or sign in when prompted) and your account is linked automatically.
- **Self-service join request** — visit `/account/join-community`, choose your community from the list, enter your unit, choose Owner or Tenant, and submit. An admin reviews and approves.

<Callout type="tip" title="If you don't see your community">
Only communities using PropertyPro show up. If yours isn't listed, your association hasn't onboarded yet — reach out to your manager and ask them to invite you directly.
</Callout>

## What a join request needs

<StepByStep>
<Step title="Open Join Another Community">
Visit `/account/join-community` — the page is titled **Join Another Community**.
</Step>

<Step title="Pick your community">
Search by community name.
</Step>

<Step title="Fill in unit details">
Enter your unit number exactly as it appears on your association records and choose Owner or Tenant. Accurate details speed up approval.
</Step>

<Step title="Submit and watch for a notification">
You'll receive an in-app notification when an admin approves or denies your request. Denied requests include a short reason so you know what to fix.
</Step>
</StepByStep>

## Switching between communities

Once you belong to more than one community, click the **Communities** button in the top bar to switch. Selecting a community reloads the dashboard with that community's data. All your help articles, FAQs, and notifications re-scope automatically.

## What admins see

If you're a community admin, pending requests appear in one of two queues — **Join requests** at `/admin/join-requests` or **Access requests** on the Residents page. See [Approving join requests](/help/residents/approving-join-requests) for which queue applies and how to triage each. Approving promotes the applicant to a full community member; denying sends them a note with your reason.

## Common hiccups

- **Unit number mismatch** — if the number you enter doesn't appear on the association's unit list, admins typically ask you to verify with a payment stub or bill. Double-check capitalization and leading zeros.
- **Wrong role** — tenants occasionally pick Owner by mistake. The role governs what you can see and edit — admins often correct it at approval time.
- **Multiple pending requests** — you can only have one pending request per community. You can't cancel or edit a pending request — wait for review, or contact your admin.
