PropertyPro ties every account to one or more communities. The first step after signing up is linking your account to the association you belong to.

## Two ways to join

Most communities use one of these paths:

- **Invitation from your management team** — you receive an email with a link. Click it while signed in (or sign in when prompted) and your account is linked automatically.
- **Self-service join request** — open Account > Join Community, choose your community from the list, enter your unit and contact info, and submit. An admin reviews and approves.

<Callout type="tip" title="If you don't see your community">
Only communities using PropertyPro show up. If yours isn't listed, your association hasn't onboarded yet — reach out to your manager and ask them to invite you directly.
</Callout>

## What a join request needs

<StepByStep>
<Step title="Open Join Community">
From the profile menu, pick **Join another community**, or visit `/account/join-community`.
</Step>

<Step title="Pick your community">
Search by name or slug. Communities mark themselves public or invite-only — public ones accept self-service requests.
</Step>

<Step title="Fill in unit details">
Enter your unit number exactly as it appears on your association records, plus your role (Owner, Tenant, Board Member, etc.). Accurate details speed up approval.
</Step>

<Step title="Submit and watch for email">
You'll receive an email when an admin approves, denies, or requests more info. Denied requests include a short reason so you know what to fix.
</Step>
</StepByStep>

## Switching between communities

Once you belong to more than one community, the community name in the top bar doubles as a switcher. Switching reloads the dashboard with the chosen community's data. All your help articles, FAQs, and notifications re-scope automatically.

## What admins see

If you're a community admin, you approve join requests at `/admin/join-requests`. You see the request's unit, declared role, and any evidence the resident attached. Approving promotes them to a full community member; denying sends the applicant a note.

## Common hiccups

- **Unit number mismatch** — if the number you enter doesn't appear on the association's unit list, admins typically ask you to verify with a payment stub or bill. Double-check capitalization and leading zeros.
- **Wrong role** — tenants occasionally pick Owner by mistake. The role governs what you can see and edit — admins often correct it at approval time.
- **Multiple pending requests** — you can only have one pending request per community. Cancel the old one first if you need to resubmit with updates.
