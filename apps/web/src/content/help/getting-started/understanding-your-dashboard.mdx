Your dashboard is the first page you see after signing in. It surfaces the work that matters most for your role without forcing you to dig through menus.

## Cards residents see

<StepByStep>
<Step title="Recent Announcements">
Up to five of the newest community-wide announcements appear here, with the most recent first. Headlines are not clickable — open Announcements from the sidebar to read the full post.
</Step>

<Step title="Upcoming Meetings">
Your next upcoming meetings with date, time, and location. Items are not clickable — open Meetings from the sidebar for full details.
</Step>

<Step title="Documents to sign">
If your community uses e-sign and you have documents awaiting signature, they appear here. Click to open the signing flow — no account switching needed. This card is hidden when you have nothing to sign.
</Step>

<Step title="Violations">
A community-wide summary of violation counts by status. Admins see open counts and recent items; the card is hidden when violations are not enabled for your community.
</Step>
</StepByStep>

## Cards admins see

Board members, CAMs, and property managers see the same dashboard cards as residents, plus an onboarding checklist at the top. For compliance status, open `/communities/[id]/compliance`. For join requests and resident management, open the Residents page.

<Callout type="tip" title="Keep the dashboard clean">
If a card looks stale, click through and either resolve it or update its status. The dashboard reflects what's actually open for you right now, so treating it as a to-do list keeps things accurate.
</Callout>

## Empty states

When a card has nothing to show, some cards stay visible with a short "nothing here yet" message. Others are hidden entirely — Documents to sign when you have nothing pending, and Violations when the feature is off for your community.

## Switching communities

If you belong to more than one community — for example, an owner with both a condo and an HOA property — click the **Communities** button in the top bar. Choose **All communities overview** or pick a specific community. The dashboard reloads with that community's data.
