Your dashboard is the first page you see after signing in. It surfaces the work that matters most for your role without forcing you to dig through menus.

## Cards residents see

<StepByStep>
<Step title="Recent Announcements">
The three newest community-wide announcements appear at the top, with the most recent first. Click any headline to read the full announcement and any attached files.
</Step>

<Step title="Upcoming Meetings">
Any scheduled board or owner meeting for the next 14 days appears here, with the date, time, and posting status. You can click through to the full notice and the agenda.
</Step>

<Step title="Documents to sign">
If your community uses e-sign, any document awaiting your signature appears here. Click to open the signing flow — no account switching needed.
</Step>

<Step title="Violations">
Open violations or compliance reports involving your unit appear here with the current status so you can follow progress in one place.
</Step>
</StepByStep>

## Cards admins see

Board members, CAMs, and property managers see the resident cards **plus** an admin summary:

- **Compliance score** — a snapshot with a direct link to the compliance dashboard.
- **Join requests** — residents who requested access and are waiting for approval.
- **Outstanding documents** — files approaching the 30-day posting deadline.

<Callout type="tip" title="Keep the dashboard clean">
If a card looks stale, click through and either resolve it or update its status. The dashboard reflects what's actually open for you right now, so treating it as a to-do list keeps things accurate.
</Callout>

## Empty states

When a card has nothing to show it stays visible with a short "nothing here yet" message. That's expected behavior — the card structure stays consistent so you can rely on the same layout every day.

## Switching communities

If you belong to more than one community — for example, an owner with both a condo and an HOA property — your community name appears in the top bar and doubles as a switcher. Clicking it reveals the other communities you can access, and the dashboard reloads with that community's data.
