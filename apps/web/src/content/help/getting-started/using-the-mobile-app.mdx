PropertyPro's web app is fully responsive. On mobile, routes under `/mobile/*` give you a touch-first layout with a bottom nav and streamlined forms.

## Installing to your home screen

PropertyPro works as a Progressive Web App — you can install it like a native app without the App Store.

<StepByStep>
<Step title="iPhone / iPad (Safari)">
Open your community URL in Safari. Tap the **Share** icon, then **Add to Home Screen**. Tap Add. The icon appears on your home screen and opens full-screen.
</Step>

<Step title="Android (Chrome)">
Open the URL in Chrome. Tap the three-dot menu, then **Add to Home screen** or **Install app**. Confirm. The app icon appears with the rest.
</Step>
</StepByStep>

## Bottom navigation

The mobile bottom nav shows the most-used features for your role — Dashboard, Documents, Meetings, and Help at a minimum. Admins may see additional tabs.

Tapping your avatar in the top right opens the profile menu with account settings, community switcher, and sign out.

## What works differently on mobile

- **Forms** are laid out for thumb reach; long labels wrap.
- **Documents** preview inline when the file is small; larger files open in your phone's default reader.
- **Payments** use Apple Pay / Google Pay when your community has Stripe enabled and your device supports it.
- **Push notifications** aren't enabled yet — you still get email and in-app notifications.

## Offline behavior

The app requires an internet connection for most actions. Reading an already-opened document or announcement works offline briefly, but new actions (submitting maintenance, paying dues) need connectivity.

<Callout type="tip" title="If the mobile layout seems wrong">
The `/mobile/*` routes are the intentional mobile surface. If you land on the desktop layout on a phone, it's usually because a link sent you to a desktop-only path — the app still works, just with more horizontal scrolling. The help center itself uses the same layout for both.
</Callout>

## Signing out

Profile menu → **Sign out**. If you share a device, sign out between uses so another resident doesn't see your dashboard.
