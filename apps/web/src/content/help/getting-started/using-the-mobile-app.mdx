PropertyPro's web app is fully responsive. On mobile, routes under `/mobile/*` give you a touch-first layout with hub-and-spoke navigation and streamlined forms.

## Adding a home-screen shortcut

You can bookmark PropertyPro or add it to your home screen as a browser shortcut.

<StepByStep>
<Step title="iPhone / iPad (Safari)">
Open your community URL in Safari. Tap the **Share** icon, then **Add to Home Screen**. Tap Add. The icon opens the site in Safari.
</Step>

<Step title="Android (Chrome)">
Open the URL in Chrome. Tap the three-dot menu, then **Add to Home screen**. Confirm. The shortcut opens the site in your browser.
</Step>
</StepByStep>

## Navigation

The mobile home screen shows navigation rows for your role — Documents, Announcements, Meetings, Maintenance, Payments, and Compliance where enabled. Tap a row to open that section.

Tap your initials in the top right to open the **More** page with account settings and **Sign out**.

## What works differently on mobile

- **Forms** are laid out for thumb reach; long labels wrap.
- **Documents** — every document has **Open** (in-app viewer) and **Download** buttons.
- **Payments** use Apple Pay / Google Pay when your community has Stripe enabled and your device supports it.
- **Push notifications** aren't enabled yet — you still get email and in-app notifications.

## Offline behavior

PropertyPro requires an internet connection. Submitting maintenance requests, paying dues, and loading new content all need connectivity.

<Callout type="tip" title="If the mobile layout seems wrong">
The `/mobile/*` routes are the intentional mobile surface. If you land on the desktop layout on a phone, it's usually because a link sent you to a desktop-only path — the app still works, just with more horizontal scrolling. The help center itself uses the same layout for both.
</Callout>

## Signing out

On the **More** page, tap **Sign out**. If you share a device, sign out between uses so another resident doesn't see your dashboard.
