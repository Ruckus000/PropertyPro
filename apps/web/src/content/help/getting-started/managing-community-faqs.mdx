PropertyPro's library of help articles covers the platform itself. **Community FAQs** are the short, community-specific quick answers — pool hours, trash pickup days, who to call for what. Every community admin can author and curate them; residents find them through help-center search and the help panel's search.

## What an FAQ is for

- **Community-specific policies** — "Are short-term rentals allowed?", "When are quiet hours?"
- **Operational details** — "When is trash picked up?", "How do I reserve the clubhouse?"
- **Contact routing** — "Who do I call for a clogged drain at midnight?"
- **Local context** — "Where is the storm shelter?", "What's the gate code policy?"

For everything else — how to use a PropertyPro feature, statutory requirements, generic guidance — the platform's help articles already cover it. Don't duplicate.

## Opening the FAQ editor

<StepByStep>
<Step title="Navigate to /help/manage">
From the help center, admins see a **Manage FAQs** link. Click it (or visit `/help/manage` directly). Non-admins are redirected back to the help center.
</Step>

<Step title="Review existing FAQs">
The editor shows every FAQ currently published for this community. Click a row to open it in the side panel.
</Step>
</StepByStep>

## Authoring an FAQ

<StepByStep>
<Step title="Add or edit in the side panel">
Click an existing FAQ row to open **Edit FAQ**, or start a new one with **Add FAQ** in the side panel. Enter the Question and Answer fields.
</Step>

<Step title="Write the question">
Phrase the question the way a resident would ask it — natural, specific, slightly informal. "When does the pool open?" beats "Pool hours."
</Step>

<Step title="Write the answer">
Keep it short — 2-4 sentences usually. If the answer is long, write a brief summary and link to a document or external resource. Long answers belong in a posted document, not a FAQ.
</Step>

<Step title="Save">
Click **Save changes** (or **Add FAQ** for a new entry). Click **Delete FAQ** to remove one. The FAQ is immediately visible to residents via help search.
</Step>
</StepByStep>

## Ordering FAQs

The FAQ editor does not currently offer reordering — FAQs display in a fixed order and there is no drag-and-drop or up/down control in the UI today (on either desktop or mobile). Until reordering ships, the practical lever you have is wording:

- **Phrase the most-asked questions clearly** so they surface well in search, which is how most residents find FAQs.
- **Keep each answer self-contained** so it stands on its own regardless of where it falls in the list.

## Editing or removing FAQs

Click any FAQ row to open it in the side panel. Make your changes and click **Save changes**, or click **Delete FAQ** to remove it. Deletion is immediate. The audit trail captures the deletion event.

<Callout type="warning" title="Outdated FAQs erode trust">
A FAQ saying "pool opens May 1" that's still up in October hurts more than no FAQ. Schedule a quarterly review to walk every FAQ for accuracy. Better to delete a stale one than leave it published.
</Callout>

## Writing guidance

### Good FAQs
- Match real questions you've received via phone or email
- Use plain language, not jargon
- Link to a specific document when the answer references one ("See the parking policy at...")
- Are dated or seasonally-aware where relevant

### Bad FAQs
- Vague ("How does this work?")
- Long, multi-paragraph answers — those belong in posted documents
- Duplicates of platform help articles (uploading documents, managing notifications)
- Stale ("As of 2024..." that's still live in 2026)

## What residents see

Residents find FAQs via help-center search and the `?` panel's search — FAQs appear in search results alongside help articles. The contextual help drawer lists articles for the current page; FAQs surface when residents search within the panel.

## Bulk operations

There's no CSV import for FAQs today — they're a low-volume content type. For initial bulk-load when onboarding a community, copy-paste each question and answer from your existing source (a Google Doc, an old website).

## Cadence

- **Weekly**: review FAQs that may be out of date.
- **Quarterly**: walk every FAQ for accuracy.
- **After policy changes**: update relevant FAQs the same day the policy changes go live.
- **After resident escalations**: when a resident asks something that should have been findable, add the FAQ that day.

## Audit trail

Every FAQ create, edit, and delete is captured in the compliance audit log with the admin's name and timestamp. If a resident later says "the FAQ told me X," the trail shows what was actually published when.

## Common questions about FAQs

- **"Can FAQs link to PropertyPro help articles?"** Yes — paste the article URL into the answer and it renders as a link.
- **"Are FAQs visible publicly?"** No, they're behind authentication. Public-facing answers belong on the [transparency page](/help/transparency/configuring-the-transparency-page).
- **"Can different roles see different FAQs?"** Yes — PropertyPro enforces role visibility on FAQs server-side. Every resident sees only the FAQs allowed for their role. You cannot edit role visibility in the FAQ editor today.
