PropertyPro keeps Florida community records, compliance, and resident communication in one place. This guide orients you so you can jump to the task you need in a few clicks.

<Callout type="tip" title="Start at the dashboard">
Your dashboard adapts to your role. Residents see dues, maintenance, meetings, and recent announcements. Community admins see compliance status, documents pending posting, and community-wide work.
</Callout>

## How to find anything

The top of every page has a search bar and a help button. Search looks across documents, residents, meetings, and announcements for your community. The help button — the question mark at the top right — opens a help window with guides for the page you're on — you can search all articles from there too.

<StepByStep>
<Step title="Open the sidebar">
On phones, tap the menu icon in the top left to open the main navigation. On desktop the sidebar is always shown. Everyone sees Dashboard, Documents, Meetings, and Announcements. Admins also see Compliance and Residents.
</Step>

<Step title="Pick a feature">
Each link leads to a dedicated page for that feature. The current page is highlighted so you always know where you are.
</Step>

<Step title="Come back to help when you're stuck">
Press `?` on any page to open the help window. It shows guides for the page you're on and searches help articles and community FAQs together.
</Step>
</StepByStep>

## What each audience usually does

- **Owners and tenants** check announcements, submit maintenance requests, and read meeting notices. Owners can also pay dues.
- **Board members and board presidents** review compliance, approve resident join requests, and post meeting notices.
- **CAMs and site managers** handle day-to-day operations — violations, maintenance triage, document uploads.
- **Property manager admins** oversee multiple communities at once from the PM dashboard.

## Florida-specific context

PropertyPro is built around Florida statute requirements for condos and HOAs.

<Callout type="florida-statute" title="§ 718.111(12)(g)">
Condo associations with 25 or more units must maintain a website with specific posted records. PropertyPro tracks posting timing and flags anything approaching the 30-day window on the compliance dashboard.
</Callout>

## Next steps

- Open **Documents** to find bylaws, budgets, and meeting minutes.
- Open **Payments** to review your balance or pay online (owners only — tenants do not see Payments in the sidebar).
- Open **Operations** to report an issue.
- Open **Meetings** to see upcoming dates and posted materials.

If anything is missing or confusing, use the feedback widget at the bottom of each article — your community admin sees what's working and what isn't.
