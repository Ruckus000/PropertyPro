# Welcome to PropertyPro

PropertyPro helps Florida communities stay compliant, manage day-to-day operations, and keep residents informed from one place.

<Callout type="tip" title="First time here?">
Start with your dashboard. It gives you a role-aware snapshot of the work, documents, and updates that matter most right now.
</Callout>

## Start with the dashboard

The dashboard highlights the work that matters most for your role. Residents usually begin with documents, payments, meetings, and maintenance. Community admins usually begin with compliance, announcements, and resident management.

## Use Help when you are stuck

Open the Help Center whenever you need a shortcut to a task, a guide, or your management contact information. The hub combines platform guides with community-specific FAQs so you can find both generic workflow help and local answers in one place.

## Common next steps

- Open Documents to find rules, budgets, minutes, and community records.
- Open Payments to review balances or complete an online payment.
- Open Maintenance to submit a new issue or check on an existing request.
- Open Meetings to review upcoming notices, agendas, and posted materials.
