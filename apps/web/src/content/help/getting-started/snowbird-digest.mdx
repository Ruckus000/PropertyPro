The community digest is an occasional email that recaps what happened while you were away — board decisions, new documents, and deadlines coming up. It's built for seasonal and out-of-state owners who don't want to check the portal every week, and it's put together automatically from what's already in PropertyPro, so your board doesn't have to write anything.

<Callout type="info" title="A courtesy summary, not an official notice">
The digest is a convenience. Official notices — meeting notices, voting, and anything your association is required to send — arrive separately and are not replaced by this email.
</Callout>

## Change how often you get it

1. Go to **Settings**.
2. Find the **Community digest** card.
3. Choose **Weekly**, **Monthly**, or **Off**.

You can also unsubscribe in one click from the link at the bottom of any digest email — no need to log in.

## Frequently asked questions

**I'm an owner. Am I already getting it?**
If your board has turned the digest on, owners receive it weekly by default. Change the cadence or turn it off any time in Settings.

**Will it ever email me when nothing happened?**
No. If there's no new activity in the period, that digest is skipped.

**I'm on the board — how do I turn it on for everyone?**
On the **Community digest** card in Settings, switch **Send the digest to owners** on. Owners then receive it weekly by default and can adjust their own cadence.
