Your profile is how your community identifies you — on directories, announcements you comment on, and records like resident lists.

## What's on your profile

- **Display name** — what other residents and staff see.
- **Email** — used for sign-in and notifications.
- **Phone** — optional, used by staff for urgent communications.
- **Profile photo** — optional, displayed next to your name.
- **Mailing address** — if different from your unit address.
- **Emergency contact** — optional, shared only with community admins.

## Making changes

<StepByStep>
<Step title="Open Settings → Account">
From the profile menu, click Settings, then Account.
</Step>

<Step title="Edit the field">
Click any value to edit. Most fields save inline — no separate Save button — but sensitive changes (email, mailing address) ask for confirmation.
</Step>

<Step title="Upload a photo">
Click the avatar. Pick a square image if possible; PropertyPro crops other aspect ratios automatically.
</Step>

<Step title="Review and leave">
Changes are live. Navigate away when you're done — no Submit step required.
</Step>
</StepByStep>

## Changing your email

Changing your sign-in email is a two-step flow:

1. Enter the new email on the Account page.
2. Click the verification link in the email sent to the new address. Until you verify, your old email still works for sign-in.

<Callout type="warning" title="Don't leave a verification pending">
If you set a new email and don't verify it, a future password reset may land in a mailbox you no longer check. Verify right away or revert to your old address.
</Callout>

## Privacy

- **Other residents** can see your display name and (if enabled) your photo on community pages.
- **Staff and board** see your full contact details so they can reach you about unit-specific matters.
- **Platform admins** have emergency access for support and incident response.
- **Other communities** where you're not a member see nothing about you.

## Deleting your profile

If you move out or no longer need access, you have two options:

- **Leave a community** — keeps your PropertyPro account for any other communities you belong to. Ask your admin to remove you from the specific community.
- **Delete your account entirely** — see [requesting account deletion](/help/account/requesting-account-deletion).
