PropertyPro uses Supabase Auth for sign-in. Your password lives with Supabase and never touches the PropertyPro application server directly.

## Changing your password

<StepByStep>
<Step title="Open Settings → Security">
From the profile menu, go to Settings and open the Security section.
</Step>

<Step title="Click Change password">
Enter your current password, then the new one twice. Pick a password you don't reuse elsewhere.
</Step>

<Step title="Save">
Your new password takes effect immediately. Existing signed-in sessions stay signed in unless you also sign out everywhere.
</Step>
</StepByStep>

## Forgetting your password

If you're locked out:

1. Go to the sign-in page.
2. Click **Forgot password?**
3. Enter your email.
4. Check your inbox for a reset link. It expires after a set window (usually 30 minutes).
5. Click the link, set a new password, and you're back in.

<Callout type="warning" title="Reset links expire">
If the link doesn't work, it may have expired. Request a new one. If the email doesn't arrive at all, check spam and confirm you used the email on file.
</Callout>

## Two-factor authentication

If your association has 2FA enabled, you'll be prompted during sign-in for a second factor — typically a one-time code from an authenticator app.

- Use Google Authenticator, 1Password, Authy, or any compatible app.
- Save your backup codes somewhere offline. They're a lifeline if you lose your phone.
- Re-enable 2FA on a new device by scanning the QR code in Settings → Security.

## Active sessions

Settings → Security shows a list of devices signed into your account. If you see something you don't recognize, click **Sign out** on that row. For extra safety, change your password right after.

## Suspicious activity

If you believe your account has been accessed by someone else:

1. Change your password immediately.
2. Sign out all sessions.
3. Check Settings → Account for unexpected changes to your email or phone.
4. Message your community admin and platform support through Help → Contact Management.

## Best practices

- Use a **password manager** — 1Password, Bitwarden, iCloud Keychain. They make unique long passwords painless.
- **Don't share** your sign-in with family members — each resident should have their own account linked to the same unit where allowed.
- **Be careful** with public devices. Always sign out before walking away.
