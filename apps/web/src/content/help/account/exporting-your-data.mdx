You can download a ZIP file of CSV exports for your community's data from Settings → Export. The export is a point-in-time snapshot of community records, not a personal account download.

## What's included

The ZIP contains four CSV files:

- **Residents** — community member records.
- **Documents** — document metadata (titles, categories, dates).
- **Maintenance requests** — maintenance request records.
- **Announcements** — announcement records.

Very large communities may receive truncated CSVs. If truncation occurs, the download response indicates it.

## What's not included

- File contents for documents (metadata only).
- Payment or violation records.
- Other communities' data.

## Requesting an export

<StepByStep>
<Step title="Open Settings → Export">
From the profile menu, pick Settings, then open the Data Export page.
</Step>

<Step title="Download Community Data">
Click **Download Community Data**.
</Step>

<Step title="Confirm your password">
Enter your password in the reauthentication dialog to confirm the request.
</Step>

<Step title="Download">
The ZIP file downloads immediately. The filename is `community-export-{communityId}.zip`.
</Step>
</StepByStep>

## How the export is formatted

Each CSV opens in Excel or Google Sheets. The ZIP contains CSV files only — no PDFs or README.

## Using the export

Common uses:

- **Record-keeping** — archive a snapshot of community data before a staff change or audit.
- **Offline review** — open maintenance or announcement records in a spreadsheet.
- **Backup reference** — keep a local copy alongside your association's primary records.

<Callout type="tip" title="Not a backup replacement">
The export is a point-in-time copy. It doesn't sync going forward, and your association's records keep updating even after you download. If you need recent data, download a fresh export.
</Callout>
