You can request a copy of the data PropertyPro holds about you. The export is a ZIP file containing CSVs and PDFs covering your account.

## What's included

- **Account profile** — name, email, phone, and any profile fields.
- **Community memberships** — communities you belong to and your role in each.
- **Payment history** — every payment, with dates, amounts, and methods (tokenized, no raw card numbers).
- **Maintenance requests** — every request you've submitted, with comments and attachments.
- **Violations involving you** — any notices, hearings, and resolutions on your unit.
- **Documents you've signed** — countersigned PDFs from the e-sign flow.
- **Comments** — comments you've posted on announcements, meetings, or violations.

## What's not included

- Payment card numbers (we never stored them).
- Other residents' data (even if you shared a thread).
- Documents your association owns (bylaws, minutes, etc.) — those belong to the community, not you.

## Requesting an export

<StepByStep>
<Step title="Open Settings → Export">
From the profile menu, pick Settings, then Export your data.
</Step>

<Step title="Confirm the request">
PropertyPro asks you to confirm via email. This prevents someone with temporary access to your account from silently exporting.
</Step>

<Step title="Wait for the email">
Most exports are ready within a few minutes. Very large accounts (many years of history, lots of attachments) can take up to 24 hours.
</Step>

<Step title="Download">
The email includes a download link that's valid for 7 days. The download is encrypted at rest until you click.
</Step>
</StepByStep>

## How the export is formatted

- **CSVs** — tabular data (payments, maintenance, memberships) that opens in Excel / Google Sheets.
- **PDFs** — any signed documents, violation notices, receipts.
- **README.txt** — a summary of what's in the ZIP.

Filenames are predictable (for example, `payments-sunset-condos-2026.csv`) so you can script around them if needed.

## Using the export

Common uses:

- **Moving** — keep a copy of your maintenance and payment history before leaving the community.
- **Taxes** — the payments CSV gives an audit-ready year of dues.
- **Record-keeping** — for your own archive, especially before a long vacation or leave of absence.

<Callout type="tip" title="Not a backup replacement">
The export is a point-in-time copy. It doesn't sync going forward, and your association's records keep updating even after you download. If you need recent data, request a fresh export.
</Callout>

## Legal basis

The export satisfies typical data-portability requests under privacy laws. PropertyPro does not charge for this — you can request as often as you like within reason.
