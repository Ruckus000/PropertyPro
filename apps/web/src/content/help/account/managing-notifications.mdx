Notifications let PropertyPro reach you when something needs your attention without you having to check the app constantly. You can turn individual channels up or down.

## Channels and categories

Notifications split across two axes:

- **Channel** — email, in-app, or both.
- **Category** — announcements, meeting notices, maintenance updates, payment reminders, violations, document requests, and more.

Each category has a channel preference you can set independently.

## Where to configure

<StepByStep>
<Step title="Open Settings → Notifications">
From the profile menu, choose Settings, then the Notifications section.
</Step>

<Step title="Toggle categories">
For each category, choose Email, In-app, Both, or Off.
</Step>

<Step title="Pick a digest cadence">
For high-volume categories (announcements, general updates), you can collapse them into a daily or weekly digest rather than one email each.
</Step>

<Step title="Save">
Changes take effect for the next notification — previously sent emails aren't affected.
</Step>
</StepByStep>

## Muting specific announcements

If one ongoing announcement thread is very chatty (for example, an active construction project), click **Mute** on that announcement. You'll still see it on your dashboard but won't get new emails for it.

<Callout type="tip" title="Don't mute meeting notices">
Meeting notices carry statutory weight — missing one can mean missing a vote or a hearing. Keep meeting-notice notifications at least In-app so you don't miss them.
</Callout>

## The notifications center

The **Notifications** page (bell icon in the top bar) lists every notification sent to you across channels. Unread items are highlighted. You can:

- Click to open the related item (announcement, meeting, payment, etc.).
- Mark all read.
- Archive old notifications.

Read state syncs across devices, so marking read on your laptop clears the badge on mobile.

## Quiet hours

PropertyPro respects a "don't email after 9pm" rule for non-urgent categories. Emergency broadcasts ignore quiet hours.

## Emails not arriving?

- **Check spam** — especially for a new account. Mark PropertyPro emails as "Not spam" to train your inbox.
- **Verify the address** — if you changed your email recently, confirm the new one in Settings → Account.
- **Deliverability problems** — if other residents report the same, the association may need to whitelist the sending domain with IT.
