Notifications let PropertyPro reach you when something needs your attention without you having to check the app constantly. You control email frequency and which notification types you receive.

## Notification preferences

On the Settings page, the **Notification Preferences** section lets you:

- Pick an **Email frequency** — Immediate, Daily digest, Weekly digest, or Never.
- Check or uncheck **Announcements**, **Meeting notices**, and **In-app notifications**.

Email frequency applies globally — you can collapse all email into a daily or weekly digest via Email frequency.

## Where to configure

<StepByStep>
<Step title="Open Settings">
From the profile menu, choose Settings. The **Notification Preferences** section is embedded on the main Settings page.
</Step>

<Step title="Set email frequency">
Choose Immediate, Daily digest, Weekly digest, or Never from the Email frequency dropdown.
</Step>

<Step title="Toggle notification types">
Check or uncheck Announcements, Meeting notices, and In-app notifications.
</Step>

<Step title="Save">
Changes take effect for the next notification — previously sent emails aren't affected.
</Step>
</StepByStep>

## The notifications center

The **Notifications** page (bell icon in the top bar) lists every notification sent to you across channels. Unread items are highlighted. You can:

- Click to open the related item (announcement, meeting, payment, etc.).
- Mark all read.

Read state syncs across devices, so marking read on your laptop clears the badge on mobile.

## Emails not arriving?

- **Check spam** — especially for a new account. Mark PropertyPro emails as "Not spam" to train your inbox.
- **Verify the address** — if you need to change your email, contact support to change your email.
- **Deliverability problems** — if other residents report the same, the association may need to whitelist the sending domain with IT.
