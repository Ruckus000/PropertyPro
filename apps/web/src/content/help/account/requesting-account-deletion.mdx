You can delete your PropertyPro account at any time. The flow includes a grace period so you can undo it if you change your mind.

## Before you delete

Consider these alternatives first:

- **Leave a community but keep the account** — useful if you're moving to a different community that also uses PropertyPro.
- **Export your data** — payments, maintenance history, and signed documents — before deleting. You can't retrieve them afterward.

## How deletion works

<StepByStep>
<Step title="Open Settings → Account → Delete account">
The deletion flow is at the bottom of the Account page.
</Step>

<Step title="Confirm intent">
Enter your password and a short reason (optional but helpful). PropertyPro tracks reasons to improve the product.
</Step>

<Step title="Grace period begins">
Your account enters a grace state — you can still sign in, but most actions are disabled. The dashboard shows a large banner with the deletion date.
</Step>

<Step title="Automatic deletion">
At the end of the grace window (typically 14 days), the account is deleted. You'll get a final confirmation email.
</Step>
</StepByStep>

## Cancelling during the grace period

Sign in any time before the deletion date and click **Cancel deletion** on the banner. Your account returns to normal immediately.

## What's deleted

- Your profile — name, email, phone, photo.
- Your notification preferences and saved payment methods.
- Your personal comments and feedback.
- Your signed-in sessions.

## What's retained

<Callout type="warning" title="Community records are kept">
Your association needs to keep certain records for regulatory and operational reasons — for example, payment ledger entries, violation notices involving your unit, and countersigned documents. These records are retained in the community's archive with your identifiers anonymized where legally allowed, but the underlying obligations remain.
</Callout>

Concretely, the following are retained in community records:

- Payment and assessment history at your unit.
- Maintenance and violation records attached to your unit.
- Signed legal documents (leases, amendments) per statute.
- Compliance audit-log entries for actions performed on your account.

## If you belong to multiple communities

Deleting the account removes you from every community you're in. If you only want to leave one community, ask that community's admin to remove you — your account stays active for the others.

## Privacy requests

If local law gives you a stronger deletion right (for example, GDPR erasure), contact your association and PropertyPro support together. We honor lawful erasure requests to the extent not barred by retention obligations.

## After deletion

You won't be able to sign in. If you want back later, you can create a new account with the same email — but your prior history won't be restored.
