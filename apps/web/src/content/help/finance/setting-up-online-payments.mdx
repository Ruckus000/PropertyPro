Online payments speed up dues collection and give you a digital receipt automatically. They're available when your association has connected Stripe.

## Is online payment enabled for my community?

Open Payments. If you see a **Pay now** button, your community has it enabled. If you don't, your association is collecting payments through another channel — usually mailed check or bank bill-pay.

## Adding a payment method

<StepByStep>
<Step title="Open Settings → Payment methods">
From the profile menu, pick Settings, then open the Payment methods section.
</Step>

<Step title="Click Add payment method">
You'll see options for card or bank account (ACH) where both are supported.
</Step>

<Step title="Enter details">
Card numbers and bank details go directly to Stripe — PropertyPro never stores them. You'll only ever see the last 4 digits and expiration.
</Step>

<Step title="Optionally set as default">
Checking **Default** uses this method automatically the next time you tap Pay now. You can change the default any time.
</Step>

<Step title="Verify">
For ACH, Stripe may send two small test deposits that you confirm in the Settings screen. Cards are usable immediately.
</Step>
</StepByStep>

## Paying with a saved method

Open Payments, tap **Pay now**, and pick a saved method. The payment goes through in a few seconds; you'll get a confirmation screen and an emailed receipt.

## Security

<Callout type="tip" title="Your numbers don't live on PropertyPro">
Card and bank details are tokenized by Stripe. We store a token, not the actual card number. If our database were compromised, your payment details would not be exposed because they aren't there to begin with.
</Callout>

## Removing a method

In Settings → Payment methods, click the row and pick **Remove**. If it was your default, set a new default so nothing interrupts future payments.

## Refunds

Refunds process back to the original method. If you paid with a card you no longer have, ask your management team — Stripe can still refund a closed card in most cases.

## Common issues

- **Card declined** — usually bank-side. Try the same card on another site, or use a different method.
- **ACH takes a few days** — bank transfers can take 3-5 business days to clear. The receipt is immediate but the funds move on bank time.
- **Community doesn't accept online payments** — your association hasn't connected Stripe yet. Encourage your board to consider it; meanwhile, follow the traditional payment instructions your manager provides.
