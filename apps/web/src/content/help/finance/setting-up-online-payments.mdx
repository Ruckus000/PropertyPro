Online payments speed up dues collection and give you a digital receipt automatically. They're available when your association has connected Stripe.

## Is online payment enabled for my community?

Open **Payments**. Unpaid charges show a **Pay Now** button next to each line item. If everything is paid up, you'll see **All caught up!** and **You have no outstanding assessments.** — no Pay Now button.

If your community hasn't connected Stripe, you'll still see your balance but won't have online payment options. Follow the traditional payment instructions your manager provides.

## Paying online

<StepByStep>
<Step title="Open Payments">
Click **Payments** in the sidebar.
</Step>

<Step title="Pay Now">
Click **Pay Now** next to the charge you want to pay.
</Step>

<Step title="Enter payment details">
The Stripe payment form opens with **Card** and **US bank account** tabs. Enter your details each time you pay — nothing is stored in PropertyPro.
</Step>

<Step title="Confirm">
Submit the payment. If your community passes processing costs to residents, a small **convenience fee** is shown added to the total before you confirm. The line item shows as paid and a receipt is emailed to you.
</Step>
</StepByStep>

## Security

<Callout type="tip" title="Your numbers don't live on PropertyPro">
Card and bank details go directly to Stripe. PropertyPro never stores card numbers — only Stripe processes the payment. If our database were compromised, your payment details would not be exposed because they aren't stored here.
</Callout>

## Refunds

Refunds process back to the original method. If you paid with a card you no longer have, ask your management team — Stripe can still refund a closed card in most cases.

## Common issues

- **Card declined** — usually bank-side. Try the same card on another site, or use a different method.
- **ACH takes a few days** — bank transfers can take 3-5 business days to clear. The receipt is immediate but the funds move on bank time.
- **Community doesn't accept online payments** — your association hasn't connected Stripe yet. Encourage your board to consider it; meanwhile, follow the traditional payment instructions your manager provides.
