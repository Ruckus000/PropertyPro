The assessments register is where admins create, schedule, and track every charge that hits resident accounts — regular dues, special assessments, and late fees. This is the admin counterpart to the resident-facing Payments view.

<Callout type="warning" title="Authority for assessments">
The board is the legal authority for setting and approving assessments. PropertyPro records what was authorized but does not vote, ratify, or recommend amounts. Always anchor an assessment record to a board action (meeting minute, written consent) so the audit trail is complete.
</Callout>

## Recurring dues

Most communities run a recurring monthly, quarterly, or annual assessment cycle. Set it up once and PropertyPro generates the charges automatically.

<StepByStep>
<Step title="Open Payments → Assessments">
From the community Payments page, open the top-level **Assessments** tab. (The admin Payments view opens on **Overview** by default, which also contains an Assessments sub-panel — either reaches the same register, but the top-level Assessments tab is the simplest entry point for creating and editing.)
</Step>

<Step title="Create Assessment">
Click **Create Assessment** and fill in:
- **Title**
- **Amount ($)** — per-unit charge
- **Frequency** — Monthly, Quarterly, Annual, or One-Time
- **Due Day** — day of the month the charge is due
- **Late Fee ($)** and **Grace Days** — flat late fee after the grace window
</Step>

<Step title="Save">
Click **Create Assessment**. Charges apply to all units automatically. Residents can pay online if your community has Stripe connected.
</Step>
</StepByStep>

## Special assessments

Special assessments are one-time charges approved by the board for a specific purpose — a roof project, an insurance shortfall, a SIRS-driven repair.

<StepByStep>
<Step title="Verify board authorization">
Confirm the board has voted to approve the special assessment, with the amount and due date in the meeting minutes.
</Step>

<Step title="Create the assessment">
Click **Create Assessment**. Set **Frequency** to **One-Time**, enter the title (e.g., "2026 Roof Replacement Special Assessment"), the per-unit **Amount ($)**, and the **Due Day**.
</Step>

<Step title="Save">
Click **Create Assessment**. Residents see the charge on their Payments page. Upload board minutes and supporting documents to the Documents library separately — they do not attach to the assessment record.
</Step>
</StepByStep>

PropertyPro does not support allocation methods (equal per unit, by percentage, custom splits) or installment schedules for special assessments.

## Late fees

Late fees are configured on the assessment itself — there's no one-off "apply a fee" action. When you create or edit a recurring assessment, set the **late-fee amount** and a **grace period** (the number of days after the due date before the fee applies). PropertyPro then adds the fee automatically to any charge still unpaid past the grace window.

To change how a late fee behaves, edit the assessment schedule and update the late-fee amount or grace period.

<Callout type="warning" title="Bylaws govern late fee amounts">
PropertyPro does not enforce statutory caps on late fees. Florida statute limits how much can be charged; your bylaws may set further constraints. Verify any late-fee amount against your governing documents before applying.
</Callout>

## The register

The **Assessments** tab lists every assessment as a card showing:

- Title and description
- Per-unit amount and frequency (Monthly, Quarterly, Annual, One-Time)
- Active or Inactive status
- Due day and late-fee settings

Click a card to open its **Line Items** panel. The panel shows per-status counts (for example, how many line items are paid, unpaid, or overdue) and the individual charges for that assessment.

## Reading collection progress

PropertyPro does not show a collection-rate chart over time. Use the per-status line-item counts on each assessment's **Line Items** panel, and open the **Delinquency** tab for units that need outreach.

## Reporting

There is no Reports section under Assessments. Use the **Delinquency** tab for past-due units, and export data from the finance export endpoints — residents can download an account statement PDF, and admins can export CSV data.

## Common patterns

- **Mid-year rate change** — increase the recurring amount effective on a specific date; existing prior-month invoices keep the old amount.
- **Move-in proration** — for unit ownership changes, manually prorate the first month's assessment.
- **Bad debt write-off** — work with your accountant. PropertyPro keeps the record; the accounting treatment happens in your books.
