Your Payments page breaks down what you owe today, what's scheduled, and what you've paid. This guide walks through what each line means.

## The balance card

At the top of the page, the **Current balance** card shows exactly one number: what's due right now, if anything. It combines:

- Unpaid recurring assessments (e.g., monthly dues)
- Any special assessments approved by the board
- Late fees applied per your governing documents
- Credits (for example, from an overpayment on a prior bill)

If everything is paid up, you'll see a balance of $0.00 and a "You're all set" note.

## Upcoming charges

Below the current balance is a list of **Upcoming assessments** — scheduled charges with their due dates. These haven't hit your account yet; they preview so you can plan ahead or set aside funds.

## Payment history

The history section lists every completed payment with:

- **Date** — when the payment cleared
- **Amount** — dollar amount
- **Method** — card, bank transfer, or offline
- **Reference** — a confirmation number useful if you need to ask management about the payment

Click any row to open a full receipt you can download or forward.

## Where late fees come from

<Callout type="warning" title="Bylaws set the rules">
PropertyPro applies late fees according to your association's governing documents. Typical patterns: a flat fee after a grace period, or a percentage of the overdue balance per month. If a fee looks wrong, check your bylaws first, then reach out to your manager.
</Callout>

## Credits and adjustments

If your account shows a credit, it usually comes from one of these:

- You paid more than the balance in a prior billing cycle.
- The board approved a pro-rated refund (for example, an early move-in credit).
- A prior payment that was posted to the wrong unit has been corrected.

Credits automatically offset future assessments.

## Common questions

- **"I paid offline — why doesn't my balance reflect it?"** Offline payments (mailed check, bank bill-pay) are recorded by your management team after they post. There's often a few-day lag. Reach out if a payment is still not reflected after a week.
- **"I was charged twice."** Open Contact Management immediately. Duplicate payments are reversed once management verifies.
- **"I want a detailed breakdown."** Request a full account export from Settings → Export — it includes a payments CSV covering every charge and payment on your account. That's the best source for taxes or record-keeping.
