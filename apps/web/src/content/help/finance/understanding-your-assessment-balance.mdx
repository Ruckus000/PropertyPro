Your Payments page breaks down what you owe today, what's scheduled, and what you've paid. This guide walks through what each line means.

## The balance cards

At the top of the page, three summary cards show:

- Your current balance (labeled by your community's balance type)
- **Total Due** — sum of outstanding charges
- **Overdue Items** — count of past-due line items

If everything is paid up, the Upcoming tab shows **All caught up!** and **You have no outstanding assessments.**

## Upcoming charges

The **Upcoming** tab lists unpaid assessments with due dates, amounts, late fees, and totals. Unpaid items include a **Pay Now** button when online payments are enabled.

## Payment history

The **Payment History** tab lists completed payments with:

- **Due Date**
- **Paid On**
- **Amount**
- **Status**

Rows are not clickable receipts. Each online payment generates an emailed receipt. Use **Download PDF** on the Payments page for a full account statement.

## Where late fees come from

<Callout type="warning" title="Bylaws set the rules">
PropertyPro applies a flat late fee after a grace period configured on each assessment. If a fee looks wrong, check your bylaws first, then reach out to your manager.
</Callout>

## Credits and adjustments

If your account shows a credit, it usually comes from one of these:

- You paid more than the balance in a prior billing cycle.
- The board approved a pro-rated refund (for example, an early move-in credit).
- A prior payment that was posted to the wrong unit has been corrected.

Credits automatically offset future assessments.

## Common questions

- **"I paid offline — why doesn't my balance reflect it?"** Offline payments (mailed check, bank bill-pay) are recorded by your management team after they post. There's often a few-day lag. Reach out if a payment is still not reflected after a week.
- **"I was charged twice."** Open Contact Management immediately. Duplicate payments are reversed once management verifies.
- **"I want a detailed breakdown."** Use **Download PDF** on the Payments page for a full account statement. Settings → Export does not include a payments CSV.
