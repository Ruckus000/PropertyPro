Payments in PropertyPro lets you see what you owe, pay online, and review your history without calling the management office.

<Callout type="tip" title="Community enablement varies">
Online payments are only available if your community has connected Stripe. If you don't see a Pay button, your association still takes payments via the traditional method your manager communicates (mail, bank portal, etc.).
</Callout>

## What you see on the Payments page

- **Current balance** — what's due right now, if anything.
- **Upcoming assessments** — scheduled charges with their due dates.
- **Payment history** — past payments with timestamps and confirmation numbers.
- **Payment methods** — any cards or bank accounts you've saved.

If your community is on a recurring assessment schedule (monthly, quarterly, annual), you'll see the next charge clearly labeled.

## Paying online

<StepByStep>
<Step title="Open Payments">
Click Payments in the sidebar, or use the Pay Dues task card from the help center.
</Step>

<Step title="Review the balance">
Check the amount due and the assessment breakdown. If anything looks wrong — a charge you don't recognize, a credit not applied — reach out to your community contact before paying.
</Step>

<Step title="Choose a payment method">
Pick a saved method or click **Add payment method** to enter a new one. Cards and ACH (bank transfer) are both supported where enabled.
</Step>

<Step title="Submit the payment">
Click **Pay now**. You'll see a confirmation screen with a reference number; a receipt is emailed within a few minutes.
</Step>
</StepByStep>

## Saving a payment method

Saved methods speed up future payments and are required for autopay.

1. Go to **Settings → Payment methods** (or click **Add payment method** from the Payments page).
2. Enter card or bank details. PropertyPro does not store card numbers — only the Stripe tokens that let you pay.
3. Optionally mark the method as your default.

You can delete a saved method at any time. If it's the method used for autopay, autopay will pause until you set a new default.

## Assessment vs. ad-hoc charges

- **Assessments** are the recurring dues your association charges every member.
- **Special assessments** are one-time charges approved by the board for a specific project.
- **Late fees** may apply when an assessment isn't paid by its due date. The rules are in your association's governing documents.

<Callout type="warning" title="Disputes">
If you believe a charge is wrong — wrong amount, charged twice, already paid offline — open Help and choose Contact Management before paying. Paying and then disputing is much harder to unwind.
</Callout>

## Receipts and taxes

- Each online payment generates an emailed receipt.
- Download a full year of receipts from the Payments page (use the **Export** button) if you need it for records or taxes.
- Association assessments are generally not tax-deductible for primary residences; consult a tax professional about your situation.
