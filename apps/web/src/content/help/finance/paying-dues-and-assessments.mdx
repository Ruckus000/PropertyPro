## Review your current balance

The Payments page shows the amount currently due, recent payment history, and any saved payment methods if your community has online payments enabled.

## Complete a payment

If your community accepts online payments, choose the payment method you want to use and follow the prompts to complete the transaction.

## Need help with a charge?

If something looks wrong, use the Help Center contact section to reach management before retrying the payment.
