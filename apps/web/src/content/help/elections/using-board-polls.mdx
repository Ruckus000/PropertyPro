Polls are a lightweight tool for gathering input between formal elections. They're **non-binding** — use them to take temperature or inform a board decision, not to trigger a legal change.

## When to use a poll vs. an election

- **Poll** — opinion gathering, scheduling preferences, amenity preferences, naming new features.
- **Election** — electing board members, amending governing documents, approving special assessments above a certain threshold, any statutorily binding decision.

Polls do **not** satisfy statutory voting requirements. If the outcome matters legally, use the formal election flow.

## Creating a poll

<StepByStep>
<Step title="Open Board → Polls">
From the sidebar, click Board and open the Polls tab.
</Step>

<Step title="New poll">
Click **New poll**. Enter a clear question and 2–5 answer options.
</Step>

<Step title="Choose the audience">
Most polls go to all owners. You can narrow to board members only for internal decisions, or to tenants for tenant-specific topics.
</Step>

<Step title="Set the window">
Pick open and close dates. Short polls (a week or less) get higher response rates.
</Step>

<Step title="Publish">
The poll appears on audiences' dashboards and fans out to email.
</Step>
</StepByStep>

## Anonymity

Polls can be anonymous or attributed:

- **Anonymous** — results show counts only.
- **Attributed** — results show who voted what (useful for board-only polls where the record of position matters).

Pick the option that matches the poll's purpose. Don't change anonymity mid-poll.

## Closing and sharing results

At the close date, voting locks automatically. Results appear on the poll page. Admins can:

- Post a summary announcement with the takeaway
- Export the raw results (CSV) for analysis
- Archive the poll (keeps the record, removes from active list)

## What polls aren't good for

- **Legally binding decisions** — use elections.
- **Controversial yes/no questions** without board discussion first — a contentious poll surfaces disagreement without a path to resolve it.
- **Frequent use** — over-polling fatigues residents. Reserve for genuinely useful questions.

<Callout type="tip" title="Poll + announcement combo">
For decisions that deserve context, post an announcement first explaining the question, then launch the poll. Residents respond more thoughtfully when they understand the tradeoffs.
</Callout>
