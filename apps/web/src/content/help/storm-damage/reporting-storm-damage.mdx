## What this is

After a storm, you can record damage you noticed to your building or the
community's common areas — a leaking roof, water in a hallway, downed siding, a
damaged pool deck. Your report goes to the association so the board and
management have a single, dated log of what happened.

**This is a record for the association — not an insurance claim.** Submitting a
report here does not notify any insurer and does not start or file a claim.
PropertyPro does not file, adjust, negotiate, or settle insurance claims and is
not a public adjuster (Fla. Stat. §626.854). To start a claim, contact your own
insurer or the association's agent of record.

> For anything life-threatening or an active emergency, call 911 or your local
> emergency number first.

## How to file a report

1. Open **Storm Damage** from your community menu.
2. Choose **Report Damage**.
3. Enter the **location** (for example, "Building B — 3rd floor hallway").
4. Pick the **type of damage** (roof, water, structural, exterior, common area,
   or other) and the **severity**.
5. Add the **date it happened**, if you know it, and a short **description** of
   what you saw.
6. Submit. You'll see it appear in the list.

Keep descriptions factual, and don't include other people's personal
information — the report is visible to your board and management.

## What happens next

Your board or management reviews each report and updates its status —
**Submitted**, **Acknowledged**, or **Closed** — as they work through them. That
status is for the association's internal tracking only. It does not reflect any
insurer decision, coverage determination, or claim status.
