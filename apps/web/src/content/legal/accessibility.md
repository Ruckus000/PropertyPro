# Accessibility Statement

**Version:** 2026-08-09.1 · **Effective Date:** August 9, 2026

---

## Our commitment

PropertyPro Florida is committed to making our platform — and the community
association websites we host — usable by everyone, including people who use
screen readers, keyboard navigation, screen magnification, or other assistive
technology.

We aim to conform to the **Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1
Level AA**.

---

## What we do

- **Design system standards.** Our interface is built on a shared design system
  with defined requirements for color contrast, focus indicators, touch target
  sizes, and text alternatives for images and icons. Interactive elements are
  built to be reachable and operable by keyboard.
- **Automated testing.** Accessibility checks run automatically against parts of
  our interface as part of our development process.
- **Contrast guidance for association branding.** Community associations choose
  their own brand colors for their public websites. Our website editor evaluates
  those color choices and warns administrators when a combination is likely to
  fail contrast requirements.
- **Semantic structure.** Pages use proper heading hierarchy, landmark regions,
  and labelled form controls so assistive technology can navigate them.

---

## Where we know we fall short

We would rather be specific than reassuring.

- **Automated testing does not cover every page.** Our automated accessibility
  checks cover part of the interface, not all of it. Areas outside that coverage
  have not been independently verified.
- **We have not completed a full third-party audit.** Our conformance claim is
  based on internal testing against WCAG 2.1 AA, not an external certification.
- **Association-supplied content varies.** Community associations upload their
  own documents, images, and text. We cannot guarantee that a PDF uploaded by an
  association is accessible, or that alternative text supplied by an
  administrator adequately describes an image.
- **Association brand colors are advisory, not enforced.** When an administrator
  chooses colors that fail contrast guidelines, we warn them — we do not block
  publication. A given association's site may therefore fall short of AA
  contrast.

We are working to close these gaps and will update this statement as we do.

## Requesting an accommodation or reporting a barrier

**If any part of PropertyPro or an association website we host is difficult or
impossible for you to use, please tell us.** We want to fix it, and we will also
help you get the information you were trying to reach.

- **Email:** [support@getpropertypro.com](mailto:support@getpropertypro.com)
- **Subject line:** please include "Accessibility" so it reaches the right person

Please tell us, if you can:

- The page or web address where you ran into the problem
- What you were trying to do
- What assistive technology, browser, and operating system you were using

**Our commitment on response:** we will acknowledge your message within
**5 business days**, and we will tell you what we can do and by when.
If a fix will take time, we will provide the information you were seeking by
another means — by email, by phone, or in another format — in the meantime,
at no cost to you.

---

## Association records and alternative formats

If you are a member of a community association and need an official association
record in an alternative format, contact your association directly — the
association is the custodian of its own records. If you have difficulty reaching
them through the platform, contact us at the address above and we will help.

---

## Third-party content

Some parts of our platform rely on third-party services, including payment
processing and embedded maps or documents. We do not control the accessibility of
those services. If you encounter a barrier in one of them, please tell us so we
can look for an alternative or provide the information another way.
